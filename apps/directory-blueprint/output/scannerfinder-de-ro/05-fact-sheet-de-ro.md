# ScannerFinder fact sheet addendum: Germany, Romania, EU

Addendum to [05-fact-sheet.md](../scannerfinder/05-fact-sheet.md). Same rules: each item is one statement, source, URL, fetch date. Every **sourced** item was fetched on 2026-10-14. **Unsourced** items may NOT be used in copy. This is a guardrail, not copy. It is not legal advice; a German or Romanian lawyer should check it before launch.

## Germany

**DE1.** Whoever wants to practise Heilkunde without being licensed as a doctor needs an Erlaubnis. Heilkunde is any professional or commercial activity "zur Feststellung, Heilung oder Linderung von Krankheiten, Leiden oder Körperschäden". A person with that permission carries the title "Heilpraktiker".
Source: Heilpraktikergesetz § 1.
URL: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/heilprg/BJNR002510939.html
Fetched 2026-10-14.

**DE2.** Practising Heilkunde without being a doctor and without the § 1 Erlaubnis is a criminal offence (up to one year in prison or a fine).
Source: Heilpraktikergesetz § 5. URL as DE1. Fetched 2026-10-14.

**DE3.** The Erlaubnis is refused, among other grounds, if an examination of the applicant's knowledge and skills by the health office shows that practising would endanger public health or patients, or if the applicant lacks reliability.
Source: Erste Durchführungsverordnung zum Heilpraktikergesetz § 2.
URL: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/heilprgdv_1/__2.html
Fetched 2026-10-14.

**DE4.** Doctors are a separate profession: practising as a doctor requires the Approbation as Arzt, and the title "Arzt/Ärztin" belongs to that practice.
Source: Bundesärzteordnung § 2 (1) and (5).
URL: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/b_o/__2.html
Fetched 2026-10-14.

**DE5.** HWG § 3: "Unzulässig ist eine irreführende Werbung." It is misleading in particular if methods, treatments, objects or other means are credited with a therapeutic effect they do not have, or if a success is falsely presented as certain.
Source: Heilmittelwerbegesetz § 3.
URL: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/heilmwerbg/__3.html
Fetched 2026-10-14.

**DE6.** HWG § 12 (2): advertising to the public for other means, methods, treatments or objects may not relate to the detection, removal or alleviation of the diseases listed in the Anlage. (The Anlage list itself was not read; do not name diseases from it.)
Source: Heilmittelwerbegesetz § 12.
URL: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/heilmwerbg/__12.html
Fetched 2026-10-14.

**DE7.** UWG § 5: a commercial act is unfair if it is misleading, including when it contains untrue statements.
Source: Gesetz gegen den unlauteren Wettbewerb § 5.
URL: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/uwg_2004/__5.html
Fetched 2026-10-14.

**DE8.** The G-BA guideline on methods in contract-doctor care lists, in Anlage II (methods that must not be provided at the expense of statutory health insurance), item 17: "Bioresonanzdiagnostik, Bioresonanztherapie, Mora-Therapie und vergleichbare Verfahren". Item 1 of the same list is Elektro-Akupunktur nach Voll. Guideline version dated 2026-03-19, in force 2026-04-09.
Source: Gemeinsamer Bundesausschuss, Richtlinie Methoden vertragsärztliche Versorgung, Anlage II.
URL: https://www.g-ba.de/downloads/62-492-4143/MVV-RL_2026-03-19_iK-2026-04-09.pdf
Fetched 2026-10-14.

**DE9.** The same guideline (§ 2) says methods not recognised by the G-BA are excluded from contract-doctor care at the expense of statutory health insurance. A narrow exception exists for life-threatening illness without a standard therapy (Federal Constitutional Court, 1 BvR 347/98). That exception is a doctor's decision, never a basis for copy.
Source and URL: as DE8. Fetched 2026-10-14.

**DE10.** Social court, LSG Nordrhein-Westfalen, 29.03.2001, L 5 KR 137/00: the court treated the committee's exclusion of bioresonance as binding and noted the committee judged these methods as "deren Wirksamkeit nicht belegt sind".
Source: openJur (court database).
URL: https://openjur.de/u/89250.html
Fetched 2026-10-14.

**DE11.** Analogy only, not about bioresonance: OLG Düsseldorf, 21.06.2005, I-20 U 70/05 held advertising for a magnetic field therapy device misleading (HWG § 3, UWG §§ 3, 5) because it suggested the effect was generally scientifically recognised and did not say the Bundesausschuss had not recognised the method.
Source: Justiz NRW court database.
URL: https://nrwe.justiz.nrw.de/olgs/duesseldorf/j2005/I_20_U_70_05urteil20050621.html
Fetched 2026-10-14.

**DE12.** UWG § 7 (2) no. 2: advertising by electronic mail without the addressee's prior express consent is always an unreasonable nuisance. (Outreach note: no cold email to German businesses; contact forms only.)
Source: Gesetz gegen den unlauteren Wettbewerb § 7.
URL: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/uwg_2004/__7.html
Fetched 2026-10-14.

**DE-U1. unsourced.** OLG München, 08.12.2016, 29 U 1893/16 ("Bioresonanztherapie"). Seen only in a commentary snippet; the outcome is unknown. Do not use.

**DE-U2. unsourced.** OLG Düsseldorf, 24.02.2022, I-20 U 292/20 (Heilpraktiker advertising a therapy as certain to succeed, HWG § 3 no. 2a). Read only on the Wettbewerbszentrale site (an association); the court page returned 404. It did not concern bioresonance. Do not use.

Gap: no court ruling or official statement on bioresonance advertising was found on an official or court source. Do not write that German courts have ruled on bioresonance advertising.

## Romania

**RO1.** Legea nr. 118/2007 regulates complementary/alternative medicine (Art. 2: health-care practices not integrated in the main system). Art. 3 names naturist, biological, nutrition, manual, "bioelectromagnetice şi energetice" therapies. Art. 4(2): to become a practitioner, a person must hold a free-practice authorisation (autorizaţia de liberă practică).
Source: Portal Legislativ, Legea 118/2007 (original text, M.Of. 305/8 May 2007).
URL: https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/81760
Fetched 2026-10-14.

**RO2.** Art. 9(1): a practitioner who is not a doctor, dentist or pharmacist may not perform certain medical acts and "nu poate folosi titlurile de doctor, medic, medic dentist". Art. 9(3): a non-physician may intervene only with the patient's written consent and a written recommendation from a doctor.
Source and URL: as RO1. Fetched 2026-10-14.

**RO3.** The annex of Legea 118/2007 lists fields such as magnetic field therapy, Reiki and chromotherapy under bioelectromagnetic and energy applications. Bioresonance is not named there. Whether a given scan session counts as a regulated "practice" is therefore not settled by the text.
Source and URL: as RO1. Fetched 2026-10-14.

**RO4.** Art. 26 required the ministry to issue implementing norms within a year (about May 2008). On 2026-10-14 the Ministry of Health lists a draft order approving them in public consultation, dated 25 September 2026. It is a draft, not law.
Source: Ministerul Sănătății, Acte normative în transparență.
URL: https://ms.ro/ro/transparenta-decizionala/acte-normative-in-transparenta/
Fetched 2026-10-14.

**RO5.** Legea 363/2007 Art. 6(1): a commercial practice is a misleading action if it contains false information. Art. 7: misleading omission of essential information.
Source: Portal Legislativ, Legea 363/2007.
URL: https://legislatie.just.ro/public/detaliidocument/88290
Fetched 2026-10-14.

**RO6.** Legea 363/2007, Anexa 1 point 17 (misleading in all circumstances): "Falsa afirmație că produsul poate vindeca boli, disfuncții sau malformații."
Source and URL: as RO5. Fetched 2026-10-14.

**RO7.** Art. 15: fine of 20,000 to 100,000 lei for practices under Art. 6 and 7 or listed in Anexa 1 (doubled where more than 100 consumers are affected). The page flags amendments applying from 27 September 2026 (OUG 18/2026), mainly new Anexa 1 points on environmental and durability claims.
Source and URL: as RO5. Fetched 2026-10-14.

**RO-U1. unsourced.** Any CMR (Colegiul Medicilor) or ANPC position on bioresonance. None found on cmr.ro, anpc.ro, ms.ro or anm.ro. Do not claim one exists.

**RO-U2. unsourced.** Press claims that practitioners must join a professional order, that CNAS may reimburse these therapies, and that practitioners are "recognised" in practice. Found only in news. Do not use.

## EU

**EU1.** Regulation (EU) 2017/745, Art. 2(1): a medical device is an instrument, apparatus, appliance, software or other article intended by the manufacturer for human use for medical purposes such as diagnosis, prevention, monitoring, treatment or alleviation of disease.
Source: EUR-Lex, Regulation (EU) 2017/745.
URL: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32017R0745
Fetched 2026-10-14.

**EU2.** Art. 5(1): a device may be placed on the market only if it complies with the Regulation. Art. 20(1): devices considered in conformity bear the CE marking (Annex V).
Source and URL: as EU1. Fetched 2026-10-14.

**EU3.** The EU's business portal says that with the CE marking "the manufacturer indicates that a product meets the requirements set out in EU product rules", that the manufacturer is responsible for it, and that no central EU body gives a certificate allowing its use. The page does not say the mark shows a product works. Our inference: a CE mark is a compliance statement, not proof of efficacy (matches UK3 in the main sheet).
Source: Your Europe, CE marking.
URL: https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/product-requirements/labels-markings/ce-marking/index_en.htm
Fetched 2026-10-14.

## What our copy may NOT say (DE / RO)

1. That a scan diagnoses, detects, tests for or screens for allergies, intolerances, pathogens, deficiencies or any disease. (DE5, DE6, DE8, RO6, EU1)
2. That a session heals, cures, treats or relieves anything, or that success is certain. (DE5, RO6)
3. That sessions are covered, reimbursed or recognised by statutory insurance, CNAS or any insurer. (DE8, DE9)
4. That bioresonance has official or scientific recognition in Germany. The G-BA lists it as excluded. (DE8, DE10)
5. Calling a Heilpraktiker or a Romanian complementary practitioner "doctor", "Arzt" or "medic". (DE4, RO2)
6. That a practitioner is approved, licensed or certified by an authority, unless we hold the authority's own record for that person. (DE1, RO1)
7. That a device is "CE approved" or "certified" as proof it works. (EU3)
8. That a Romanian practitioner is covered by the Legea 118/2007 norms. They are still a draft. (RO4)
9. Any claim about what German or Romanian courts or regulators have said about bioresonance advertising. (DE-U1, DE-U2, RO-U1)
10. Cold email to German businesses. Contact forms only. (DE12)

## What our copy may say

1. "In Germany, a Heilpraktiker is a state-licensed profession. A Heilpraktiker is not a doctor." (DE1, DE4)
2. "Doctors in Germany are a separate profession and hold an Approbation." (DE4)
3. "In Romania, complementary and alternative practice is governed by Law 118/2007. Practitioners who are not doctors may not use the title doctor." (RO1, RO2)
4. "Sessions listed here are wellness sessions. They are not medical tests and do not diagnose or treat any condition." (carry-over of main sheet, plus DE5, RO6)
5. "Bioresonance sessions are not a service covered by German statutory health insurance." (DE8)
6. "A CE mark is a manufacturer's statement of compliance with EU rules. It is not proof that a device works." (EU3)
7. "If you have a health concern, see a doctor." (main sheet, UK2 and IE2)
