About Paradise Pools By Design

Paradise Pools by Design says it has designed and built luxury pools and spas across Central Florida for nearly two decades. Its site sets out a step by step construction process that includes a gunite shell and decking finished with brick pavers, travertine or concrete. It also has a commercial division, which it says builds pools and spas for hotels, resorts and multifamily developments, and the menu adds splash pads, a showroom and a financing page. The site refers to an industry leading warranty. Screen enclosures appear only as a barrier option and a footer step in the construction outline, so treat them as unconfirmed. Google Maps shows a 4.5 rating from 62 reviews, 52 at five stars and 5 at one star, and lists pool repair as a second category. No license number appears in the data.
