About Weller Pools LLC

Weller Pools in Apopka positions itself as a commercial aquatic builder working across the U.S. and the Caribbean since 1974. Its site menu groups projects into water theme parks, community developments, municipal pools, competition pools, hotels and resorts, fitness and wellness, international work and splash pads. It offers design/build packages or work on pre-designed projects. Testimonials on the site praise its commercial pool construction and recommend it for construction or renovation work, including water parks, fountains and spas. The site gives no license number, financing or warranty details, and it is aimed at commercial clients rather than homeowners. A letter on the site confirms it worked as a subcontractor on one project, and the Maps listing posts hours. Google Maps shows a 4.7 rating from 6 reviews, 4 of them five stars and 2 of them four stars.
