About Aqua Dreams Pool & Outdoor

Aqua Dreams Pool & Outdoor describes itself as a custom pool builder covering Tampa, Clearwater, Sarasota and nearby areas, with the tagline From Dig to Dive. The site lists pool design and planning, pool installation, and renovation and remodeling, plus outdoor living work such as outdoor kitchens, paver patios and walkways, retaining and seating walls, landscape lighting and artificial grass. It says it works with financing partners for new builds and remodels, with fast approvals and monthly plans, and it states that its contractors are licensed and insured in Florida. A homeowner review posted on the site praises an in ground pool and paver project. Maps notes veteran ownership. Its rating is 4.6 from 14 reviews, with 12 five-star, one four-star and one one-star rating. The record does not name pool materials, a warranty or commercial work.
