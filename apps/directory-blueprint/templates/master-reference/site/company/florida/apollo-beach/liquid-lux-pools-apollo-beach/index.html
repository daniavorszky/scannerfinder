About Liquid Lux Pools

Liquid Lux Pools lists swimming pool installation, remodeling and renovation, resurfacing and replastering, maintenance, repair, equipment and pump repair, heater repair, leak repair, and deck and tile repair. According to the site it uses 3D design software to model custom pools for clients, and it calls itself licensed and insured. The listing is in Apollo Beach, and the record does not say which nearby areas the company serves, so check that before hiring. Deck work on the site is described as repair and resurfacing, not installing new decks or pavers. One review posted on the site mentions a spa and exercise routine for a spouse's therapy, but that is not evidence the company builds spas. Google shows a 5.0 rating from 10 reviews, all five stars. The record has no warranty or financing information.
