About EDX POOLS

EDX Pools describes itself as a South Florida builder of custom luxury pools, working on new construction, renovation, plaster, repair, equipment upgrades and maintenance. The site says its new pools start as gunite and shotcrete shells, and it lists infinity edges and resort-style designs. Renovation copy covers reshaping, tile, coping and decking, and the FAQ says a spa or water features can be added during a renovation. A customer review published on the site says EDX built a pool and deck for a boutique hotel and handled the commercial permitting. The site calls the company a fully licensed and insured pool contractor but gives no license number in the record. It mentions no financing or warranty. The Google Maps listing is in Boca Raton and has four reviews, all five stars.
