About Liberty Pools - Pool Construction & Renovation Contractor

Liberty Pools designs and builds custom pools from a Boca Raton base and also serves Palm Beach County and the Port St. Lucie area, according to its site. The site lists new pool construction with 3D design, pool and spa design styles, and renovation of existing pools, and it cites license CPC 1459207. It describes a lifetime structural warranty on the shell for new construction, with separate terms for cosmetic remodels and resurfacing. Financing is offered through three lending partners named on the site: HFS, Lyon Financial and Acorn Finance. Liberty says it belongs to a family of companies that also covers weekly pool care and site preparation. The site does not mention decking, pavers or commercial work. Google Maps shows a 4.8 rating from 246 reviews, 235 at five stars and 10 at one star.
