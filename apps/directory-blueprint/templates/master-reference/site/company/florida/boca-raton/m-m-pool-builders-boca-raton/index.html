About M&M Pool Builders

M&M Pool Builders is a family-owned Boca Raton company that says it has more than 10 years of experience and builds custom gunite pools for homeowners in Boca Raton and Palm Beach County. The site's main focus is new pool construction, with luxury pool design, multi-level pools with integrated spas, and remodeling that includes replastering, tile replacement and repairs. It also installs outdoor kitchens, pergolas, artificial turf and patios in pavers, concrete, natural stone, brick or wood, and it says it handles permit applications. The footer shows license CPC1459651. It describes itself as voted a top 5 swimming pool contractor, without naming the source of the vote. Financing, warranties and commercial work are not mentioned. Google Maps shows a 4.9 rating from 44 reviews, with 43 at five stars and one at one star.
