About Tucker Design Build Inc

Tucker Design Build Inc is a landscape and hardscape firm in Boca Raton whose site says it has been building pools from Florida to the Hamptons and has worked across the Caribbean. The menu covers hardscape, driveways, sports surfaces, landscape, vertical gardens, tree moving, patios, outdoor kitchens, lighting, resorts and a Pools & Patios section. The site says it offers landscape design for civic, institutional, commercial and residential clients, and it shows that finance is available. It gives no license number, and it does not say what kind of pools it builds or whether it remodels, offers spas or provides a warranty. Pools appear to be one part of a wider design-build business. On Google Maps it has a 4.1 rating from 45 reviews, 33 at five stars, 2 at four stars and 10 at one star.
