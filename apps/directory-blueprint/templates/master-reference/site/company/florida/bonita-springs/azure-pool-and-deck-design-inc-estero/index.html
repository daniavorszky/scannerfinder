About Azure Pool and Deck Design, Inc.

Azure Pool and Deck Design, Inc. is a family business in Estero that says it specializes in resurfacing and remodeling pool areas and backyards. According to the site, it does everything from new pool construction to repairs, full renovations, spa additions, equipment replacement, pool heaters and paver patios, and it is licensed for residential and commercial work in Florida, with CPC1459270 in the footer. Other sections cover pool bottom refinishing, tile and stone, outdoor kitchens, decks, water features, and a Financing page. A client review posted on the site describes replacing lanai decking and refinishing a courtyard pool and hot tub. No warranty is mentioned. Google Maps shows a 4.9 rating from 66 reviews, 64 at five stars and 2 at one star.
