About Christie's Luxury Pool Builders

Christie's Luxury Pool Builders works from Southwest Florida and names Naples, Bonita Springs and Estero as its service area. Despite the name, the site centers on renovation and restoration: resurfacing of pools and spas, waterline tile, coping, pool decks, paver, travertine and stone decking, equipment, and water features. It says in-house crews handle residential and commercial work and that the company is fully licensed and insured, and a number beginning RP appears in the footer. A heading also names custom water features and spas. Nothing on the site says it builds new pools, and it mentions no financing or warranty. Google Maps shows a 4.9 rating from 41 reviews, 40 at five stars, and it identifies as women-owned. Google Maps also lists it as a deck builder and equipment supplier.
