About McDaniel Construction of SW FL, Inc

McDaniel Construction of SW FL, Inc is a general contractor in Estero and not a specialist pool company. The site calls it a licensed and insured Class A general contracting firm with over 35 years in construction. Its menu lists new home construction with insulated concrete form walls, remodeling, kitchen renovations, roof repairs, Hurricane Ian recovery and pool construction, with pool additions named in the company text. Brick and concrete pavers are also offered. The site gives no detail on pool types or financing. Its contract note tells the customer to apply for manufacturer warranties, which is not a warranty from the company. Google Maps shows a 4.4 rating from 14 reviews, 11 at five stars and 2 at one star.
