About Florida Pool ®

Florida Pool is a pool finish specialist in Boynton Beach. Its site is built around Pebbletec pebble and glass bead finishes, with a three-step process for choosing texture and water color and samples brought to the customer's door. It describes itself as a certified Pebbletec applicator serving Palm Beach, Broward, Miami and the Keys with more than 20 years of experience, and it handles replasters as well as new pool finishes. The site publishes both material and workmanship warranty terms and offers warranty registration. It does not present itself as a pool builder, and the record has nothing on licensing, financing, decks, spas or commercial work. Google Maps shows a perfect 5.0 rating from 6 reviews, all of them five stars, and posted hours.
