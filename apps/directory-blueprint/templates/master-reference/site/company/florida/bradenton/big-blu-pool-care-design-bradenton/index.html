About Big Blu Pool Care & Design

Big Blu Pool Care & Design builds inground pools, renovates existing ones and runs weekly maintenance for homeowners in Sarasota and Bradenton. The site says it has built pools since 2005 and gives Florida certified pool contractor license CPC-1457173. It describes steel-reinforced gunite shells covered by a lifetime structural warranty, with most custom projects finishing in one to two months. It says it is an approved builder with Lyon Financial. Renovation work includes resurfacing, tile replacement and equipment upgrades, spas can be added, and a commercial projects item appears in its service list. Weekly maintenance is described as starting at $100 a month. Google Maps shows a 4.9 rating from 43 reviews, 39 of them five stars, 3 four-star and 1 two-star. Screen enclosures and pavers are not mentioned.
