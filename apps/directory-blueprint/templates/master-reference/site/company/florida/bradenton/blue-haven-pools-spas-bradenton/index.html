About Blue Haven Pools & Spas

Blue Haven Pools & Spas is a Bradenton listing whose website reads as a national franchise site, with a location finder and a claim of more than 60 offices across the country, so confirm the local service area by phone. The site describes custom gunite inground pools, integrated spas, imported stone decking and remodels that include resurfacing and deck renovation. It says most inground gunite pools run from $60,000 to $100,000 or more, and it offers financing for qualified buyers, with a heading about 100 percent financing. Blue Haven describes itself as an experienced custom pool builder that connects visitors to the closest location. Licensing, warranty and commercial work are not mentioned. Google Maps shows a 4.0 rating from 4 reviews, three five-star and one one-star.
