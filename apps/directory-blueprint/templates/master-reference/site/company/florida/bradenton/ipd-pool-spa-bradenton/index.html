About IPD Pool & Spa

IPD Pool & Spa is a family-owned pool contractor with a Bradenton address, serving Bradenton, Lakewood Ranch and Sarasota, according to its website. It describes an in-house team of pool builders, with leadership overseeing each step from design through the dig to completion. The site covers custom pavers for the pool deck and has a pool financing page, and Florida license CPC 1457636 appears in its footer. It says hundreds of homeowners have chosen it and calls itself five-star rated. The site does not say which pool materials it builds with, and remodeling, warranty and commercial work are not mentioned. The company name includes Spa, but the text shown does not describe spa work. Google Maps shows a 4.8 rating from 29 reviews, 27 of them five stars, 1 four-star and 1 one-star.
