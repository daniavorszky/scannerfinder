About Jackson Custom Pools & Outdoor Living LLC

Jackson Custom Pools & Outdoor Living LLC builds custom pools and outdoor kitchens for homeowners in Lakewood Ranch, Bradenton, Parrish, Palmetto, Sarasota and Anna Maria Island, according to its website. The site describes a three-step process of design, planning and execution, and says the team manages every step of construction. Its meta text mentions luxury pools, spas and outdoor kitchens. Financing runs through three named lending partners, Lyon Financial, HFS Financial and Viking Capital. License CPC1459124 appears on the site, along with a warranty claim link, though the warranty terms are not spelled out. Google Maps shows a 4.9 rating from 255 reviews, with 247 five-star, 3 four-star and 5 one-star ratings. The site says nothing specific about pool materials, remodeling or commercial work.
