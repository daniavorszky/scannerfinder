About L & S Pool

L & S Pool is a family-owned pool company in Bradenton that says it has been a local source since 1970 and serves most areas of Manatee and Sarasota Counties. The site shows state certified swimming pool contractor license CPC1457965. It describes new pools, complete renovations, repairs, water analysis and free in-home consultations, along with a store selling equipment, tile, coping pavers, finishes and supplies. It invites quotes on pool, spa or patio projects, and a customer review published on the site says a pool and its decking were completely remodeled. The site does not mention financing, a warranty or commercial work. Google Maps shows a 4.3 rating from 16 reviews, 12 of them five stars, 2 one-star, 1 three-star and 1 four-star.
