About Reed's Swimming Pool Services

Reed's Swimming Pool Services says it has designed and built high-end residential pools and spas since 1981 from its Brandon shop, which also runs a retail pool store. The site names David Reed Jr. as co-owner and chief designer and says he supervises jobs. It lists design and construction, remodeling and renovation, interlocking pavers and travertine decks, fire pits, waterfalls and weekly cleaning and maintenance. It says it builds residential and commercial pools in unusual styles such as zero edge and infinity edge. Reed's says it partners with Lyon Financial. No license number or warranty appears in the record. Google Maps shows a 4.0 rating from 42 reviews, 29 at five stars and 8 at one star. The menu links to a store, and the site invites visitors to drop by the shop.
