About Splash City Pools

Splash City Pools describes itself as a veteran-owned Tampa Bay builder of custom pools, spas, remodels and outdoor living spaces, and its site calls it Tampa's number one trusted builder. Its menu has residential, commercial and fiberglass pools, remodeling and renovation, screen enclosures, outdoor kitchens, pergolas, financing and warranty pages. The site says every project includes a two-year workmanship warranty covering labor and material and that flexible financing is available. It says it is a fully licensed and insured Florida pool contractor serving Tampa, Brandon, Riverview, Valrico, Lithia and Wesley Chapel, though no license number appears in the record. Google Maps shows a 4.9 rating from 77 reviews, 75 at five stars and 2 at one star. A project manager evaluates the space and provides a custom 3D design and estimate, the site says.
