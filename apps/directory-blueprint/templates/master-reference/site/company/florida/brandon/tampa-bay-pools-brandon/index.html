About Tampa Bay Pools

Tampa Bay Pools, based in Brandon, says it has built custom inground pools since 2001 and serves the Tampa area, Bradenton, Sarasota, Riverview and other nearby towns. The site describes gunite pools, custom spas, outdoor kitchens, screened enclosures, water and fire features and decking, and says it takes residential or commercial work. It advertises pre-approved financing and a design showroom, and it lists Florida license CPC1460499. Every pool, it says, has a lifetime shell warranty, with a three-year warranty on pump, filter, valves and lights. It claims a 100% completion rate. Google Maps shows a 4.6 rating from 568 reviews, 496 at five stars and 44 at one star. The site also offers 3-D design previews and says clients pick finishes and features in its showrooms.
