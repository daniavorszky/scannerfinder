About Coastal Oasis Pools

Coastal Oasis Pools is a second-generation, veteran-owned pool contractor serving Citrus, Hernando and Pasco counties, with Spring Hill and Brooksville listed on its site. It says it handles new pool construction, remodeling, resurfacing, equipment upgrades, repairs and weekly service, and it says a remodel can add a built-in spa. License number CPC1461347 appears on the site, and the menu has a financing page. Free estimates are offered through a form and a phone number, and the FAQ topics include build time, permits and cost for a new pool. Nothing in the data covers a warranty, pool shell types or commercial work. Google Maps shows a perfect 5.0 rating from 123 reviews, all of them five stars, and lists pool repair as a second category.
