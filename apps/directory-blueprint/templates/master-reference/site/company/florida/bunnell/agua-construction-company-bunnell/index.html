About Agua Construction Company

Agua Construction Company is a general contractor serving Palm Coast, Flagler County and neighboring counties, with pools as its lead service. The site says it builds custom inground gunite pools and spas and handles remodels and resurfacing, and it also does home remodeling, kitchens, bathrooms, roofing and new homes. Pools come with travertine over a concrete sub-deck, LED lighting, salt systems and variable speed pumps, and an optional screen enclosure is installed during the build. The site says all work is done in-house and points to handicap accessibility as a specialty of its owner. It lists three license numbers, including a pool contractor license, and a financing partner. Google Maps shows 4.5 from 72 reviews, 62 of them five stars and 7 of them one star.
