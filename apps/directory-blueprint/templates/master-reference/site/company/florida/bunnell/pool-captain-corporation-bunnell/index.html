About Pool Captain Corporation

Pool Captain is based in Palm Coast and says it has been building pools for more than 18 years. The site walks through a staged build that includes steel inspection and shotcrete, then grading, and its sample specification lists a one-year builder warranty and a steel-reinforced footer for a screen enclosure. It also offers remodels and pool service, and its summary adds fire pits, patios and summer kitchens. Decking, tanning ledges and swim-outs are named in the pool features. A financing section credits a lending partner of more than 15 years. The footer shows a residential contractor license and CPC 1457452. The record says nothing about commercial work. Google Maps shows 4.3 from 28 reviews, with 22 five-star ratings and 5 one-star ratings.
