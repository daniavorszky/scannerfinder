About Cape Coral Pool Builders

Cape Coral Pool Builders describes itself as a team that designs, builds and maintains pools in Cape Coral and Fort Myers, and its menu also links to Naples. The site says one crew builds each pool from start to finish. Services include inground pools, custom pools, pool repair and above ground pools, and it says it offers concrete inground installation, renovation work, and decking installed with interlocking tiles. It states that flexible financing options are available and that it provides a warranty, and the footer shows license CPC1460180. It also promotes pool cleaning as a simple, affordable service. The site does not mention spas, screen enclosures or commercial work. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 16 reviews, all of them five stars.
