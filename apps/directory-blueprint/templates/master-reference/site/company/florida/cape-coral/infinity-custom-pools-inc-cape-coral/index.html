About Infinity Custom Pools, Inc.

Infinity Custom Pools, Inc. builds custom inground pools and spas from an office on Country Club Blvd in Cape Coral, with a second office in Bradenton in Manatee County. The site says it serves Lee, Charlotte, Sarasota, Manatee and Collier counties, and its menu covers pool options, custom spas, financing, the building process and service pages for Cape Coral, Fort Myers and Lehigh Acres. Listed pool details include integrated spas, paver decking, screen enclosures and built-in hot tubs. It shows Florida Certified Pool Contractor license CPC1460712 and says its pools come with strong warranties. It describes itself as a high-end luxury builder. The site does not name specific pool materials or say whether it remodels or takes commercial work. Google Maps shows a 4.7 rating from 15 reviews, 14 of them five stars and 1 of them one star.
