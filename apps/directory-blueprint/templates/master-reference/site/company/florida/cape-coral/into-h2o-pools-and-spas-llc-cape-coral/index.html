About Into H2O Pools and Spas, LLC

Into H2O Pools and Spas operates from a design center on SE 47th Terrace in Cape Coral and serves Lee, Charlotte and Collier counties. The site says the team has nearly 45 years of experience designing, building and repairing inground swimming pools and spas, and it describes a state registered residential and commercial pool contractor license held on every job. New pools and spas, renovations and water features are all listed. It also installs brick pavers, natural stone, travertine, and spray deck or stamped concrete, and its site description mentions screen enclosures. The financing page names a lending partner, and the site says it can add a spa to an existing pool. It does not mention a warranty or specific pool materials. Google Maps shows a 4.3 rating from 43 reviews, 35 of them five stars and 7 of them one star.
