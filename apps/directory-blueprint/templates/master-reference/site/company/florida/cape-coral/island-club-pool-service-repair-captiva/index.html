About Island Club Pool Service & Repair

Island Club Pool Service & Repair works on North Captiva Island and is listed in Captiva. The site introduces John and Sarah, with 40 plus and 20 plus years of experience, and says John's background spans commercial and residential pool construction, maintenance and repair. What it offers now reads as service: maintenance, repairs, and supply and installation of new pool and patio equipment from Pentair, Jandy and Aquacal, along with advice on remodeling. It calls itself a registered warranty service station, which is equipment warranty work and not a warranty on its own installations. The site does not say it builds new pools, so treat it as a service and repair option and confirm by phone before asking about construction.
