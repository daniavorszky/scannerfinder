About Palazzo Pool & Spa

Palazzo Pool & Spa works out of Cape Coral and serves Southwest Florida, including Naples and Fort Myers. Its website presents the business as a design-driven builder of luxury pools and outdoor living spaces, with real-time 3D visualization so clients can see a project before construction begins. The site lists new builds as well as renovations, including work on existing pools such as modern finishes, added spas, features and lighting. It shows the license number CPC1459122 and says the company is licensed and insured. The site says nothing about pool materials such as gunite or fiberglass, decking, screen enclosures, financing, warranties or commercial work, so ask about those directly.
