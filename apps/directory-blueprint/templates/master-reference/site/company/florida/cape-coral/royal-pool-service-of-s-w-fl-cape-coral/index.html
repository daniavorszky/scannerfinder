About Royal Pool Service of S.W. FL

Listed on Google Maps as Royal Pool Service of S.W. FL, this Cape Coral business has a website branded Keller Pools that describes a family owned, full service pool contractor serving the community since 1999. It runs two Cape Coral store locations selling heaters, lights, pumps and salt systems, plus a service department for leak detection and equipment installation. The site says it has a design studio to design and build or remodel pools, resurfaces pools, and replaces vinyl liners as a Florida licensed contractor, and it lists license CPC-057285. It says financing is available and mentions an industry leading warranty on its vinyl liners. It does not mention screen enclosures, pavers or commercial work. Google Maps shows a 3.9 rating from 19 reviews, 13 of them five stars and 4 of them one star.
