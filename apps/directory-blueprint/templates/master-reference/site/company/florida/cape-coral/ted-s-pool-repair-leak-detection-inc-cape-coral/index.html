About Ted's Pool Repair & Leak Detection, Inc

Ted's Pool Repair & Leak Detection, Inc serves Cape Coral, and its website describes a business that both repairs and builds pools. Menu items cover leak detection, pool repair, and new construction of pools and spas, and the site says Ted has built and repaired pools since 1981. It also lists remodels and add-ons, including adding a spa to an existing pool, and says decks can use pavers, travertine or keystone with exposed aggregate interiors. The site shows license CPC-1456608 and lists Best of Cape Coral awards for 2014, 2015 and 2016. Its site description oddly refers to a property developer based in Punta Gorda, so confirm the business details by phone. It does not mention financing or a warranty. Google Maps shows a 4.3 rating from 21 reviews, 17 of them five stars and 2 of them one star.
