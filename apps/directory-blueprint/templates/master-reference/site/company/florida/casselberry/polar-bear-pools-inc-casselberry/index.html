About Polar Bear Pools Inc

Polar Bear Pools has operated out of Casselberry since it was incorporated in 1986, and the site calls it an owner-operated custom pool and spa builder with more than 20 years of building. Its stated specialty is major pool renovations, and it also builds new pools, spas, fountains, waterfalls, grottos and sport pools. Related work named on the site includes custom rock features, pool decking, pavers, acrylic decking, lighting, salt systems and pool heating. The footer shows a state certified pool contractor number, CPC 029473. The site is dated 2009 and is thin, and the record does not mention financing, a warranty or commercial pools. Google Maps shows 4.7 from 3 reviews, with two five-star ratings and one four-star rating.
