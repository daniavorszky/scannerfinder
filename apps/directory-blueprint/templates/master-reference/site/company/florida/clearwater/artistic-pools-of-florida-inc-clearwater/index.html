About Artistic Pools of Florida Inc.

Artistic Pools of Florida is a Clearwater business that describes itself as a state licensed pool, spa and deck contractor, and its site shows certified pool contractor credentials with the number CPC1458877, including commercial. Its services cover new pool construction, surface, tile and deck remodels, equipment repair, cleaning and maintenance, and free delivery of chemicals and supplies, with pool finishes, tile, coping and brick pavers listed as products. Its surface work includes acid wash, polishing, refinishing and tile replacement. A finance page carries offers from lending partners. The site also lists government procurement codes and vendor information. The record gives no warranty. Google Maps shows a low 2.7 rating from 25 reviews, with 9 five star and 12 one star ratings.
