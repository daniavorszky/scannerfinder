About Florida Pool Tech

Florida Pool Tech, based in Clearwater, says it has offered pool equipment repair, leak detection and custom construction since 2009 around Tampa Bay, Bradenton and Sarasota. On the construction side it describes custom inground pools, remodels and renovations, spas, water features, outdoor kitchens, travertine pool decks and 3D renderings, and its menu has a page on pool construction financing. The site lists two contractor licenses, CPC1458524 and CPC1461676, and says its leak detection and skimmer replacement cover residential and commercial pools. It also runs an online pool store and mentions showrooms. The record does not mention a warranty or screen enclosures. Google Maps shows a 4.6 rating from 190 reviews, 171 of them five stars and 19 at one star.
