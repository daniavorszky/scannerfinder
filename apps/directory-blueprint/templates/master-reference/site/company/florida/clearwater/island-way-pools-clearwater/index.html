About Island Way Pools

Island Way Pools builds custom concrete shell pools, and its site says it has more than 20 years of experience, over 1,500 pools built and new projects finished 8 to 12 weeks after contract signing. Services include shotcrete pool construction, remodels, outdoor living construction, screen enclosures, hardscaping and commercial pools, with a spa or spillover spa offered as an added feature. The site mentions a showroom with finishes, tile and decking, and its menu has financing and warranty pages. It states that it is a licensed and insured builder serving Pinellas and eight other counties, but no license number appears. Google Maps shows a 4.7 rating from 188 reviews, 167 of them five stars and 8 at one star.
