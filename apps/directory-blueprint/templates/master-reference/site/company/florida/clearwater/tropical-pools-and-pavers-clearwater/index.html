About Tropical Pools and Pavers

Tropical Pools and Pavers, listed in Clearwater, says it has more than 25 years of experience building pools, spas and paving for homeowners across the Tampa Bay and St. Petersburg area. Pages also cover complete pool renovations, waterfalls and custom options, and describe its custom pools as energy saving.
