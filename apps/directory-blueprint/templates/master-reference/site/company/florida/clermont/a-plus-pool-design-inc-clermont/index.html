About A Plus Pool Design Inc

A Plus Pool Design Inc says it has served Central Florida since 1981 and handles pool maintenance, equipment repair, resurfacing and renovation, and custom new pool design and build. The site has a Pool Packages page, a Spas page and a pool school, and says customers can qualify for new pool financing through Lyon Financial. Renovation ideas include water features, a spa or a sun shelf, and resurfacing is called its specialty. It shows a license number, CPC1457174, and describes itself as licensed and insured. The record does not mention a warranty, commercial work or specific pool materials. Google Maps shows a 4.4 rating from 27 reviews, 22 at five stars and 4 at one star. Google Maps also lists it as a pool cleaning and repair service, with hours posted.
