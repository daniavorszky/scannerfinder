About Blue Horseshoes Pools OCF INC

Blue Horseshoe Pools is based in Clermont and says it has served the region since 2008, with a focus on commercial pool service. The site lists pool maintenance, repairs, resurfacing, equipment sales and renovations, and its services menu also names pool construction, with a line about turning a backyard into a personal oasis. It answers a licensing question by saying the company and its contractors are licensed and insured, without a number, and it mentions a Realtor program. Spas appear only as equipment it services. Warranty and financing are not mentioned. Google Maps shows a 3.0 rating from 10 reviews, with five at five stars and five at one star, and it identifies as Latino-owned and women-owned. It says its staff are certified pool and spa technicians, and Google Maps also files it as a pool cleaning service.
