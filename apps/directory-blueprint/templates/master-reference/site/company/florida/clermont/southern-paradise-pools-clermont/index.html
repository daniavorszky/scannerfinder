About Southern Paradise Pools

Southern Paradise Pools and Spas, in Clermont, describes itself as a growing company started by owner Marcus Fuggi, who has worked in pool construction for years and got a contractor's license after buying his first home. The site offers new pool construction and remodeling, with a 3D design program and free in-home estimates. Its FAQ says plainly that it does not build fiberglass or vinyl pools. A financing page names two partner lenders, Lyon Financial and HFS Financial. The record does not give a license number, a warranty or any commercial work. Google Maps shows a 4.6 rating from 28 reviews, 25 at five stars and 2 at one star. It says each pool is custom designed by the homeowner, and that a smaller company can often finish a build faster than a larger one.
