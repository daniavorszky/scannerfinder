About AJ Pools Cleaning Repairs & Renovations

AJ Pools Cleaning Repairs & Renovations is listed in Coconut Creek, though its site title is written for Boca Raton and it names Parkland, Coral Springs and Coconut Creek among the areas served, so confirm the service area by phone. The core service is weekly pool cleaning, and the site says its staff are certified pool operators. It also does repairs and equipment installation, including gas heaters and heat pumps, plus remodeling and replastering. Its pages mention commercial repairs and industry-standard warranties on workmanship and materials for remodeling and replastering. New construction is not clearly offered, and the site gives no contractor license number. Google Maps shows a 4.8 rating from 130 reviews, 124 at five stars and 6 at one star.
