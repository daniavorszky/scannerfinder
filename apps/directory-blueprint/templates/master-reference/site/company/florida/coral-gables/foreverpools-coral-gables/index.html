About ForeverPools

Glass tile resurfacing is the whole focus of ForeverPools, a Coral Gables listing on Google Maps under swimming pool contractor. The site describes the company as a pool renovation firm that resurfaces old pools with European glass mosaic tile, and says it serves the southeastern United States and the Caribbean. It claims more than 1,000 pools renovated or resurfaced with glass tile, in both commercial and residential markets, and pitches the material as long lasting and resistant to acids and alkalis. Visitors can request a free estimate and browse before and after photos and tile collections. Nothing on the site says the company builds new pools, and it does not mention financing, a warranty, a contractor license, screen enclosures or decking, so confirm those by phone. On Google Maps it holds a 4.9 rating from 164 reviews, with 161 of them five stars, one four star and two one star.
