About Greenbrook Pools

Greenbrook Pools says it specializes in building swimming pools, spas, rockscapes and water features, and its Coral Gables listing sits on the edge of a service area the site describes as running from Star Island and Key Biscayne to the Ocean Reef Club, through the Florida Keys and north to the Palm Beaches. The website has separate residential and commercial sections, so it takes both kinds of work, and it says the company has been awarded Best Swimming Pool and Spa Builder in South Florida, though the record does not name who gave the award. The site has only a few pages and shares no details on pool materials, remodeling, financing, warranty or licensing. Google Maps shows a single review, a one star rating, and no posted hours, which is too little to judge the company. Call to confirm what it offers.
