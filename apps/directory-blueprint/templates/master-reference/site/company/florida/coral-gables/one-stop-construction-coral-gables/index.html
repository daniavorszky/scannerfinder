About One Stop Construction

One Stop Construction presents itself as a full service general contractor for South Florida, working from Coral Gables, and pools are one line in a long service menu. The site lists pool installation, renovations, liner replacement, equipment repair, cleaning and inspections next to roofing, windows, kitchens, bathrooms, decks, brick pavers, fences and landscaping. It says it takes residential and commercial projects and has 25 years of experience. There is a financing page with flexible options, and the site says it gives an industry leading warranty on its services. That warranty line appears beside roof and window offers, so ask whether it covers pool work. No pool contractor license number shows up in the record, and nothing describes pool types such as gunite or fiberglass. Google Maps shows 4.7 stars from 82 reviews, 74 of them five stars, and lists categories for general contractor, deck builder and roofing contractor.
