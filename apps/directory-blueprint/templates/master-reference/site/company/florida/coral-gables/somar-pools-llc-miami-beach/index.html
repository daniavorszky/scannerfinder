About Somar Pools, LLC

Somar Pools works Miami Dade, Broward and Palm Beach counties and the Caribbean, with a Miami Beach listing and separate Fort Lauderdale and Miami phone lines. The site says it has focused on luxury pool design, construction, leak detection and maintenance for more than two decades, and it describes itself as a premier, award winning firm without naming the awards. Its builds include infinity edge pools and spa and pool combinations with automation and salinity systems. The menu lists construction, remodeling, repairs, leaks, pool decking, commercial pool service, spa service and repair, chlorine alternatives and Jandy warranty work, which is equipment warranty repair and not a warranty from the company. A published customer review praises a pool remodel. No license number or financing appears in the record. Google Maps shows a 4.6 rating from 16 reviews, with 12 at five stars.
