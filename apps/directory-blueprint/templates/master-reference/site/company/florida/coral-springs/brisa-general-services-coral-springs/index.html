About Brisa General Services

Brisa General Services operates as Brisa Pools, a family owned firm serving Coral Springs, Parkland, Boca Raton and the rest of Broward County. The site says it has built pools since 2001 and lists new pool construction, remodeling, maintenance, outdoor kitchens and pergolas, with its own crew for repairs, leak detection, pool decks, pavers and acid washing. It shows license CPC 1459300, has a financing page and a commercial section, and describes team members who oversee commercial projects. Pre-paid packages and a cost page are also on the menu. The site does not mention a warranty or spa work. Google shows 4.9 stars from 301 reviews, with 289 at five stars and 7 at one star. The site also has awards, a timeline and pages quoting what homeowners, competitors and suppliers say about the company.
