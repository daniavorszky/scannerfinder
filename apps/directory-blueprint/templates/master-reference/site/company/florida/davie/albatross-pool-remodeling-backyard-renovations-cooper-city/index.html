About Albatross Pool Remodeling & Backyard Renovations

Albatross Pool Remodeling & Backyard Renovations works from Cooper City and centers its site on pool and patio remodeling in South Florida. It lists pool resurfacing with Diamond Brite, a glass waterline, coping, pavers, pool decks, patios, walkways and driveways, plus outdoor kitchens and pergolas, and says complete pool remodels take about a week. A one year warranty on sinking or shifting pavers is posted, and the site says the company offers a warranty on its work. A Financing page is in the menu. The site does not say it builds new pools and shows no license number. Google Maps also lists it as a building firm, general contractor and custom home builder. It shows a 5.0 rating from 27 reviews, all at five stars.
