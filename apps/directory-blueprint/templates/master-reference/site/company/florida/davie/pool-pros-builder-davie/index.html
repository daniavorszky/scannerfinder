About Pool Pros Builder

Pool Pros Builder describes itself as a South Florida pool company building new custom pools and remodeling existing ones across Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach counties, with showrooms and a Davie listing. The site says it designs luxury inground pools with custom 3D designs, and it also builds pool decks, pergolas, outdoor kitchens and fire pits. Its menu covers new pools, commercial pools, remodeling, pool features and financing, and it publishes starting prices for new pools and remodels. It says it is fully licensed and insured, uses in-house teams rather than subcontractors, and backs all new pools with a 50 year lifetime warranty. The site cites more than 300 completed projects. Google Maps shows a 4.8 rating from 113 reviews, 106 of them five stars and 6 of them one star.
