About The Time Is Now Design And Build

The Time Is Now Design And Build is a Davie outdoor living company serving Broward and Palm Beach counties, with artificial turf as a main service alongside pools. The site says its in-house team designs, engineers, permits and builds custom in-ground pools, with features such as sun shelves, waterfalls, LED lighting and built-in spas, and it lists a 3D visual design step before construction starts. It also offers pergolas, putting greens, landscaping and lighting, plus patios and hardscapes in travertine, marble and porcelain. The company says it is licensed and insured and stands behind its work with warranties on materials and construction. Financing, commercial work and pool remodeling are not mentioned. Google Maps shows a 4.9 rating from 158 reviews, 156 of them five stars and 2 of them one star.
