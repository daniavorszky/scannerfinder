About Pools and Spas of Central Florida

Listed in Daytona Beach as Pools and Spas of Central Florida, this business links to a website branded Pools 'N Spas that is aimed at Northeast Florida, with a Jacksonville focus and service areas such as Duval, Clay, Nassau and St. Johns counties. Daytona Beach is not among the areas named, so confirm the service area before you call. The site describes custom pool construction and pool renovation, handling design, permits and construction. Clients can add built-in spas, travertine or marble decking, screen enclosures, pavers, fire pits and summer kitchens, and it says it can rebuild a pool enclosure. A financing page is in the menu. The site does not state a license number, a warranty or commercial work. Google Maps shows 3.7 from 9 reviews, with six five-star ratings and three one-star ratings.
