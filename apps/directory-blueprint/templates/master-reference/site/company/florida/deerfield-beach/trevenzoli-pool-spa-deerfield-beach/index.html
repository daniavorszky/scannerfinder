About Trevenzoli Pool & Spa

Trevenzoli Pool & Spa is a Deerfield Beach company that says it specializes in custom concrete pool construction, spa installation and pool remodeling across South and Central Florida. The site describes design, engineering and construction of concrete pools, custom spas designed to pair with a pool, and redesign of existing pools, noting that pools can be expanded, adjusted and updated like any other part of a home. Menu pages cover financing, construction steps, service areas and a service request form, and the company says it has financing partners with options for a new pool. Licensing, warranty, screen enclosures and commercial work are not mentioned. Google Maps shows a 4.9 rating from 115 reviews, 112 of them five stars, with 1 four star, 1 three star and 1 one star.
