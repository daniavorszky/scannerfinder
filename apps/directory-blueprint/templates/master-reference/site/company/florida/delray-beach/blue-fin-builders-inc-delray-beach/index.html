About Blue Fin Builders, Inc.

Blue Fin Builders, Inc. is a Delray Beach pool company whose site says it has taken pool building seriously for 30 plus years. It describes gunite pools and spas, with the shell formed by shooting gunite or shotcrete over steel rebar, followed by tile, coping and a deck in concrete, pavers or marble pavers. The site says it also does renovation and structural repairs to pools and spas. It lists licenses CPC# 1457009 and CGC# 062035, and has a menu page called Loan Information, though the record does not say what it covers. A 2018 copyright date suggests the site may not be current. Warranty and commercial work are not mentioned. On Google Maps it has a 4.5 rating from 26 reviews, 23 of them five stars and 3 of them one star.
