About Endless Summer Pools Of Florida Inc.

Endless Summer Pools of Florida is a Delray Beach luxury pool builder led by brothers Ross and Tommy Reardon, according to its site. It says the company owns a form-and-steel division and a gunite company, so much of the structural work stays in-house, and it claims over 50 years of combined experience. It talks about starting the pool design while a home is still under construction. The site says it partners with Lyon Financial to offer clients financing, and it shows license CPC-1458674. Remodeling, warranty, spas and commercial work are not mentioned in the record. Google Maps shows a 4.5 rating from 58 reviews, with 50 five-star and 5 one-star ratings. The site lists two Delray Beach addresses. A form on the site asks whether the client has a property survey.
