About Premier Pools

Premier Pools of Southwest Florida is a family owned and operated pool builder on McCall Road in Englewood, with office hours listed as Monday to Friday, 9 to 4. The site says it has won the Englewood Sun Herald's Best of Englewood Readers' Choice award for pool builder in years including 1997, 2021 and 2024. It shows custom pool photos, calls itself a Hayward builder that is Florida licensed and insured, and offers pool heating options.
