About Straight Line Aluminum Construction, LLC

Straight Line Aluminum Construction is a family-owned aluminum contractor in Englewood that Google Maps files under swimming pool contractor. Its own site leads with gutter installation and says the company has served Englewood, Sarasota and Port Charlotte since 2011. Listed work includes pool cages, screen enclosures, acrylic rooms and custom aluminum additions, plus concrete work, paver installations, handrails, entries and carports. The site says it takes on both residential and commercial properties. It does not say the company builds swimming pools, and it does not mention financing, a warranty or a license number, so treat it as an enclosure and aluminum specialist and not a pool builder. The Maps listing shows a 4.9 rating from 24 reviews, with 23 five-star reviews and one three-star review.
