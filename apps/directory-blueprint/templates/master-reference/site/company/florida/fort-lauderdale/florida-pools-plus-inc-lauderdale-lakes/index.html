About Florida Pools Plus Inc.

Florida Pools Plus Inc. works from Lauderdale Lakes and puts new pool construction and complete pool and deck remodeling at the top of its site. Its page copy describes certified pool contractors taking commercial and residential jobs, along with resurfacing, coping, tile, and renovations. Equipment listed includes Jandy automation, salt systems, variable speed pumps and LED lighting, and deck options include brick pavers, travertine and marble. A pool project financing page is part of the menu, and the site also mentions a pool supply store. The site offers free quotes and lists partner brands it works with. No license number appears, and nothing in the record covers warranties, so ask about both before you sign. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 10 reviews, all five stars.
