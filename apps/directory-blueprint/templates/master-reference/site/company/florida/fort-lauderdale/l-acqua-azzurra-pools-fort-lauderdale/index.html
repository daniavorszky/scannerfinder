About L'Acqua Azzurra Pools

L'Acqua Azzurra Pools works out of Fort Lauderdale and says it covers all of Broward County and southern Palm Beach County. The site is built around pool maintenance and pool system repair, with heater service, system upgrades and leak detection. It also offers pool remodeling, including replastering and refinishing with brands such as River Rok and Diamond Brite, and paver pool decks. It describes a husband and wife team, a factory-trained crew, and services for homeowners and businesses, and it shows a Licensed & Insured banner. It mentions plastering a new pool, but nothing says it builds new pools. Financing and warranty are not mentioned. On Google Maps it is marked Latino-owned and women-owned, offers help in English, Portuguese and Spanish, and holds a 4.9 rating from 97 reviews, 93 of them five stars.
