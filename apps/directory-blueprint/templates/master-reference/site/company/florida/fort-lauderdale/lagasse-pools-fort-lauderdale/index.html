About LAGASSE' POOLS

LaGasse' Pools has worked in South Florida since 1954, according to its site, which says it began as a local tile and pool operation and grew into a custom pool construction company. It takes residential and commercial projects, and the commercial pages cover community pools, hotels and resorts, fountains and features, and cold plunge installations, with the site citing developers and municipalities as past clients. It lists contractor license CPC1461326 and says it is bonded and insured for commercial work. Residential and outdoor living pages mention renovations, paver driveways, walkways and patios, and outdoor kitchens. The record does not say what kinds of pools it builds, and it does not mention financing or a warranty. Google Maps shows a 4.3 rating from 43 reviews, with 34 at five stars and 5 at one star.
