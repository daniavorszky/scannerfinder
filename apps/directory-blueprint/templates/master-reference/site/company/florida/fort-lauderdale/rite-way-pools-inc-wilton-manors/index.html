About Rite Way Pools Inc

Rite-Way Pools Inc, in Wilton Manors, says it has been building pools for 20 years and serves Fort Lauderdale and Broward County. The site presents it as a commercial and residential builder of new pools and spas, with pages for renovations and resurfacing. It lists paver and concrete decks, screen enclosures, salt chlorine generators, automation and other equipment, plus a three year warranty on all Hayward equipment it installs. A financing page names Lyon Financial as a partner. The site gives two license numbers, CPC1456651 and CPC1457882, and describes the Burr family owners as the people customers deal with instead of commissioned salespeople. A review reposted on the site mentions an inground pool build. Google Maps shows a 4.8 rating from 46 reviews, 44 of them five stars and 2 at one star.
