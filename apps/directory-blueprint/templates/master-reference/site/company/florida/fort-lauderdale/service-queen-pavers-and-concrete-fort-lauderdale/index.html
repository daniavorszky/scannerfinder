About Service Queen Pavers and Concrete

Service Queen Pavers and Concrete presents itself as a paver and concrete contractor working across Broward, Palm Beach and Miami-Dade counties, and says it has served homes since 1998. The site focuses on driveways, patios and pool decks in interlocking brick pavers, travertine and poured concrete slabs, with an online quote tool and a three step process of site visit, quote approval with permitting, and installation. It also says it offers pool remodelling, and it advertises flexible financing including 0% financing. The site says it takes residential or commercial properties, but that refers to paving and not to pools. The record does not mention building new pools, a license or a warranty. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 70 reviews, 69 at five stars and one at four stars.
