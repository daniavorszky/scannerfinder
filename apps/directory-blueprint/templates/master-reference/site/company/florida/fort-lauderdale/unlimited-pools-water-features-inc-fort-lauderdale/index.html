About Unlimited Pools & Water Features Inc

Unlimited Pools & Water Features Inc describes itself as a family owned and operated business with more than 25 years in the trade. The site says it specializes in new custom pools, water features, remodeling and major renovations, and its service list adds spa additions, fountains, wet walls, structural decks, paver decking, driveways and repairs. It gives a state license number, CPC1458641, and lists addresses in Fort Lauderdale and Pompano Beach. The site says its mission is to build custom pools with excellence and lasting relationships, and it credits customer referrals for its growth. The record does not say which pool materials it works in, and it does not mention financing, a warranty or commercial work. Google Maps shows a 4.8 rating from 19 reviews, with 18 at five stars and one at one star, and no posted hours.
