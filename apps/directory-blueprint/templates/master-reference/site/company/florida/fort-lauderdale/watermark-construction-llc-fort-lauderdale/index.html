About Watermark Construction LLC

Watermark Construction is a family-owned pool contractor and general contractor serving Fort Lauderdale and Broward County. The site says it has over two decades of residential and commercial pool building and outdoor construction experience, and it describes itself as a fully licensed and insured general contractor. Services include new pool construction, pool remodeling and resurfacing, custom spas that can be standalone, spillover or attached, patio and driveway pavers, pergolas, pressure washing and paver sealing. It runs a showroom on North Federal Highway in Fort Lauderdale with pool and patio tile, pavers and finishes, open weekdays and Saturday by appointment. The record does not say what types of pools it builds, and it does not mention financing or a warranty. Google Maps marks it veteran-owned and shows a 4.3 rating from 49 reviews, with 38 at five stars and 7 at one star.
