About Andy's Pool & Spa Inc

Andy's Pool & Spa Inc works from Fort Myers and describes itself as a pool and spa company operating since 2001. Its site shows a state pool contractor license, CPC1458971, and lists services running from cleaning, equipment repair and leak detection to new construction, renovations, automation and paver decks. It says it maintains, builds and renovates residential pools and maintains and renovates commercial ones, and it mentions custom pools, spas and fountains and working with a designer on new projects. The site also says it is an authorized retailer for many equipment products, and hot tub servicing appears in the page title. Financing, warranties and specific pool materials such as gunite or fiberglass are not mentioned. The business has a 4.7 rating on Google Maps from 13 reviews, with 12 at five stars and one at one star.
