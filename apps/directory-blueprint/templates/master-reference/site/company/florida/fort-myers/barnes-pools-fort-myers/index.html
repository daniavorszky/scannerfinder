About Barnes Pools

Barnes Pools has been in business since 1982, when Al Barnes founded it in Cape Coral after years of hands-on pool construction experience. It now lists an address on Old US 41 in Fort Myers. The site calls it a full-service company that designs and custom-builds pools, features custom gunite pools and spas in any shape and size, and keeps a repair department. Renovations that give an older existing pool a fresh look are also offered. The footer shows license number CPC057248. The site is short and does not mention financing, warranty terms, commercial work or screen enclosures, and it names no equipment brands or finishes beyond calling its finishes top of the line. Google Maps gives it a 4.4 rating from 25 reviews, with 18 five-star ratings, 3 four-star, 2 three-star and 2 one-star.
