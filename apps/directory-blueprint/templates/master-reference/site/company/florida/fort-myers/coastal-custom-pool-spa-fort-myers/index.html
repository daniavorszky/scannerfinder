About Coastal Custom Pool & Spa

Coastal Custom Pool & Spa says it has been building pools in Southwest Florida since 1994 and reports over 1,000 completed pools. The site puts Naples first among its service areas, with Fort Myers, Sanibel, Captiva, Marco Island, Estero and Bonita Springs also named, so confirm the service area with the office for a Fort Myers job. Its three service lines are custom pool design and build, pool renovation and remodeling, and outdoor living spaces such as outdoor kitchens, pavilions, lanais and pergolas, with hardscaping that includes pavers and decking. Each client is paired with a dedicated project manager. The site shows license number CPC1461084 and says the company stays available for warranty support after the final walkthrough, though it gives no warranty terms. It does not mention financing, commercial work or specific pool materials. Its Google Maps rating is 4.8 from 88 reviews, 83 of them five stars and 5 of them one star.
