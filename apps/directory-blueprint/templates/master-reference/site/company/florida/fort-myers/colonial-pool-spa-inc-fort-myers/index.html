About Colonial Pool & Spa, Inc.

Founded in 1984, Colonial Pool & Spa says it builds residential pools and services and automates residential and commercial pools around Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Naples and the rest of Southwest Florida. New construction on the site covers design, permitting, excavation, steel, gunite, tile, decking and equipment, and it installs new vinyl liner pools and replaces liners in existing ones. Renovation work lists resurfacing, waterline tile, coping and paver decks, and the site also covers weekly maintenance, repairs, heaters and remote automation. It says it builds pools to suit local lots, with screened enclosures, sun shelves and canal-front views among the features it mentions. It shows pool contractor license CPC 1458219 and offers a commercial bid request. Financing and a warranty are not mentioned. Maps shows a 4.8 rating from 304 reviews, 278 of them five stars and 13 at one star.
