About Oak Wells Aquatics - Fort Myers

Oak Wells Aquatics - Fort Myers is a franchise location of a pool brand that the site says has built pools and spas in Florida since 2014, and the site admits the Fort Myers office is new. It serves Lee, Collier and Charlotte counties from an address on Alico Commerce Court and lists license CPC1461081. The menu covers residential and commercial pools, new custom pool and spa construction, remodeling, paver installation, pool equipment, lighting, water play pools, and competition, hotel and resort pools. A financing page names Viking and Lyon Financial as lending partners. The testimonials shown come from customers in Georgia and northern Florida rather than Fort Myers, which fits the new local office. The site does not mention a warranty or specific pool materials. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 8 reviews, all five stars.
