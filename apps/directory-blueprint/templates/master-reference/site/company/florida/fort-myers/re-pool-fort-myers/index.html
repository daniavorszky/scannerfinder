About Re-Pool

Re-Pool is a family-owned pool remodeling and resurfacing business serving Lee, Collier and Charlotte counties from Fort Myers. Its site focuses on renovation: resurfacing, tile, coping, mosaics, and granite and marble paver decks, with project pages on a waterfront mosaic pool, a screened pool and a marble spa and pool renovation. Headings also mention swimming pool construction and remodeling services, and it offers commercial renovation for hotels, HOAs and community pools. The site says it is licensed and insured, lists warranty-backed workmanship, and mentions a display showroom with tile and material samples, but it gives no license number. It cites more than 25 years of experience. Financing is not mentioned. Google Maps shows a 4.5 rating from 17 reviews, 15 of them five stars and 2 of them one star.
