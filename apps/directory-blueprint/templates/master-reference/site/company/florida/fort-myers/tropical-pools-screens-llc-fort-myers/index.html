About Tropical Pools & Screens,LLC

Tropical Pools & Screens, whose site title also uses the name Tropical Pavers and Pools LLC, installs pools around Fort Myers. The site lists concrete pools and fiberglass pools, with Latham, Sun Pools and Barrier Reef named in the menu, plus a comparison page for fiberglass, vinyl liner and concrete. It also covers pool and spa renovations, patios and decks with travertine pavers, and pool screens and screen enclosures. It says it offers free 3D modeling and has financial partners for financing. The footer lists CGC1522066 and CPC1457626. The site does not say it builds vinyl liner pools, and it gives no warranty terms or commercial work. Google Maps shows a 4.7 rating from 42 reviews: 38 five-star, 2 one-star and 2 two-star.
