About Aqua Pool & Patio Co

Aqua Pool & Patio Co is a pool company on Beal Parkway in Fort Walton Beach with a second location in Pensacola. Its website says the company has served the area since 1949 and describes it as full service for homes and commercial properties. The services listed include custom in-ground pool construction in vinyl and gunite, renovation of existing pools, vinyl liner replacement, replastering and resurfacing, and overlay pavers for worn pool decks. It also runs weekly maintenance, equipment repair, in-store water testing and a showroom with outdoor furniture and grills. The site shows Florida license number CPC 1458704. It does not mention financing, a warranty or screen enclosures. On Google Maps the business holds a 4.2 rating from 139 reviews, 98 of them five stars and 20 of them one star.
