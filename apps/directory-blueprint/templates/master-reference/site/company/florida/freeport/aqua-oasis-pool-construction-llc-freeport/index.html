About Aqua Oasis Pool Construction LLC

Aqua Oasis Pool Construction builds custom inground pools from a Bay Grove Road address in Freeport and lists Destin, Santa Rosa Beach, 30A, Panama City Beach and Niceville among its service areas. The site says it builds shotcrete pools, pool and spa combinations, modern geometric and freeform designs, tanning ledges and water features, and that it renovates existing pools with resurfacing, coping and decking updates, lighting and equipment upgrades. The footer shows license CPC 1460903. Its FAQ says renovation projects begin with a consultation, and it names Okaloosa, Bay, Washington and Jackson counties as well. The record does not mention financing, a warranty or commercial work. Google Maps shows 45 reviews with a 4.7 rating, 41 at five stars and 3 at one star.
