About Southern Blue Construction inc

Southern Blue Construction runs a showroom on Bulldog Road in Freeport, open weekdays and by appointment on Saturday. The site describes swimming pools, spas, outdoor kitchens, pavilions and patios, and it lists concrete services and pool maintenance too. It says the team can build a pool from the ground up or renovate an existing one, and a customer testimonial credits it with finishing a new pool on time. Financing is offered through a named lending partner, and the site footer shows license CPC1495194. Warranty terms, pool materials and commercial work are not covered in the record. Google also files it under concrete supplier categories. Google Maps shows 68 reviews with a 4.5 rating, 58 at five stars and 6 at one star.
