About Florida Leisure Pool & Spa

Florida Leisure Pool & Spa has served the Gainesville area since 2005, according to its site, and covers new pools, renovations, hot tubs, repairs and cleaning. The site says it works with fiberglass pool manufacturers, replaces vinyl liners using Latham liners, and installs new hot tubs and spas. It also has an outdoor kitchens page and a financing page. A Florida pool contractor license, CPC1458232, appears in the footer, and the fiberglass models are described as backed by their manufacturers' warranties. The site describes the liners as algae resistant and UV resistant. Nothing in the record mentions gunite building, screen enclosures, pavers as a service or commercial work. On Google Maps it has a 4.7 rating from 207 reviews, 189 of them five stars and 14 of them one star.
