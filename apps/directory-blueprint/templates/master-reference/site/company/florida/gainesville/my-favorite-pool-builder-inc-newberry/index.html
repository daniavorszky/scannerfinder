About My Favorite Pool Builder, Inc.

My Favorite Pool Builder builds and installs inground pools across the Jacksonville, Gainesville and Lake City area, and its Google Maps listing is in Newberry. The site says it is a San Juan fiberglass pool dealer and also builds concrete and gunite pools, with replastering and remodeling, for residential and commercial customers. It describes designing inground pools together with spas and says it is a Dream Maker Spa dealer. Other menu sections cover patio covers, screen rooms, sunrooms, fencing and the Ecofinish surface, and a Financing page is listed. A CPC license number appears in the footer. The warranty comments in the record come from general advice about plaster products, so a warranty on its own work is not confirmed. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 14 reviews, all five stars.
