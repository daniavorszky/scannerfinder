About Southern Escapes LLC

Southern Escapes LLC is a Gainesville company that builds, renovates and maintains swimming pools for homes and businesses. The site lists new pool construction, inground pools, remodels from minor repairs to full tile replacement, leak detection and regular cleaning, and says it takes residential and commercial projects. It offers financing options and describes a design process that includes a written specification sheet. One published customer comment describes a screen enclosure built around a custom heated saltwater pool, and another mentions a sister company, Lawn Enforcement, handling the surrounding landscape work. The site gives no license number and no warranty. It says it has two local offices. On Google Maps it has a 4.1 rating from 27 reviews, 21 of them five stars and 5 of them one star.
