About Swim State Pool Service

Swim State Pool Service builds custom concrete inground pools in Gainesville and Alachua County. Its project write-ups describe digging, forming and shooting the gunite, then adding raised spas, sun shelves, paver decks and, on older pools, new tile and plaster. The site states plainly that it builds concrete only, with no vinyl and no fiberglass, and it also lists pool cleaning and repair. It describes itself as family owned, says it handles every step in-house, and lays out a five step build process. Financing runs through Lyon Financial, the site shows license CPC1461266, and it says its technicians are Certified Pool Operators. It does not mention a warranty, screen enclosure building or commercial work. On Google Maps it has a 4.9 rating from 62 reviews, 59 of them five stars.
