About Blue Solutions Pools

Blue Solutions Pools describes itself as a three generation family pool and spa builder that has worked in Northeast Florida since 1999 and has completed over 400 projects. The website is written around Jacksonville, while Google Maps places the listing in Green Cove Springs, so confirm that the service area covers your address. The site lists inground pool construction in gunite and fiberglass, spas and hot tubs, remodeling, refinishing, re-tiling, re-decking, travertine decks, screen enclosures, cleaning, and repairs. It has a financing page, refers to Florida State Certified Pool Contractor credentials and membership in the FSPA, and mentions lifetime structural warranties on its pool shells and a 35 year warranty on a hot tub line. Commercial work is not mentioned. Google Maps shows a 3.8 rating from 25 reviews, with 17 five star ratings and 7 one star ratings.
