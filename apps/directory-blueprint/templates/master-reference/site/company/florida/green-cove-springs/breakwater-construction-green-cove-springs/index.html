About Breakwater Construction

Breakwater Construction is a custom pool builder in Green Cove Springs, and its site headlines custom pools, spas and screened enclosures. The company lays out its process in a pool school and FAQ series that puts build time at two to four months, with payments tied to six or seven project milestones and a project manager guiding the work. It offers a pricing estimator and a financing page that names Lyon Financial and HFS Financial. The site says the company provides a two year warranty covering craftsmanship and material defects. It lists two license numbers, CPC1457423 and CBC1250908. The record does not say whether the company builds gunite or fiberglass pools, or whether it takes commercial work. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 292 reviews, with 291 at five stars and one at two stars.
