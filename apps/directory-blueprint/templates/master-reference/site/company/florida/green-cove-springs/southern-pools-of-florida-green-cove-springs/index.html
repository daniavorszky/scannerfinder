About Southern Pools of Florida

Southern Pools of Florida is a family owned pool builder serving Green Cove Springs, Clay County and the Jacksonville area, with the owners doing the work with one dedicated crew, per the site. It builds custom concrete pools, including a packaged concrete model, and installs fiberglass pools from its Imagine Pools line. It also does spillover spas, resurfacing, tile, decking, full renovations, equipment and automation, and it will add a screen enclosure. The site says plainly that it does not install vinyl liner pools. Pavers are its preferred deck finish. It shows contractor license CPC1459857, mentions financing through Lyon Financial, and says the fiberglass shells carry a manufacturer structural warranty and a gelcoat warranty, on top of its own workmanship coverage. Commercial work is not mentioned. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 46 reviews, all five stars.
