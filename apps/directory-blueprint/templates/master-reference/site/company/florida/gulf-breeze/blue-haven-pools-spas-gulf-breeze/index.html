About Blue Haven Pools & Spas

Blue Haven Pools & Spas serves Gulf Breeze with a site that describes new pool construction, renovations, resurfacing, equipment installation and repairs. The company lists locations across Florida and one in Mobile, Alabama, and its site says its inground pool construction is based in Florida and Alabama. A customer review posted on the site praises a gunite pool build with a deck and enclosure, and the site's build outline covers decks in pavers, natural stone or stamped concrete. It says its designers can share pool financing information, and it claims to be the only builder with an in-house warranty team, with Warranty and Financing pages in the menu. The site gives no license number and does not describe fiberglass, vinyl liner or commercial work. On Google Maps it has a 4.7 rating from 574 reviews, 522 of them five stars and 31 of them one star.
