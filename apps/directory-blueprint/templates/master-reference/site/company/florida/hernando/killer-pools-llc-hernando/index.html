About Killer Pools, LLC

Killer Pools, LLC builds custom pools out of Hernando, and its site title points to The Villages. The company says its designs are tailored to budget and style, and it produces personalized renderings so customers can see a new pool in their own backyard. It also advertises pool remodels for outdated pools. The site publishes pool packages in several sizes, such as a 12 by 24 rectangle and a 14 by 28 figure eight with a sun shelf, with equipment lists. One package includes an eight foot screen enclosure, travertine coping and paver decking, and the equipment package carries a three year warranty. A financing page lists three partners, including HFS. No contractor license number appears in the record. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 24 reviews, all five stars.
