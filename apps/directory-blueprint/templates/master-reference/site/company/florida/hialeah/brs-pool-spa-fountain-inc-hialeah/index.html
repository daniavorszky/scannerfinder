About BRS Pool Spa & Fountain, Inc.

BRS Pool Spa & Fountain is a Hialeah company focused on commercial swimming pools for condominiums, hotels and similar facilities. The site says it has provided pool services since 2001 and lists full service maintenance, water management programs, chemical delivery, CPO and AFO education, installation and repair, and renovations, along with new pool construction. It says it can assign dedicated staff who work only at your facility, and that certified professionals report on site daily. It advertises a 1-year warranty on its commercial pool construction and maintenance work. It says it serves municipal, institutional, competition and leisure pools. The record shows no license number and no financing. On Google Maps it has a 4.6 rating from 9 reviews, 8 of them five stars and 1 of them one star.
