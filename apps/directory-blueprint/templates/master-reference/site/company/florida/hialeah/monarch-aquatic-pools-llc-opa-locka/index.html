About MONARCH AQUATIC POOLS LLC

Monarch Aquatic Pools presents residential and commercial pool design, construction, renovation, restoration, automation and maintenance from an Opa-locka listing. Its menu points to hotels and resorts, high-rise pools, penthouse pools, estate pools and Miami projects. Listed services include infinity edge and glass pools, structural upgrades and resurfacing of existing pools, and pool deck construction in stone, porcelain and architectural systems. Commercial construction is described as large-scale pools for resorts, communities and hotels, and the site says each project starts with planning and engineering of the water system, structure and finishes. Pool automation is also listed as a service. The record shows no license number, financing, warranty or spa information. Google Maps has a single review, five stars, so the 5.0 rating rests on very little.
