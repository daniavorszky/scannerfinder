About Focco Pool Builders of Miami

Focco Pool Builders of Miami is a pool construction company with a North Miami listing that, according to its site, serves Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach counties. It says it handles brand-new pool installation as well as remodeling and renovation, from updated finishes to structural repairs. The site adds that it can take care of pavers and outdoor kitchens alongside pool construction, and that it takes residential and commercial projects, including resorts and businesses. A CPC license number is shown on the page, and free quotes are advertised, and the site describes a step-by-step build process from design consultation to completion. The record does not mention financing, warranties, screen enclosures or specific pool materials. On Google Maps it has 16 reviews and a 5.0 rating, all five stars.
