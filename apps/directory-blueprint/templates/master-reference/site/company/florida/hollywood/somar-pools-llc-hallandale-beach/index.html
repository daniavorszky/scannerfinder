About Somar Pools, LLC

Somar Pools works in Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach counties and the Caribbean, according to its website, and it lists separate phone lines for Fort Lauderdale and Miami. It says it has practiced pool design and construction in South Florida for more than two decades. The service menu covers construction, remodeling, repairs, leak detection, pool service, commercial pool service, spa service and repair, pool decking and automation. Project write-ups describe spa and pool combinations, and a published customer review praises a pool remodel. The menu also lists Jandy warranty work, which points to repairs under an equipment maker's warranty rather than a warranty on its own construction, so that stays unconfirmed. No license number or financing page appears in the record. Google Maps shows 4.7 from 46 reviews, 41 of them five stars, and no posted hours.
