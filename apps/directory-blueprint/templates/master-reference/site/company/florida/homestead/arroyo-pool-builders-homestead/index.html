About Arroyo Pool Builders

Arroyo Pool Builders is a Homestead listing whose website presents the company as Arroyo Pools, a custom builder working across Miami-Dade and South Florida, with service pages for Miramar, Weston, Pinecrest, Coral Gables and Key Biscayne. The site says it builds custom concrete pools and offers a fixed-scope 12 by 24 concrete configuration, and it argues for concrete over fiberglass or above-ground pools. Remodeling covers resurfacing, tile, lighting, spa additions and structural work. Projects are said to start in the mid $50,000 range, with a cost page listing bands up to $300,000 and more. The site shows State Certified Contractor number CPC1458626 and says the company is led by a State Certified Commercial Contractor, though it frames that work as residential. Financing and warranty are not mentioned. Google Maps shows 4.4 from 54 reviews, 45 of them five stars.
