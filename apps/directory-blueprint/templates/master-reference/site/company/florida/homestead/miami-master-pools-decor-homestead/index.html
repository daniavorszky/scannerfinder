About Miami Master Pools & Decor

Miami Master Pools & Decor is a Homestead pool contractor with a 4.6 rating from 60 reviews: 54 five stars, five one-star ratings and one three-star rating. Its website is titled as pool construction and renovation, and it lays out a five-step process running from concept and plan through design, construction and launch. Customer reviews posted on the site praise the owner, Alexi, for a complete pool remodel and for re-plastering a 1978 pool with new coping, waterline tile, skimmer and light. The site text does not mention pool materials, spas, decks, financing, a license number or a warranty, so ask about those directly. The business describes itself as working across the Miami area.
