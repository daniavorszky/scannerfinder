About Sugarmill Woods Pool & Spa

Sugarmill Woods Pool & Spa serves Homosassa, Sugarmill Woods and Citrus County, and its site says it has been in business since 2008 with a team that has over 20 years of pool building experience. It describes itself as licensed and insured and as a Florida Swimming Pool Association member. Services listed are concrete pool installation, custom design with 3D renderings, remodeling, paver decks and driveways, resurfacing, equipment, maintenance and cleaning, and new screen enclosures or rescreening. Financing options are offered, and the menu also lists pool covers, fountains, waterfalls and safety fences. The record mentions no license number, warranty or commercial work. On Google Maps it has 261 reviews and a 5.0 rating, with 259 five-star ratings, and the site says it also has 162 direct reviews of its own.
