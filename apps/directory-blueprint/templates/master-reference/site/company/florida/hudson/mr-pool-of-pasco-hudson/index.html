About Mr Pool of Pasco

Mr. Pool of Pasco & Crystal River is a family owned retailer of above-ground pools, portable spas and hot tubs, with showrooms in Hudson and Crystal River and a start date of 1981 on its site. It lists several above-ground pool brands and hot tub lines, plus supplies such as pumps, filters, liners, chemicals and covers. The site advertises professional delivery and installation, and a customer review on it describes a first above-ground oval pool. It also mentions no-money-down financing for qualified buyers. Inground work, licensing, warranty and commercial jobs are not covered in the record, so it fits best as an above-ground pool and spa supplier. Google Maps shows 275 reviews with a 4.6 rating, 226 at five stars and 20 at one star.
