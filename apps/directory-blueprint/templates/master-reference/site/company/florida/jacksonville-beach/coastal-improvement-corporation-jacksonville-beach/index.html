About Coastal Improvement Corporation

Coastal Improvement Corporation is a general contractor first and a pool builder second. Its site describes itself as a licensed and insured Florida general contractor and roofing contractor since 1991, working on custom homes, additions, renovations and commercial projects across the state. Among those services it includes complete in-ground pool installation and demolition, and it says it designs custom pools around each client's property and budget. The pool section is a small part of a construction-focused site, so look at it as a general contracting firm that takes on pool jobs. Remodeling of existing pools, financing and warranty terms are not mentioned, and no license number is shown. Google Maps shows a 4.5 rating from 15 reviews, 13 of them five stars.
