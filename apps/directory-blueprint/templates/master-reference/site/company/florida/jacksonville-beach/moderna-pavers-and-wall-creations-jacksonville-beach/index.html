About Moderna Pavers and Wall Creations

Moderna Pavers is a paver and outdoor living contractor in the Jacksonville Beach area, and the pool contractor label on Google Maps is a poor guide to what it does. The site says it has installed paver driveways in north Florida since 2006, with owner Justin Canova on site at every job. Other pages cover paver design, pricing, an FAQ and outdoor living projects. Swimming pools, hot tubs and screen rooms appear only in a list of features a homeowner might add to a yard, and the company says it works with outside experts for those, so it does not present itself as a pool builder. It states that it is fully licensed and insured to $1,000,000 per occurrence. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 4 reviews, all five stars.
