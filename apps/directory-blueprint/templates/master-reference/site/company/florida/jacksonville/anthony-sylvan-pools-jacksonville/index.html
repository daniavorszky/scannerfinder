About Anthony & Sylvan Pools

Anthony & Sylvan Pools runs a Jacksonville office of a national company that says it has been building pools since 1946. Its site covers build, renovate and maintain, with custom concrete inground pools as the stated specialty, fiberglass pools in select markets, plunge pools, swim spas and turnkey designs. Renovation pages list resurfacing, coping, tile and decking, mastic repair and pool covers. It advertises a lifetime warranty and flexible financing options. The record gives no license number and no mention of commercial work. Maps lists wheelchair accessible seating and restroom. Whether fiberglass is offered in Jacksonville itself is unclear, so confirm by phone. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 82 reviews, 80 of them five stars, one four star and one three star.
