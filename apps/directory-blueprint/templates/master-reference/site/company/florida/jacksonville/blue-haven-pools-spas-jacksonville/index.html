About Blue Haven Pools & Spas

Blue Haven Pools & Spas in Jacksonville presents itself as a custom inground gunite pool builder, and the site says the brand has built pools since 1954. Its pages cover construction, renovations, resurfacing, equipment, repairs and a warranty page. Deck installation is described as pavers, natural stone or stamped concrete, and pool designers can explain financing options. The site says it is the only builder with an in-house warranty team, which is its own claim. Maps also lists concrete contractor, hot tub repair and pool supply store subtypes, but the record does not confirm spa building, screen enclosures, commercial work or a license number. Google Maps shows a 4.4 rating from 203 reviews, 171 of them five stars and 24 of them one star.
