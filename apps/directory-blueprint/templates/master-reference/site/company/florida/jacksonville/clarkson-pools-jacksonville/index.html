About Clarkson Pools

Clarkson Pools is a family owned Jacksonville company that says it has designed and built pools since 1990 across Northeast Florida. Its site lists design and build, residential pools, commercial pools, renovations, outdoor living, service and repairs, and financing. It describes custom pools with spas, cabanas, pergolas and outdoor kitchens, renovation options such as new waterline tile, upgraded pool decks and spa additions, and a warranty with lifelong support. The site also says it is a licensed commercial pool contractor and claims more than 100 industry awards. A review on the site refers to a Clarkson installed inground pool. It does not say which pool materials it builds. Google Maps shows a 4.5 rating from 293 reviews, 248 of them five stars and 31 of them one star.
