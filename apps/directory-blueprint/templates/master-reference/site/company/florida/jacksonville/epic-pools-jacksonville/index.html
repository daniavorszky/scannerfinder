About Epic Pools

Epic Pools builds custom inground pools and spas in Jacksonville and across Duval and St. Johns counties, and its site says it has done so for over 17 years. It lists residential and commercial new construction, remodeling, service and repair, financing and an FAQ. A client review on the site describes an inground gunite pool. The site puts typical inground builds at $60,000 to $75,000, and says most finish in 8 to 12 weeks once construction begins. Renovations cover new plaster, tile, coping, equipment, sun shelves and spa additions. It shows license CPC1457438 and lists a limited lifetime warranty on pebble finish and a 3 year warranty on equipment. Google Maps shows a 4.7 rating from 109 reviews, 97 of them five stars and 6 of them one star.
