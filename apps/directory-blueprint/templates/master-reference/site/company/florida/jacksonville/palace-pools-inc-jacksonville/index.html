About Palace Pools Inc

Palace Pools Inc has served Jacksonville and Northeast Florida since 1991, according to its site, and builds inground pools and spas along with remodeling and maintenance. The site shows a Florida certified swimming pool contractor license, CPC048227, and mentions an A+ Better Business Bureau rating. Menus list a construction process page, water features, renovation, service and repairs, full pool services, pool cleaning and financing, plus a customer stories page. A warranty link appears in the site navigation, but its terms are not in the record, and pool materials and commercial work are not spelled out. Google Maps shows a 4.1 rating from 80 reviews, 58 of them five stars and 14 of them one star, with online estimates and onsite services listed.
