About Parry Pools

Parry Pools has served Jacksonville and Northeast Florida since 1975 and calls itself a trusted pool builder. The site says it handles new pool construction, renovations, repairs, inspections and maintenance for residential and commercial clients, from apartment complexes to backyard pools. It adds that William Parry III joined in 1989 to expand into full pool builds. Its process covers permitting, 3D design, construction and waterproofing, decking, tile and finish installation, and it mentions integrated spas, sun shelves and water features. Two license numbers, CPC 056638 and CPC 044955, appear on the site. No financing or warranty is mentioned. Google Maps shows a 4.2 rating from 15 reviews, 12 of them five stars and three at one star. The site offers a request-a-quote form and a Jacksonville phone line.
