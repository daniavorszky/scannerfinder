About Poolside Designs, Inc

Poolside Designs, Inc. is a Jacksonville custom pool company at 3741 San Jose Place, with a license listed as CPC1456579. The site says it builds inground pools and custom concrete pool and spa projects, and offers full renovations with new finishes, lighting and tile. It also mentions custom pool enclosures, paver decks, fire features, outdoor kitchens and pergolas, and it offers 3D pool design. It describes itself as an authorized dealer, links to a Hayward page, and has a financing page. The record does not mention a warranty, fiberglass, vinyl liner or commercial work. The site calls the company Northeast Florida's premier custom pool builder, which is its own claim. Google Maps shows a 4.6 rating from 21 reviews, 19 of them five stars and two at one star.
