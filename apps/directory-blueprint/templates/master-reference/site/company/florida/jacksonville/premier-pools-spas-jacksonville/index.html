About Premier Pools & Spas

Premier Pools & Spas of Jacksonville is a local office of a national builder, and the site calls the brand the largest pool builder in the world, which is its own claim. The Jacksonville pages cover gunite, fiberglass and vinyl inground pools, spas and hot tubs, spools, commercial pools, remodeling, fire and water features, lighting and outdoor living. The site puts typical budgets at $75,000 to $90,000 and mentions screen enclosures and decking upgrades as items that raise the total. It has a financing page and shows License CPC1459957 at a Philips Highway address. The record does not give a warranty. Google Maps shows a 4.7 rating from 89 reviews, 77 of them five stars and 6 of them one star.
