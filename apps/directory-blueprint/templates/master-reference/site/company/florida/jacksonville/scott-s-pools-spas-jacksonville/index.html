About Scott's Pools & Spas

Scott's Pools & Spas builds custom inground pools in Jacksonville and says it focuses only on new construction, not maintenance, cleaning or remodels. The site lists concrete shotcrete pools, integrated or standalone spas, water features, aluminum framed pool cages, and hardscapes and pavers. It puts custom builds at $100,000 to $200,000, with some starting at $60,000, and says a free 3D design is included. The process runs from consultation through permits, construction and a pool school handoff. It states financing is available for qualified homeowners, calls itself licensed and insured, and has a Warranty Information page. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 69 reviews, all five stars. The record does not mention fiberglass, vinyl or commercial work.
