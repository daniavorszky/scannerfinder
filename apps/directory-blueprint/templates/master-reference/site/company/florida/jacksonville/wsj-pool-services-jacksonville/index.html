About WSJ Pool Services

WSJ Pool Services is the pool arm of WSJ Remodeling, Handyman and Pool Services, a Jacksonville home services company. The site says it offers kitchen and bathroom remodeling, handyman work and home renovation alongside pool services described as installation, cleaning, repair and renovation. It says it has more than 15 years of experience, describes itself as fully licensed and insured in Florida, and says it offers flexible financing on home improvement projects. Its Google Maps categories are pool cleaning and pool repair. Because pool work is one line of a broad home services business, treat it as a service and renovation option, and confirm by phone whether it builds new pools. Nothing in the record mentions a warranty. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 17 reviews, all five stars.
