About Artech Pools Inc

Artech Pools Inc, listed in Jupiter, presents itself as a builder of custom pools and spas and says it has designed pools since 2000. Its menu has inground pools, pool shapes and materials, and financing, and its service list adds pool and deck remodeling, waterfalls, outdoor kitchens, leak detection, maintenance, equipment installation and screen enclosures. A manager named Jairo Rojas is described as a professional engineer and a certified pool contractor. The site summary oddly refers to another company and its national locations, so confirm details by phone. Google shows 4.4 stars from 13 reviews, 11 at five stars and 2 at one star. The site says the engineering side has also designed septic systems and other backyard projects. It also lists a free estimate request form.
