About RHR Pools

RHR Pools, Inc. builds custom swimming pools and spas in Jupiter and the Palm Beaches, with a showroom, and the site also mentions new pools and renovations for backyards of any size. Its construction page walks through excavation, steel, a reinforced gunite shell and plumbing inspections, and the FAQ covers pool barrier rules and decking choices such as marble and brick pavers. It offers two financing options, lists tile, stucco and pebble finish suppliers, and shows license CPC1459849, bonded and insured. No warranty is mentioned. Google shows 4.2 stars from 36 reviews, 27 at five stars and 6 at one star. It names Pentair, National Pool Tile Group, Florida Stucco and Pebble Tec as product partners, and its Commerce Way address is in Jupiter.
