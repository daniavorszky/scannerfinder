About Crystal Clear Pools

Crystal Clear Pools builds new pools in the Lower Keys from a base in Summerland Key. Its site says it specializes in concrete and gunite pool installations, and the menu also has a Fiberglass Pools page. The page title says it takes commercial as well as residential projects. The company describes itself as fully licensed and insured, but no license number is shown. A Financing Your Pool page walks through general ways to pay for a pool, such as home equity loans and low interest credit cards, so it reads as guidance rather than a lender partnership. The gunite page discusses in-ground pools and built-in spas as design possibilities, and it explains that gunite pools need regular upkeep and occasional resurfacing of the interior. The site does not mention a warranty or remodeling work. Google Maps shows a 4.6 rating from 9 reviews, 8 of them five stars and 1 at one star, with weekday hours from 8 to 5.
