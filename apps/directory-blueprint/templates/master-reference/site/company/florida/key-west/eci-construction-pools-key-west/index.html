About ECI Construction & Pools

ECI Construction & Pools is a general and marine construction company in Key West that also lists pool construction. Its site says it serves the Lower Florida Keys from Key West to Big Pine, and that it is a certified building contractor and certified pool contractor. Projects include new construction, renovations and repairs, with a start-to-finish client process ending in a final walk through. The site calls itself a pool builder providing outdoor living space, and it has a Pools and Decks page. Its commercial wording refers to buildings and marinas, so it does not show commercial pool work. It does not mention financing or warranties, and it does not say which pool types it builds, so ask about gunite, fiberglass or vinyl before you plan around it. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 4 reviews, all five stars.
