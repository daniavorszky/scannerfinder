About Premier Pools of The Keys

Premier Pools of The Keys builds swimming pools and spas in Key West, with Summerland Key and Big Coppitt Key also named on the site. The site describes a locally owned company founded by Frank Herrada and says it builds above ground and inground pools. Its process pages walk through excavation, steel placement, plumbing, shotcrete, tile and coping, electrical, interior finishes and cleanup. It also talks about updating tired pools with water features or new decking, and it has a pool service and maintenance page. The site says it is the only pool builder in the lower Keys, which is its own claim. It does not mention financing, warranties or a license number in the material read here. Google Maps shows a 4.8 rating from 17 reviews, 16 of them five stars and 1 of them one star.
