About Osceola Aquatics Inc.

Osceola Aquatics serves Osceola County, Kissimmee and St. Cloud, and the site describes it as a pool builder with more than 20 years in the area. Its pages walk through new pool construction step by step, from design and layout through excavation, steel, gunite, plumbing, deck and tile, with paver or travertine decks as an option and spas built alongside pools. A separate renovation team handles resurfacing, remodels and renovations for commercial and residential pools, and the menu also lists pool service, pool supplies and water treatment. The site does not mention financing, a license number or a warranty. On Google Maps the business has a 3.9 rating from 33 reviews, 20 of them five stars and 7 of them one star.
