About My Favorite Pool Builder, Inc.

My Favorite Pool Builder, Inc. is listed in Lake City, and its site presents a Jacksonville area company that also names Gainesville, Lake City, Tallahassee and Valdosta, Georgia as markets, with separate phone lines for Jacksonville, Lake City and Gainesville. It says it builds concrete and gunite inground pools and spas, installs fiberglass pools as a San Juan dealer, and sells DreamMaker spas. The site lists remodeling, replastering, screen rooms, sunrooms, patio covers, fencing and financing, and says it takes residential and commercial work. The footer gives license CPC1459058. Its menu also lists Cliffrock and Ecofinish products, gazebos and pergolas, and it says it has decades of experience with concrete pools. Warranty terms for its own work are not stated. Google Maps shows 31 reviews, all at five stars.
