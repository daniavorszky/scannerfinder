About Princess Fiberglass Pools

Princess Fiberglass Pools presents a line of modern fiberglass pool models made in Florida, with names such as Elodie, Claudia and Biki, and says its pools are built off-site and installed in days. The site describes inground fiberglass pools and compares them with concrete and vinyl liner pools, but it does not say it builds those. It mentions a certified local network of Florida dealers and installers and a structural and surface warranty, which suggests it works through dealers, so confirm whether it installs directly and where it serves. Its FAQ also covers how fiberglass compares with other pool types. No license number, financing or commercial work appears in the record. Google Maps shows 4 reviews, all at five stars.
