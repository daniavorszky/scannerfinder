About Pools By Bradley

Pools By Bradley designs and builds custom pools and spas in Central Florida, with offices listed for Lake Mary and New Smyrna. The site calls it a custom pool designer and builder, and its menus cover water features, lighting effects, sunshelves and entertainment features alongside spas, with further sections on pumps, filters, sanitizers, lighting and heaters. Home builders get their own page, and the company says well known builders trust it. The footer shows license CPC1457923, and the menu includes financing. The site also says the company received a 2026 service award seal. Remodeling, screen enclosures, decking and warranties are not covered in the material reviewed. On Google Maps it holds 4.9 stars from 261 reviews, with 253 at five stars, 3 at one star, 2 at two stars, 2 at three stars and 1 at four stars.
