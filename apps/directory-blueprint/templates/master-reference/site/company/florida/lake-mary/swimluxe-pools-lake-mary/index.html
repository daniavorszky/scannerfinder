About Swimluxe Pools

Swimluxe Pools sells and installs fiberglass pools out of Lake Mary and says it has more than 12 years in pool design and installation. The site shows a collection of models, some with prices, such as Natal at $6,550 and Cali at $14,800, and describes the pools as quick to install with easy maintenance. It says its fiberglass pools are life warranted, and a warranty registration link and a financing link appear in the site navigation. One collection entry is named Rio Spa, and the menu lists pools and spas together. The site does not state a license number or mention remodeling, decking or commercial work. On Google Maps it shows a 5.0 rating from 26 reviews, 25 at five stars and one at four stars.
