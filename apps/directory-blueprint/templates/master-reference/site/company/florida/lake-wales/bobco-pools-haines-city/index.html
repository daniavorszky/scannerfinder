About Bobco Pools

Bobco Pools, listed in Haines City, describes itself as a custom pool, spa, outdoor kitchen and patio builder covering Central Florida, from Orlando to Winter Haven and Lakeland to Tampa. The site says the company was founded to build custom pools and that it specializes in outdoor kitchens, pergolas, decks and pavers. Menu pages cover pools and patios, spas, equipment and water features, and its blog posts discuss water features and inground pool design ideas. It does not state a license number, financing, a warranty or remodel work. Headings on the site also cover reasons to own a custom swimming pool and a rock pool feature. Google Maps shows a 4.9 rating from 19 reviews, 18 at five stars and 1 at four stars.
