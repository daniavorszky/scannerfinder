About Casual Way Custom Pools

Casual Way Custom Pools calls itself a family owned in-ground pool builder with more than 25 years in business, serving Polk and Highlands counties from Lake Wales. It says it holds state certification as a contractor. The site describes custom gunite pools in many shapes and sizes and also major remodels of older pools, and it says a pool can be finished about five to six weeks after contracting. Deck choices are acrylic Flo Crete, pavers or stamped concrete, and screen enclosures are offered in several styles. Equipment brands named include Hayward, Pebble-Tech and Nexxus lighting, and the site says equipment warranties depend on the package chosen. It states that it does not provide financing but points owners to outside options. Fiberglass, vinyl, spas and commercial work are not mentioned. Google Maps shows 4.3 stars from 19 reviews, with 13 five star ratings, 2 four star, 2 three star and 2 one star.
