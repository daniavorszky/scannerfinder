About The Pool-spa spot

The Pool-spa Spot is a family owned Lake Wales business that says it has more than 30 years in the pool industry and is veteran owned according to its Maps profile. The site says it specializes in new pool construction, pool restoration and pool chemicals, and that it builds concrete or vinyl liner pools, including a budget line of vinyl inground pools starting at 25 thousand dollars with permit and engineering included. It also does remodeling, resurfacing and repairs, and says it can add features such as fountains, waterfalls, tanning ledges and spas. It says it services concrete, vinyl liner and fiberglass pools, works with Lyon Financial on financing, and offers military discounts. Each job starts with a free yard evaluation. The record shows no license number or warranty. Google Maps shows 4.0 stars from 8 reviews, 6 five star and 2 one star.
