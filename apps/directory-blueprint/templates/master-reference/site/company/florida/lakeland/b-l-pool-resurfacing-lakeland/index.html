About B & L Pool Resurfacing

B & L Pool Resurfacing is a Lakeland business focused on surfacing existing pools, and its site says it was established in 1998 with the owner on every job. Services include a fiberglass finish system, exposed aggregate finishes, pavers, and vinyl liner conversion, and the site says it serves Central Florida and offers free estimates. It lists a state license number, CPC 1456633. The site says it is the only company around that offers a fiberglass finish, which is its own claim. It does not say it builds new pools, and it does not mention financing, a warranty, spas or commercial work. Google Maps shows a 4.6 rating from 23 reviews, with 19 at five stars and one at one star.
