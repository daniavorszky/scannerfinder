About Executive Pools Inc

Executive Pools, Inc. is a commercial pool builder serving Central Florida, and its site says it has worked since 1997. It says it specializes in pools for apartment communities, resorts, municipalities, assisted living facilities and housing communities, and also constructs spas, fountains, splash pads, slides and lazy rivers. The site says it has an in house engineer and a state commercial pool license, and prints the number CPC056954. It says it delivers turnkey projects from concept to completion and has completed numerous projects throughout Central Florida. It does not mention residential pools, financing, a warranty, remodeling or decking. Google Maps shows a 3.8 rating from four reviews, with two at five stars, one at four stars and one at one star, and no posted hours.
