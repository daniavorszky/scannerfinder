About Goldsmith Pools

Goldsmith Pools is a Lakeland custom pool builder, and its site offers classic and custom pool lines plus remodels. It says it can guide customers through every step, whether they want a timeless classic or a one of a kind custom pool. The team page lists Levi Goldsmith and Dillon Hornbuckle as licensed pool builders, and the menu links to financing options with named lenders and a page on care instructions and warranty. The financing page carries lender copy from Viking, Lyon Financial and HFS Financial. The site makes its own claim to be the top custom builder in Lakeland. It does not describe pool materials, decking, spas, screen enclosures or commercial work, and it gives no license number. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 52 reviews, with 50 at five stars and two at four stars.
