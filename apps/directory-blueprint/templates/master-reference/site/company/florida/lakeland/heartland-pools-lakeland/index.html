About Heartland Pools

Heartland Pools, which its site also calls Heartland Commercial Pool Services Inc., handles cleaning, repair and resurfacing for residential and commercial pools in Lakeland, Winter Haven, Bartow, Auburndale and Haines City. Its site lists weekly pool maintenance, pump and motor repair, remodeling, complete deck resurfacing, heater installation, and resurfacing for pools, spas and fountains. It shows a state license number, CPC #1458868, and has a financing page. It mentions a finish and warranty in passing, but does not say the company gives a warranty itself. Nothing on the site says it builds new pools. Google Maps also tags it as a hot tub repair service and water pump supplier and shows a 4.9 rating from 8 reviews, with seven at five stars and one at four stars.
