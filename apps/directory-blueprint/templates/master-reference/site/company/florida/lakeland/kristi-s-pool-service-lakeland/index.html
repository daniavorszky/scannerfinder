About Kristi's Pool Service

Kristi's Pool Service is a Lakeland company focused on resurfacing and remodeling, according to its site. It lists quartz, pebble and glass bead finishes, vinyl liner installation, deck resurfacing with pavers or travertine, variable speed pumps, lighting, automation, heating and drain and clean work, and it promotes a makeover in as little as 48 hours. It describes itself as a licensed and insured pool contractor serving Lakeland and the surrounding area, with location pages for Winter Haven, Sebring, Babson Park and Auburndale. The site mentions a one year warranty on its work and a ten year manufacturer warranty on finishes. It does not say it builds new pools, and it does not mention financing. Google Maps shows a 4.5 rating from 99 reviews, with 84 at five stars and 11 at one star.
