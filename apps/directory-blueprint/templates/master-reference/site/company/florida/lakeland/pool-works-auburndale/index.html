About Pool Works

Serving Lakeland, Bartow, Winter Haven and the rest of Polk County, Pool Works describes itself as a family-owned, third-generation business with more than 30 years of pool service experience and over 40 service trucks. The site says it belongs to the Florida Swimming Pool Association. Its menu covers weekly cleaning, green pool cleanup, salt and chlorine systems, repairs, resurfacing, remodeling, electric heaters, pavers and pool decks, water features, supplies and pumps. New Pools appears only as a menu item, with no detail behind it in the record. The site mentions certified pool technicians but no contractor license number, and it does not state financing, a warranty or commercial work. Google Maps shows a 4.8 rating from 784 reviews, 729 of them five stars and 18 of them one star.
