About Waterscapes Pools and Spas

Waterscapes Pools and Spas builds custom pools for the Lakewood Ranch area, and its site lists offices in Tampa and Bradenton as well. It shows classic and freeform pool shapes, describing freeform design as soft curves that blend with a natural landscape, and it also lists outdoor kitchens and pool features. The site says it builds commercial pools for large local developers, and it names club and community projects among its commercial work. It has a Pool Warranty page and a warranty department with its own phone line. The record mentions no license number and no financing plan beyond a seasonal savings incentive. Google Maps shows 213 reviews with a 4.5 rating, 182 at five stars and 19 at one star.
