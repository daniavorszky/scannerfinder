About Aquatic Surfaces FL

Aquatic Surfaces FL is a Largo company built around fiberglass pool resurfacing. Its site describes a hand-laid process that applies a non-porous, monolithic fiberglass coating over concrete pools, and it lists benefits such as durability, lower chemical needs and easier cleaning. It shows license number CPC1461624 on the page. A customer review published on the site says the owner struggled to find a company willing to resurface a pool in poor shape before choosing this one. The record does not say the company builds new pools or installs complete fiberglass shells, so treat it as a resurfacing option and confirm other work by phone. Financing and warranty terms are not mentioned. Google Maps shows a 4.9 rating from 46 reviews, with 45 five-star ratings and 1 one-star rating.
