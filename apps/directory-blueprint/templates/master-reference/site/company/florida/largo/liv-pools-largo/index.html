About LIV Pools

LIV Pools builds custom gunite pools out of Walsingham Road in Largo and describes itself as a design-forward builder for the Tampa Bay area. The site says an in-house team handles design, pricing and construction, shows customers an instant 3D design with an itemized price, and includes Hayward automation and a salt system on builds. Service menus cover new pools and spas, remodeling, decking, screen enclosures, outdoor kitchens, turf, heating, lighting and commercial pools and spas. It says financing is available on approved credit and lists several licenses, including CPC1459998 and a general contractor license. The site mentions a lifetime shell warranty and Hayward's factory warranty on installed equipment. Google Maps shows a 4.9 rating from 30 reviews, with 29 five-star ratings and 1 one-star rating.
