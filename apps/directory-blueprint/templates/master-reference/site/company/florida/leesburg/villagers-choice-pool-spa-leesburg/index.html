About Villagers Choice Pool & Spa

Villagers Choice Pool & Spa is a Leesburg pool builder that says it designs and builds custom pools and spas, with a main focus on new pool installations with or without a spa. The site lists service areas across Leesburg, The Villages, Ocala, Clermont, Wesley Chapel and Citrus County, and one headline claims a pool built in just weeks. It offers financing through Lyon Financial. A customer review on the site describes an inground pool heater installed by the company, and another reviewer credits the team with designing and building a backyard. The site does not give a license number or mention remodeling, warranties or commercial work. Google Maps notes it identifies as LGBTQ+ owned and shows a perfect 5.0 rating from 21 reviews, all five stars.
