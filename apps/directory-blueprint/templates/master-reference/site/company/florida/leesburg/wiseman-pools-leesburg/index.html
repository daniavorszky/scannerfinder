About Wiseman Pools

Wiseman Pools is a Leesburg builder of residential and commercial swimming pools. The site says founder Shawn Wiseman learned the trade at one of the larger Central Florida builders, and it describes the company as a licensed builder with projects ranging from small in-ground spas to million dollar installations with multiple pools and spas. A construction walkthrough explains a concrete shell installed with gunite or shotcrete, in-ground plumbing inspections, and a choice of concrete or paver deck. It adds that a spa can be built along with the pool. The site also links a pool and spa equipment page and the Florida Swimming Pool Association. It gives no license number and does not mention financing, warranties or remodeling. Google Maps shows a 3.5 rating from 38 reviews, with 20 at five stars, 9 at one star, 4 at two stars, 4 at three stars and 1 at four stars.
