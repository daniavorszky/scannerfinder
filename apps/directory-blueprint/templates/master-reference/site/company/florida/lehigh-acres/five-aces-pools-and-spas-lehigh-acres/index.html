About Five Aces Pools and Spas

Five Aces Pool & Spa specializes in fiberglass pools and spas, and the site says it is family owned and operated across four generations. It offers inground fiberglass pools around Fort Myers and shows separate pages for Lehigh Acres, Bonita Springs, Naples and Gateway. The company says the pools come from San Juan Pools, which backs a 25 year structural warranty on its projects, and that financing options are available. The site promotes a quick build, saying a pool can be swimming in about three weeks. The site also stresses keeping pools clean and working well for years. It does not mention remodeling, licensing or commercial work. Google Maps shows a 4.1 rating from 25 reviews, 17 of them five stars, 3 of them four stars and 5 of them one star.
