About JB POOL CONSTRUCTION LLC

JB Pool Construction LLC builds custom pools and handles the outdoor work around them from its Lehigh Acres base. The site lists new construction and renovation, including resurfacing, complete remodels, spa additions, sun shelves and water features, along with leak detection and repairs. A second service line covers screen enclosures, pool cage design and installation, and repairs, and a third covers pavers and travertine for patios, driveways and walkways. It offers a free estimate and 3D design. The site does not name pool materials such as gunite or fiberglass, and it does not mention financing, a license number, a warranty or commercial jobs. The Google Maps listing has a 4.7 rating from 14 reviews, 13 of them five stars and 1 of them one star.
