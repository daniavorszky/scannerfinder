About Florida Pool Enclosures Inc

Florida Pool Enclosures is a family-owned screen enclosure contractor in Longwood, run by the Delahoz family according to its site. It says it has served Central Florida since 1996 and has custom built over 20,000 enclosures across Orange, Seminole, Lake, Osceola, Volusia and Sumter counties. Its pages cover pool enclosures, two-story enclosures, screen rooms, decorative columns, lanai work and rescreening. The site says it is a fully licensed and insured Florida contractor, that its work is permitted and inspected, and it has a Financing page. It does not say it builds swimming pools, so treat it as an enclosure specialist and not a pool builder. Google Maps shows a 4.8 rating from 529 reviews, with 485 five-star and 23 one-star ratings.
