About Orange County Pools & Pavers

Orange County Pools & Pavers is a Longwood-listed pool remodeling and paver company. Its site describes pool remodeling and renovation, pool tile, pool interior finishes, acrylic spray decks, paver installation, pavers cleaning and sealing, and retaining walls, and its description adds landscaping, water features and outdoor fireplaces and kitchens. Service areas named include Deltona, Oviedo, Apopka, Kissimmee, St Cloud, Clermont and Titusville. The site says it warranties its work. It carries Belgard products and links Belgard's commercial paver catalog, which is not the same as taking commercial jobs. It does not spell out new pool construction and gives no license number or financing. Google Maps shows a 4.7 rating from 41 reviews, 37 of them five stars and 3 of them one star.
