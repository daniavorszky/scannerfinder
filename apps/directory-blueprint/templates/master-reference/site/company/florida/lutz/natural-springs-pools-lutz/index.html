About Natural Springs Pools

Natural Springs Pools & Spas is a family-owned Tampa Bay builder with a Lutz office on Whitaker Road, and its site says it started building swimming pools in 1979. It describes designing and building pools and spas from budget projects to backyard resorts, and it offers concrete pools, which it also calls gunite pools, as its custom option. A page on smaller yards mentions the pools, decks and screen enclosures it builds there. The site has a financing page tied to Lyon Financial, shows license CPC057112, and lists dozens of Tampa Bay neighborhoods it serves. Rock pools appear among the residential styles. It does not mention remodeling, fiberglass, a warranty on its work or commercial jobs. Google Maps shows 4.4 from 135 reviews, with 105 five-star and 14 one-star ratings.
