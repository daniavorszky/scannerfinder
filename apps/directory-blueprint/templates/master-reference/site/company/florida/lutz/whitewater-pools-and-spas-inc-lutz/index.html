About WhiteWater Pools and Spas Inc.

WhiteWater Pools and Spas Inc. is a family-owned Lutz company that says it has built pools for over 45 years anywhere in Tampa Bay. The site describes new pool builds from start to finish and remodels that include retiling, new interior finishes and pump and filter upgrades. It also builds custom pool decks, pavers around pools, outdoor kitchens and fire pits, and says it has built and remodeled residential and commercial pools in many shapes. A financial options page is linked, and the footer shows license CPC057151 with a Lutz address. The page footer carries a 2020 copyright date, so check that the details are current. It does not mention spas beyond the name, or a warranty. Google Maps shows 5.0 from 14 reviews.
