About Florida Coast Above Ground Pools

Florida Coast Above Ground Pools is a Melbourne company that says it specializes in above ground pool installation, pool liner replacement and pool repairs, and its menu lists in-stock pools, Atlas pools and pool liners. The site also describes semi inground pool installs across many Florida cities, from Brevard County towns to places such as Apopka and Brooksville. It says about half of its pool customers finance through LightStream, and its menu has an online application. It offers no license number, warranty or commercial details. Because the site names cities well beyond Melbourne, confirm the service area for your address. Google Maps shows 4.3 from 20 reviews: 15 five stars and 3 one star. Google Maps also lists it as a swimming pool repair service.
