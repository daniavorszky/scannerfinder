About Intercoastal Pool & Spa

Intercoastal Pool & Spa is a Melbourne pool builder that says it has served Brevard and Indian River counties since 1992. The site describes custom gunite pools built inground, plus fiberglass installs when they suit the budget or property, with optional attached spas. Its service list covers residential and commercial pool building, remodeling, equipment installation and repair, screen enclosures and summer kitchens, and it mentions paver decks. The company says it has completed over 500 pools, is licensed under Florida CPC055620, warranties its work, and uses materials backed by manufacturer warranties. It also says it offers flexible financing. Commercial work covers HOAs, hotels, resorts and multi-family properties. Google Maps shows 4.0 from 139 reviews: 98 five stars and 29 one star.
