About Martin Pools & Spas

Martin Pools & Spas is a Melbourne firm that says it has built pools in Brevard County since 1996. The site says it builds in-ground concrete pools and shows a steel-reinforced gunite shell in its package descriptions, which come as pool and spa, pool and screen, and pool-only options. Gallery pages cover free-form and geometric pools, including a lakefront double-spa pool, and decking and coping appear as a stage of the build. According to the site, each pool comes with a warranty, and it advertises loans up to $200,000 with terms up to 25 years. Its license is listed as CPC1460488. It mentions fiberglass and vinyl only when comparing costs. Google Maps also lists a pool supply store category and shows 4.0 from 82 reviews: 56 five stars and 16 one star.
