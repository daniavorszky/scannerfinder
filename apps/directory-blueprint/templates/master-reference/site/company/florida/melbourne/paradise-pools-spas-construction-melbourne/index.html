About Paradise Pools & Spas Construction

Paradise Pools & Spas Construction has served South Brevard County since 1982 from 2525 Aurora Road, Suite 104 in Melbourne, according to its site. It says it specializes in pools for existing residential installations rather than builder homes, and it separates new construction from remodeling, with residential and commercial remodel pages. The menu also lists a retail store, a pool school section and a financing page. The site gives its license as RP0064679/SW103 and says it is licensed and insured. It says the Better Business Bureau once named it business of the month. Spa building, warranty and specific pool materials are not covered. Google Maps shows 4.3 from 22 reviews: 18 five stars and 4 one star. Google Maps files it as a swimming pool contractor, spa, swimming pool and pool repair service.
