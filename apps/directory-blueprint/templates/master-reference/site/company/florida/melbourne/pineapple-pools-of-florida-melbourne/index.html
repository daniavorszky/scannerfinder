About Pineapple Pools of Florida

Pineapple Pools of Florida builds modern custom pools in Brevard County and says it focuses on newer homes, condos and businesses. The site says it constructs concrete, fiberglass or vinyl pools depending on the customer's choice, and it also handles remodels, repairs on those same pool types, screen enclosures, summer kitchens, custom decking and coping, and weekly cleaning. It says it works for both residential and commercial pool owners, and a separate page covers kiddie splash zones. The site gives no license number, financing or warranty information, and it does not mention spas as a service. Google Maps shows 4.5 from 54 reviews: 45 five stars and 5 one star. Google Maps files it only as a swimming pool contractor, and the site menu also lists pool repair as its own service.
