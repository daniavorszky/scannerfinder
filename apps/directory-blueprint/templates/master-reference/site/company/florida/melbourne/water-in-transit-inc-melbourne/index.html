About Water in Transit, Inc.

Water in Transit, Inc. is a Brevard County pool contractor with an address on North Harbor City Boulevard in Melbourne. The site says the company has been building custom pools, renovating existing ones and laying paving stone decks, patios and walkways for more than 35 years, and it lists licensed and insured status since 1988 with license number CPC1457906. Other services on the menu are pool equipment installation and repair and pool leak detection, which the site says is built into every new pool. It says it stands behind its work with warranties and ongoing support. The site links an article on reasons to own a fiberglass pool, but nothing states that it installs fiberglass pools. It does not name other pool materials, financing or commercial work. Google shows a 5.0 rating from four reviews, all of them five stars.
