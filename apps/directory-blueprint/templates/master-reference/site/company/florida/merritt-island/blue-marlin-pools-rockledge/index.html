About Blue Marlin Pools

Blue Marlin Pools of Brevard is a Rockledge pool builder that says it has been building pools and spas since 1994. The site has a gallery of recently completed pools, a step-by-step construction process page, customer reviews and an FAQ. It also shows pool equipment and control products, including a salt system with pool and spa automation that runs from a phone app. The menu has a Financing page and a link to Lyon Financial. The record does not mention a license number, warranties, remodeling, commercial work or specific pool materials, so call to confirm those details. Google Maps shows a 4.5 rating from 69 reviews, with 59 five-star ratings, seven one-star ratings, two two-star ratings and one three-star rating.
