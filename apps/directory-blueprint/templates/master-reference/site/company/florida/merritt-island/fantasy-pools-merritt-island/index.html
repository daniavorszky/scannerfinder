About Fantasy Pools

Fantasy Pools of Florida builds concrete and fiberglass pools in the Merritt Island area, according to its site. It sells San Juan fiberglass pools, which the site says have been made since 1958, and it describes fiberglass pools and spas suited to inground and above ground installs. The site also offers concrete pools, pool accessories and remodeling, including spa additions, new paver decks and water features, and it lists stamped concrete among decking choices. It says it partners with a lender for pool loans and links to three loan companies. A testimonial on the site says a pool was finished in 7 weeks from the initial dig. The site does not mention a license number or warranty. Google Maps shows a 4.5 rating from 40 reviews, 34 of them five stars and 4 of them one star.
