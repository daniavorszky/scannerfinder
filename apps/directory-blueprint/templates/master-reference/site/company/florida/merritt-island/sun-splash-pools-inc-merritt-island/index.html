About Sun Splash Pools, Inc.

Sun Splash Pools, Inc. is a Merritt Island pool builder that says it has served Brevard County since 1999. Its site describes custom inground pool construction, pool remodeling and design, and vinyl liner replacement and installation, with service pages for Palm Bay, Melbourne, Indialantic and Titusville among other Space Coast towns. It talks about pool and spa combinations and says adding a spa during the initial build is more cost effective. The menu has a financing page, and the footer shows license CPC1459233. The site does not say it installs fiberglass pools, and it mentions concrete or shotcrete only in a general FAQ answer. It does not mention warranties or commercial work. Google Maps shows a 4.5 rating from 8 reviews, 7 of them five stars and 1 of them one star.
