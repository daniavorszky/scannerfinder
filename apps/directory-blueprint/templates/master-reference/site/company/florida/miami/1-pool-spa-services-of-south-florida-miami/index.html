About 1 Pool Spa Services of South Florida

Family-owned and based in Miami, 1 Pool Spa Services of South Florida says it started in 2013 doing maintenance and grew into custom pool construction and remodeling. The site lists construction, remodeling, maintenance, repair, salt systems and spa and landscaping work, with area pages for Miami, Fort Lauderdale, Kendall, Naples, Cape Coral and other places. It describes its new builds as custom concrete pools with integrated spas, waterfalls and LED lighting, and shows package pricing of $38k to $44k for a 24x12 ft pool and $49k to $54k for a 15x30 ft pool. Older pools can be renewed with new finishes such as pebble. Project photos show decks in paver, marble and travertine. The site says financing is available and that it serves clients in English and Spanish. It does not state a license number or mention a warranty. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 24 reviews, all five stars.
