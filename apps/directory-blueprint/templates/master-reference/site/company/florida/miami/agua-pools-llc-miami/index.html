About Agua Pools LLC

Agua Pools LLC is a Miami company that says it has more than 15 years of experience in pool construction and remodeling and serves clients across Florida. The site lists custom pool construction, remodeling, maintenance, leak and crack repair, Diamond Brite finishes, energy-efficient equipment, waterfalls, fountains, lighting, spas, pool decks, pavers and landscaping. It says it is licensed and insured and works on residential and commercial projects, and it cites 500+ happy customers and 300+ finished projects. The team is bilingual and free initial estimates are offered, and the site lists separate phone lines for Spanish and English. Financing and warranties are not mentioned. Google Maps shows it as Latino-owned with English and Spanish assistance and a 5.0 rating from 6 reviews, all five stars.
