About Blue Star Pool Construction & Services

Blue Star Pool Construction & Services is a family owned Miami company that says it has worked since 2004 on custom pools and patios. The website lists new pool construction, pool remodeling with resurfacing, tiling, decking and lighting, weekly maintenance, heater and pump installation, and patio construction with pavers and decks. It says the company is fully licensed and insured in Florida and offers financing through partners that include HFS Financial. Its menu has a pools and patios page, a financing page and a gallery, and a testimonials section appears on the home page. The site does not name the pool material and does not mention a warranty or commercial work. Google Maps notes it as women-owned and Latino-owned. It has a 5.0 rating from 36 reviews, all of them five stars.
