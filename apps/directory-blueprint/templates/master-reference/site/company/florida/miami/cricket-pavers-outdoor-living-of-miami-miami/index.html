About Cricket Pavers Outdoor Living of Miami

Cricket Pavers Outdoor Living of Miami is a paver and outdoor living contractor that Google Maps lists under swimming pool contractor, alongside concrete, paving and remodeling categories. Its website covers driveways, patios, pergolas, outdoor kitchens and artificial turf, and its menu includes a pools section with new pool construction, pool remodeling and resurfacing, pool deck pavers and marble, and coping and waterline tile. The site says the company installs residential and commercial pavers and describes itself as voted the number one paver company, a claim with no named award behind it. The page title names Miami, Fort Lauderdale and Palm Beach as areas served. Pool material, financing, warranty and license details are not stated. Pavers and decking look like its strongest line.
