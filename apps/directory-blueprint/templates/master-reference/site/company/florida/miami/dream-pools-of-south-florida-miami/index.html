About Dream Pools of South Florida

Dream Pools of South Florida is a Miami custom pool builder whose website is built around new pool construction, pool decks, pergolas and outdoor kitchens. The site says the company is a licensed Certified Pool and Spa Contractor with license CPC1458041 and more than 20 years of experience, and cites 65 or more completed projects in Miami-Dade County. Its build stages describe a rebar frame followed by shotcrete for the shell, and decks finished in materials such as travertine pavers. Services also include 3D design, permitting and a personal walkthrough. The company works with three financing partners, named on the site as Lyon Financial, VistaFi and Viking Capital. Fiberglass, vinyl liner, remodeling and warranty are not mentioned. Google Maps shows it as Latino-owned, with a 4.6 rating from 101 reviews, 89 of them five stars and 9 of them one star.
