About Elite Pools of South Florida

Elite Pools of South Florida is listed on Google Maps with a 5.0 rating from 64 reviews, all of them five stars, and its website trades under the name Pools 305. The site says it builds new pools and spas, and also offers resurfacing, remodeling of existing pools, marble pool decks, tile and coping, and paver driveways and patios. It says it has 15 years of experience, offers water features such as slides and waterfalls, and advertises zero down with 100 percent financing. It also mentions an inground spa added to a custom pool, and free estimates done in the home. The site says it is Miami-Dade certified but gives no license number, and it does not mention a warranty or commercial work. Pages are offered in English and Spanish.
