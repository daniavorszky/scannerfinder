About Gray Dolphin Pools

Gray Dolphin Pools & Construction, Inc. is a family-owned company serving Miami-Dade, Broward and Monroe counties, according to its site. It describes new pool construction, renovation of existing pools, and pool maintenance and repairs, says it can custom design and build the pool a client wants, and says it delivers pools and spas with personalized service. A financing page with an application link says it offers flexible financing, with low monthly payments or special plans. The site says the company is licensed and insured, and it includes pool safety tips, a gallery and references. Decking, warranties and commercial work are not mentioned. Google Maps shows a 4.2 rating from 9 reviews: 7 five star, 1 two star and 1 one star.
