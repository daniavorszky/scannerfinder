About iGUi Pools

iGUi Pools sells and installs fiberglass pools in South Florida, and its website title calls it a supplier and installer. The site says more than 1,000 pools have been delivered, that 17 pool shells, 3 spa shells and 2 tanning ledges come in 7 colors for over 150 combinations, and that builds average about 4 weeks. It describes a showroom of more than 8,000 square feet, monthly payment options through Hearth and HFS Financial, and a team that includes Certified Pool Contractors, in-house designers and financing specialists. Every shell is said to carry a lifetime structural warranty from the factory. The menu also has a concrete versus fiberglass comparison page, though the record does not say the company builds concrete pools. Vinyl liner, remodeling and commercial work are not mentioned. On Google Maps it has a 4.1 rating from 160 reviews, with 111 five stars and 28 one star.
