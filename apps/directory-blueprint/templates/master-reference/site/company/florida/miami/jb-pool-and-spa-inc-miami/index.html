About JB POOL AND SPA INC.

JB Pool and Spa Inc. is a family owned Miami company that says it has serviced commercial and residential pools and spas for more than 10 years. The website, which is in English and Spanish, lists maintenance and cleaning, water treatment, and products and accessories, and it mentions working with brands such as Hayward, Pentair, Jandy, Zodiac and Polaris. Its inspection items include filters, skimmers, drains, tile and grout, and pool decks. The menu has a Pools page, but the record gives no text saying the company builds new pools, so call to confirm.
