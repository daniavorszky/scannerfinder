About Miami Pools

Miami Pools calls itself a full service custom pool contractor specializing in construction of new swimming pools and spas, with help on remodeling, renovation, resurfacing and monthly maintenance. The site says it serves both commercial and residential properties and helps clients design a pool to fit a budget. Pages cover design and installation, services, a gallery and an about section, and an email address is given for contact. The headline calls the company Miami's pool professionals, which is its own claim. The site does not show a license number, financing, a warranty or pool type details, and the Google Maps listing has no posted hours, so confirm those by phone. Google Maps shows a 4.4 rating from 12 reviews: 7 five star, 3 four star and 2 three star.
