About Pool & Patio King Services

Pool and Patio King Service, in Miami, centers on resurfacing and surface work around the pool. Its site lists pool resurfacing, Diamond Brite, travertine, tile, mosaics, Kool Deck, brick coping, spray deck, interlocking brick pavers and water features. It calls itself a specialist in pool renovations and makeovers, and says a renovation consultant visits the property, inspects the pool and makes recommendations. Maps also tags it as a deck builder and pump supplier. The site mentions a warranty in its start-up care notes, saying improper filling can void it. It does not say that it builds new pools, and it says nothing about financing, licensing, spas or commercial work. The listing has posted hours and two five-star reviews, for a 5.0 rating.
