About Pool Service Specialist

Pool Service Specialist trades as Pool Service Miami-Dade and offers pool construction, remodeling, maintenance and leak detection in South Florida. The site says it is a Florida state-licensed contractor for pool and spa construction and remodeling, gives license number CPC1459642, and says it covers residential and commercial work across the state. Its construction process is shown as excavation, formwork and shotcrete, with geometric, free form and classic layouts to choose from. Pool and spa remodeling has its own heading on the site. Maintenance services include salt water pool service and acid wash or chlorine bath. The site says hundreds of residential and commercial customers have chosen it. Financing, decking and warranties are not mentioned. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 14 reviews, all five stars.
