About Pools By German

Pools By German is a family owned company working in Miami-Dade, Broward and Monroe counties. Its website says the owners have more than 20 years in the swimming pool business and that the company grew out of a maintenance-only operation into a pool and spa builder. The site title advertises custom built pools, and the service headings are inground pools, maintenance, renovations and repairs. It says it specializes in in-ground pools and describes its renovations as makeovers of existing pools, with landscaping, construction and design choices. It also sells maintenance plans for keeping a pool in shape. The site does not mention gunite, fiberglass or vinyl construction, financing, warranties, screen enclosures, pavers or a license number. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from three reviews, all five stars, and no posted hours.
