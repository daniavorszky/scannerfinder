About Pristine Pools And Pavers

Pristine Pools And Pavers is a Miami business whose site focuses on consulting and design for pool and paver remodeling, including pool remodel planning, pool deck and patio design, paver design, and plumbing and systems advice. It says that when a project is ready to build, it can connect homeowners with licensed contractors to carry out the work. Customer reviews on the site mention a pool resurfaced with Diamond Brite, a new pool and paver deck, and a commercial pool at an apartment complex. The site lists service areas across Miami-Dade and Broward, including Pinecrest, Kendall, Hialeah and Hollywood. It does not state its own license, financing or warranty. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 11 reviews, all five stars.
