About Shoreline Swimming Pool Contractor

Shoreline Swimming Pool Contractor works in Miramar and calls itself a contractor specializing in custom inground pools and poolscapes. The site has service sections for gunite pools, pool installation, renovation, backyard design, hardscapes and pergolas, plus a section on liner pools that it calls affordable and quick to install. Renovation work can include new tile and coping, decking, pumps, lighting and spa additions, and landscape packages can include spa areas, fire features and outdoor kitchens. Pergola and backyard design are offered as separate services, so the company also covers full backyard makeovers beyond the pool itself. The site does not show a license number, financing terms, a warranty or commercial work. Google Maps lists a 4.9 rating from 48 reviews, 47 of them five stars and one a one-star.
