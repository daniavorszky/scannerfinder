About Picture Perfect Pools

Picture Perfect Pools serves Lake County and The Villages from Tavares. The site says the company has built, remodeled or repaired more than 2,000 pools since 1999. Its services list new construction, remodels, custom hardscapes, equipment installation, weekly maintenance and Hayward warranty service, and the menu mentions a Flagstone Pavers certification. It says it is licensed and insured. Financing runs through two lender pages, Lyon Financial and HFS Financial. The site says it is an official Hayward warranty station, which covers manufacturer equipment claims and is not counted here as a warranty on its own work. Google Maps shows 4.8 from 103 reviews, 96 of them five stars, 6 one star and 1 four star. Commercial work is not mentioned.
