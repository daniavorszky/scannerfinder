About Custom Pools of Naples

Custom Pools of Naples describes itself as Southwest Florida's premier inground pool contractor, a claim the record does not back with an award or ranking. The site says it is a full service pool and spa contractor that designs and engineers new pools, spas, fountains and water features to a client's specifications. Its menu covers pools and spa, custom features and water features, plus a gallery and team page. Copy about adding or renovating a pool on a property suggests it takes renovation work too. The record does not mention financing, license numbers, a warranty, decking or a pool material.
