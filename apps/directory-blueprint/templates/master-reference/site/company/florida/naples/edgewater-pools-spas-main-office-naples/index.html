About Edgewater Pools & Spas | Main Office

Edgewater Pools & Spas runs its main office in Naples and works across Collier and Lee Counties. The site says the company has served the area since 2008 and now has a team of more than 60 people who create and care for pools and spas. Its menu covers weekly maintenance, equipment, upgrades, new pool construction and pool renovations, with location pages for towns from Marco Island to Fort Myers. It says equipment it installs carries the manufacturer's warranty, and it promotes energy-efficient pool care. The site does not mention financing, a contractor license number or commercial work, and it does not name pool types such as gunite or fiberglass. On Google Maps it holds a 4.9 rating from 402 reviews, with 386 five star ratings and 10 one star ratings. Maps also lists online estimates and on-site parking.
