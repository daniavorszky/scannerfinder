About Pool and Patio Works

Pool and Patio Works is a Naples custom pool builder that says its in-house team handles each build. The site lays out an all-inclusive process covering planning and approvals, construction, coping, tile and decking, and pool start-up, with 30 days of pool service included. It also builds custom spas, remodels existing pools and offers high-end pool cage construction, and it says it transforms outdated lanais. The menu includes pool features and pool service pages. Its meta text calls it a top custom builder, which is its own claim. The site does not mention financing, a license number, a warranty or commercial work. Google Maps also marks it as veteran-owned and women-owned, with a 4.4 rating from 46 reviews, 39 of them five star and 6 of them one star.
