About Rock Star Pools

Rock Star Pools is a Naples builder that serves both homeowners and businesses, and its site is organized into residential and commercial sections under the tagline design, build, swim. Residential work includes custom pool design, pools with connected spas and custom remodels, with rockwork and waterfalls as a specialty. On the commercial side it lists water features, resort attractions and municipal projects, and it says it builds commercial pools. The site does not show a license number, financing or a warranty, and it does not name pool types such as gunite or fiberglass. Google Maps lists English and Spanish language assistance and shows a 4.0 rating from 14 reviews, with 10 five star ratings, 3 one star and 1 three star.
