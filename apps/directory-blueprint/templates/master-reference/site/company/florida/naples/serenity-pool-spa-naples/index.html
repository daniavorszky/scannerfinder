About Serenity Pool & Spa

Serenity Pool & Spa is a Naples custom pool builder whose site says it has been crafting pools and luxury spas since 2004, with a portfolio that includes projects in Sarasota. It describes builds ranging from intimate residential pools to resort-style work, and says it also serves developers, community associations and the commercial market, with commercial aquatic construction handled in-house. The site adds fire features, screen enclosures, fence enclosures, decks and landscaping to the mix. It names a warranty manager who serves as the point of contact after construction. The site calls itself award-winning without naming an award, and the record shows no license number, financing terms or remodeling page. The Google Maps listing is a sharp contrast to the marketing, with a 1.0 rating from 4 reviews, all at one star.
