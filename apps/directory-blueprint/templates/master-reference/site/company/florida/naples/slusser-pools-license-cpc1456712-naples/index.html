About Slusser Pools - License CPC1456712

Slusser Pools & Spa is a Naples custom pool builder whose listing name carries its license number, CPC1456712, and the same number appears on its website. The site says the business has built custom pools and outdoor spaces for families in Naples and Southwest Florida since 1980. Its services include custom pool design and construction, pool and spa renovation, spa upgrades, travertine and pool decking, tile and coping, water features, and equipment and plumbing work. The site describes a contract with a final price, and it presents itself as a residential builder. It does not mention financing, a warranty, screen enclosures or commercial work, and it does not name pool types like gunite or fiberglass. Google Maps shows a 4.7 rating from 159 reviews, with 143 five star ratings and 9 one star ratings.
