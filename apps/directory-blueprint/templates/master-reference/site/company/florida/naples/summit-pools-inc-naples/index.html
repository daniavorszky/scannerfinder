About Summit Pools, Inc.

Summit Pools, Inc. builds custom residential and commercial pools around Naples and the rest of Southwest Florida, and it also handles renovations, repairs, and service and maintenance. The site describes a build process that includes 3D renderings, steel reinforcement, plumbing and electrical work, and applying a gunite shell, with spas, waterfalls, lighting and custom decking among the features. It says the company has more than 23 years of experience in commercial pool construction. The menu has a showroom, a payment page and a finance page. The site shows no license number and does not mention a warranty, screen enclosures or fiberglass and vinyl options. Google Maps shows a 4.8 rating from 104 reviews, with 98 five star ratings and 5 one star ratings.
