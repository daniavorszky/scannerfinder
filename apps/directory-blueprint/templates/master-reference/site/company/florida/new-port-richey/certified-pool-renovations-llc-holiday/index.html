About Certified Pool Renovations, Llc

Certified Pool Renovations serves the Tampa Bay area from Holiday and shows license number CPC 1459640 on its site. It says it specializes in custom concrete and gunite pool construction through a design-build process, with add-on spas, water features and custom shapes. Its services also include remodeling and renovation, resurfacing and refinishing, new tile and coping, pool decking, water features and lighting upgrades. It describes coping and edge materials such as glass, ceramic, natural stone and travertine. The site mentions weekly service as something it can arrange. On Google Maps it has a 4.9 rating from 32 reviews, 31 of them five stars and one at one star. The site does not mention financing, a warranty, screen enclosures or commercial work, and it does not say whether it builds fiberglass or vinyl pools. Confirm those points by phone.
