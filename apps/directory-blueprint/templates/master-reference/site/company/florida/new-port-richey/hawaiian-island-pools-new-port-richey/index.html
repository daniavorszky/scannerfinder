About Hawaiian Island Pools

Fiberglass is the whole focus at this New Port Richey pool installer. The site describes a veteran-owned, family-run company that installs inground fiberglass pools across the Tampa Bay area, and it credits itself with more than 30 years in business and over 1,200 pools built. The menu lists a long run of named pool models plus three spa and spillover designs, and the company also builds paver decks and screen enclosures alongside the pool. A Financing page lists several lending partners. The site shows contractor license CPC 1461196 and refers to a lifetime structural warranty backed by the shell manufacturer. It says it handles permits and inspections in the four counties it serves. Nothing in the record points to concrete or vinyl pool work, remodeling of existing pools, or commercial jobs. On Google Maps it holds a 4.8 rating from 102 reviews, 96 of them five stars and 3 of them one star.
