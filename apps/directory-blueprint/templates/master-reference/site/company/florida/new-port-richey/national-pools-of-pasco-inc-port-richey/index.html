About National Pools of Pasco, Inc

National Pools of Pasco builds residential and commercial pools around the Tampa Bay area, and its site says the company has 50 years or more in the business. The construction process page walks through a gunite shell, plumbing, tile and coping, a deck finished with pavers or another surface, and an optional screen enclosure over the pool and deck. It says a standard warranty comes with every pool. A financing page appears in the menu, and the site names community pools it has built, such as Waterchase and Seven Oaks. It lists Citrus, Hernando, Pasco, Hillsborough, Pinellas, Manatee and Sarasota counties as its service area. No license number appears in the text read. Google Maps shows a 3.3 rating from 21 reviews, with 12 five-star and 9 one-star ratings.
