About Piccs Designs Pool & Spa

Based in New Port Richey and serving a long list of Tampa Bay and Hernando County communities, Piccs Designs presents itself as a boutique custom pool builder run by a friends-and-family team with generations of construction experience. The site says it handles pools of all shapes, hot tubs, waterfalls, fire features and custom patio finishes, and it also takes on remodels such as resurfacing, tile updates and full redesigns with new decking. Brick pavers, travertine, marble and limestone are named as materials. It lists two contact lines, one for new construction and remodels and another for maintenance and repairs, and the footer carries license numbers CPC1460641 and CPC1460938. A Financing page links to two lenders. The record does not say which pool materials it builds in, and it does not mention a warranty or commercial work. Google Maps shows 4.7 from 77 reviews, with 70 five-star ratings and 5 one-star ratings.
