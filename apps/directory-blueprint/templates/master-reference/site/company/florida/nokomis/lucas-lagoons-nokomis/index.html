About Lucas Lagoons

Lucas Lagoons is a Nokomis custom pool builder led by Lucas Congdon, and its site says the company was featured on a television show called Insane Pools. The site describes design and construction of luxury pools, lagoons, waterfalls, grottos, spas, outdoor kitchens, bars, lighting and landscaping, and it cites a resume of over 50 design awards without naming them. Its menus include new pools, remodeled pools, a commercial section and a program for authorized contractors, and the site says Congdon has worked on both residential and commercial projects. It serves Central and South Florida, including Sarasota, Hollywood, Miami and the Keys. License number CPC1459096 appears on the site. Financing and warranties are not mentioned. Google Maps shows 5.0 from 5 reviews, all at five stars.
