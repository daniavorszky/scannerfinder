About Swim Incorporated

Swim Incorporated is a design-build pool company with a Nokomis listing and a Sarasota focus on its website, where it gives 2001 as its start. The site says its pools are gunite, with a 3D design shown before commitment, an itemized written proposal, and 4 to 5 months from contract to first swim. It also says it is an exclusive builder in four Toll Brothers communities. Its FAQ describes custom inground pools with optional spas and water features, concrete decks with pavers or travertine offered as upgrades, and remodeling, resurfacing and feature upgrades for existing pools. It says it works with two financing companies, Lyon Financial and Viking Capital, and holds a Florida Certified Pool/Spa Contractor license issued in 2001. Warranty terms and commercial work are not mentioned. Google Maps shows 4.3 from 26 reviews, with 20 at five stars, 3 at one, 2 at three and 1 at two.
