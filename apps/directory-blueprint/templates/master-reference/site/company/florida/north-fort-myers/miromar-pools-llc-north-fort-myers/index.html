About Miromar Pools LLC

Miromar Pools LLC builds custom residential and commercial pools across Lee and Collier Counties, according to its site, with offices listed in North Fort Myers and Naples. The site says the company is Hispanic and women owned, has more than 15 years in the trade, and uses its own crew with no subcontractors. Services include integrated spas, sun shelves and waterfalls, resurfacing, coping, waterline tile and equipment upgrades, travertine and paver decking, and outdoor kitchens and pergolas. It says it partners with lenders for financing. The footer shows license numbers CGC 1534564, CPC1458006 and EC13014603. The site does not say which shell types it builds and does not mention warranties. Google Maps shows a 4.3 rating from 219 reviews, 179 of them five stars and 33 of them one star.
