About Florida Leak Solutions Inc

Florida Leak Solutions in North Lauderdale is presented on its own site mainly as a leak detection company, and it also advertises pool building and remodeling. The site says it is owner-operated, licensed and insured, with more than 15 years of experience, and it shows license CPC1460126. Detection work covers pool and pool system leaks, under-slab and plumbing leaks, meter line leaks and thermal imaging, for residential and commercial customers. A build and remodel menu page points to a pool designer tool for shapes, finishes and features, and the text talks about designing and constructing new or existing pools. Every leak inspection carries a 35 day warranty, according to the site, which also says you do not pay if it fails to find the leak. Financing, pool materials and decking work are not covered in the record. Google Maps shows 4.5 from 8 reviews, 7 of them five stars and 1 of them one star.
