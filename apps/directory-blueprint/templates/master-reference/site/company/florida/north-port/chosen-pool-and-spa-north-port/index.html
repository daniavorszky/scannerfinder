About Chosen Pool and Spa

Chosen Pool and Spa says it has spent over ten years building luxury custom pools across Sarasota, Lakewood Ranch, Longboat Key and the surrounding Southwest Florida area. Its site walks through a shotcrete build, from excavation and steel reinforcement to tile, decking and startup, and it also lists spas, water features, outdoor kitchens, marble decking, pool renovation and equipment service. The company says it is licensed and insured and offers financing to homeowners in a long list of towns that includes North Port. A builder partnership page invites contractors to submit plans for pricing. The site is centered on Sarasota rather than North Port, so confirm coverage. It does not mention a warranty. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 4 reviews, all five stars, with online estimates offered.
