About Gilley's Custom Home Builders

Gilley's Custom Home Builders is a residential builder serving North Port, Port Charlotte, Punta Gorda and South Gulf Cove that says it has worked in Florida for over 20 years. The site is mostly about new homes, home remodeling and detached garages. It does point to a pool builders division, which it says is ready to build pools, and the menu has a Custom Pools page. A Warranty page also appears in the menu, though its terms were not part of the material. Financing, license numbers, pool types and pool remodeling are not covered, and the pool work is presented as one division of a home building company. Google Maps shows a 4.8 rating from 26 reviews, 24 of them five stars, 1 of them four stars and 1 of them one star.
