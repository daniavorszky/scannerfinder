About Gilley's Custom Pools Inc.

Gilley's Custom Pools Inc. designs and builds pools in North Port, Port Charlotte, Venice and Englewood. The site covers custom inground pools in gunite and fiberglass, and says a fiberglass pool can be installed in about a week. It also lists spas, remodeling, outdoor living spaces, natural stone coping and decking, and patio screen enclosures and pool cages. The company says it works with financing providers on payment plans, and its process ends with a final walkthrough and warranty. It also mentions commercial clients. The site talks about the value of hiring a licensed contractor in general but shows no license number of its own. One customer review on the site describes a paver problem that the company later fixed. Google Maps shows a 4.8 rating from 35 reviews, 33 of them five stars and 2 of them one star.
