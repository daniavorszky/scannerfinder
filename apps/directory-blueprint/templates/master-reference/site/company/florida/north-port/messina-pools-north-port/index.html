About Messina Pools

Messina Pools builds custom concrete pools and also handles repairs and remodels, according to its site. Pool packages are listed as starting at 58k, and add-ons include spas with jets, sheer descent waterfalls, automation, salt systems, sun shelves, outdoor kitchens, stone pavers and screen enclosures. Remodel options range from resurfacing and waterline tile to LED lighting, paver or natural stone decks, and equipment upgrades. A financing page names Lyon Financial as a preferred partner. The company offers a free design and quote. The site also shows a repair service option, so it takes on existing pools as well as new builds. A license number, warranty terms and commercial work are not mentioned. Google Maps shows a 4.7 rating from 75 reviews, 68 of them five stars and 6 of them one star.
