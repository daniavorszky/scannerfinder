About Above The Rest Aquatics Inc.

Above The Rest Aquatics Inc. builds and services pools from an Oakland Park address and says it serves all of Broward and Palm Beach counties. The site says the owner's family has 27 years in the industry and builds inground concrete, inground vinyl liner, above-ground and fiberglass pools. Its services include new builds, remodels, vinyl liner changes, repairs, pool service, leak detection and automation. The footer shows license CPC#1459626. A customer review posted on the site says the owner warranties his pools for two years and describes a quick liner replacement. The site does not mention spas, screen enclosures, decking, financing or commercial work. Google Maps shows a 4.4 rating from 106 reviews, with 86 five-star and 15 one-star ratings.
