About Signature Pools & Spas Inc

Signature Pools & Spas Inc is an Oakland Park pool builder run by Scott and Stephanie Taggart, according to its site. Scott is described as coming from a construction background and visiting every job site to supervise and help build. The site shows sections for pools, spas, water features, negative edge designs and small yard pools, and says its inground spas have therapeutic jets. It mentions experience building pools in compact yards. Many of its pools include water features, and its negative edge designs are described as blurring the line between the pool and its surroundings. The site does not state a license number, remodeling, financing, a warranty or commercial work, so ask about those directly. Google Maps shows a 4.0 rating from 23 reviews, with 17 five-star and six one-star ratings.
