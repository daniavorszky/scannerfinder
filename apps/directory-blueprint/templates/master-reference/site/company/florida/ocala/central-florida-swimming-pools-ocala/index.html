About Central Florida Swimming Pools

Central Florida Swimming Pools Inc. has operated from the same South Magnolia Avenue location in Ocala since 1968, according to its site, and describes itself as a custom builder of in-ground concrete swimming pools. Its FAQ says every job is priced individually rather than sold as a package, that it builds in-ground concrete spas, including stand-alone ones, and that it remodels pools, from tile and interior refinishing up to removing a pool and rebuilding. A showroom displays tile, pavers, travertine, water features and fire pits, and there are separate pages for pool cleaning and for financing through Lyon Financial. The site lists a state certified license, CPC029635. Nothing in the record covers a warranty or commercial work. Google shows a 4.5 rating from 71 reviews: 60 at five stars, 9 at one star and 2 at four stars.
