About Cool Pool of Florida

Cool Pool of Florida is an Ocala fiberglass pool installer that says it has served Marion County since 2022. The site describes the company as a fiberglass specialist and inground pool builder, and says it is an authorized installer of Latham and San Juan shells, typically installed in as little as 45 days. It lists a one year workmanship warranty plus the manufacturer's lifetime structural warranty, offers financing with pre-qualification, and shows license CPC1461644 as a commercial and residential pool contractor. Other pages cover pool resurfacing and pool fence installation. The site does not describe screen enclosures or deck installation as its own services. Maps also lists fiberglass repair. Google shows a 4.5 rating from 24 reviews: 21 at five stars and 3 at one star.
