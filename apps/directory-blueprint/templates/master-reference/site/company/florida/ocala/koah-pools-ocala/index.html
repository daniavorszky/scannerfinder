About Koah Pools

Koah Pools is an Ocala builder of custom concrete pools, spas and outdoor living spaces, working across Marion County and nearby Central Florida counties. Its site walks through consultation, site review, design, plans and construction, and says its own shotcrete crews form the pool shell, with a tile specialist and finish selections following. Finished work shows paver patios, stone decks and raised spas, and reviews on the site mention a new inground pool and a renovation. Financing runs through Lyon Financial for qualified homeowners. The site lists a residential license, CPC1460543, and a commercial license, CPC1461375. Screen enclosures appear only as a site feature to plan around, and warranty terms are not described. Google shows a 4.8 rating from 24 reviews: 22 at five stars, 1 at three stars and 1 at one star.
