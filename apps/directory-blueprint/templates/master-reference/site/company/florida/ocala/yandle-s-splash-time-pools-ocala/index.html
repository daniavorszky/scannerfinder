About Yandle's Splash Time Pools

Yandle's Splash Time Pools has built pools in Marion County since 1993, according to its site, which says the owners each hold a Florida swimming pool contractor license. The company describes concrete pools in many shapes, and its building process walks through inspection, the shotcrete step, backfill, tile, plumbing and forming the pool deck. It says a screen enclosure, if purchased, is measured and installed after the deck, though it does not say who builds it. The site has a financing page, and its FAQ suggests home equity loans as one option. It notes that warranty protection depends on the builder still being in business but does not describe its own warranty. Commercial work, spas and remodeling are not mentioned. Google shows a 3.9 rating from 65 reviews: 45 at five stars, 15 at one star and 5 in between.
