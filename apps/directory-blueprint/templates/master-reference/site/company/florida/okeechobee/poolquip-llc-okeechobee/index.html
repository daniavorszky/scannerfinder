About POOLQUIP LLC

POOLQUIP LLC is a family-owned commercial and residential pool builder in Okeechobee. Its site says the founder has more than 34 years in the industry and that the company works in all phases of construction, renovation and repair, from waterparks to custom spas. It names service across Highlands, Okeechobee, Martin, Indian River and St. Lucie counties and gives a state CPC license number. A Financing page says it partners with Lyon Financial for pool loans, and the site invites requests for a free quote on a new pool or remodel. The site also mentions past projects. The record does not mention warranties, screen enclosures or specific pool materials. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 16 reviews, all five stars, with online estimates listed.
