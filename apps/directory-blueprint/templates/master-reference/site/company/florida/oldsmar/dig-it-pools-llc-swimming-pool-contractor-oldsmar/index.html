About DIG IT POOLS LLC Swimming pool contractor

Dig It Pools is an Oldsmar pool builder that says it has served upper Tampa Bay since 2015, covering north Pinellas, west Hillsborough and Pasco counties. Its main lines are new pool construction, renovation, resurfacing, and paver and pool decks, with freeform and geometric designs shown in its gallery. Site text and published reviews mention inground pools, spa additions, water features, lighting, heaters and chillers, and outdoor kitchen stonework. The site says it stands behind its work and will cover the warranty details at the first meeting, though it does not spell them out. One review on the site says the customer compared four quotes before choosing the company. No license number, financing or commercial work appears in the material. Google Maps shows 4.9 from 68 reviews, with 66 at five stars, 1 at three and 1 at one.
