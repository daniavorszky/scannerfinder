About Five Star Pool

Five Star Pool works across Tampa Bay from an Oldsmar listing, and its site says it has built custom pools for about 20 years. The pages cover pool renovations, new pool builds, screen enclosures, outdoor kitchens, and driveways and patios. The site describes custom inground pools, resurfacing with pebble and quartz finishes, tile, and decking work with pavers in materials such as marble and travertine. It names cracked or worn surfaces and outdated layouts as the problems its renovation process targets, and it says it gives quotes after an on-site consultation. It does not state a license number, financing, warranty or commercial work. The phone number is 813.792.7665.
