About Freestyle Pools & Spas

Freestyle Pools & Spas has operated from Burbank Road in Oldsmar since 1983, according to its site, building custom residential and commercial pools across Tampa Bay. It describes custom pools, luxury spas, decks, screen enclosures, fire and water features, and pool remodeling, and it shows renovation projects with new pavers, travertine decks, pebble interiors and salt systems. Its process runs from consultation and custom design through planning and permits. The site lists a license, CPC035658, and the phone number is 813-855-4040. Financing and warranty terms are not mentioned, and it does not say whether it builds gunite, fiberglass or vinyl pools. Google Maps rates it 3.9 from 31 reviews, with 21 at five stars, 8 at one star and 2 at four.
