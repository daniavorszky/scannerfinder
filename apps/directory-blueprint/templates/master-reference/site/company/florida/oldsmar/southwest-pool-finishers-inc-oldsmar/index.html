About Southwest Pool Finishers Inc

Southwest Pool Finishers Inc is a Florida licensed and insured corporation in Oldsmar that focuses on pool plastering and finishes. The site says its main scope is prepping pools for plaster and plastering them for contractors, and that it also installs new equipment, repairs old equipment and adds automation. It describes commercial and residential remodeling and restoration across the Tampa Bay area and features Pebble Tec finishes among its products. It also calls itself family owned. Nothing there says it builds pools from scratch, so treat it as a finishing and remodeling specialist. Water features come up in the FAQ, but the answer stays with its plaster scope, so the material does not confirm spa work. Google Maps shows 4.9 from 17 reviews, with 16 at five stars and 1 at four.
