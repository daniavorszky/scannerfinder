About Artesian Pools of East Florida Inc

Artesian Pools of East Florida works out of offices in Orlando and Daytona Beach and serves Central Florida. The site says it has built custom inground gunite pools since 1976 and that both offices build new pools. Major renovations and resurfacing are handled only through the Daytona Beach office. It also shows water features, fire features and a gallery of recent pool and spa installations, with license numbers for both offices, including CPC1457037 for Orlando. A customer review on the site praises the company's warranties. The record does not mention financing or commercial work, so confirm those by phone. Confirm the service area too, since the offices are in Orlando and Daytona Beach rather than Orange City. Google Maps shows a 3.0 rating from 4 reviews, 2 of them five stars and 2 of them one star.
