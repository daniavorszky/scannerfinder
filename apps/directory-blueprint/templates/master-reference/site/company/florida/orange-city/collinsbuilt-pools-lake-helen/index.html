About CollinsBuilt Pools

CollinsBuilt Pools, Co. is a family-owned custom pool and spa builder at a Lake Helen address, serving Central Florida. The site says the owners are present throughout the pool building process and that its mission is to build custom swimming pools. Pages cover custom designs, pool automation, a building process and a project gallery. A financing page says the company can put customers in touch with its finance team, and it describes a lender offering loans from $5,000 to $100,000. The site gives no license number, warranty, remodeling, decking or commercial details, and it does not say what pool materials the company works with. Google Maps shows 72 reviews and a 4.9 rating, with 69 at five stars, one at four stars and two at one star. Confirm licensing and materials by phone.
