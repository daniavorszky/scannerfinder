About Crystal Pools And Spas Of North Florida Inc

Crystal Pools And Spas Of North Florida Inc serves the Orange Park area and describes itself as a locally owned company that offers installation and service for all types of pools. The site cites more than 20 years in one place and 25 years in another. It says it builds custom inground concrete and inground vinyl pools using CAD and 3-D design, and it has pages for new construction, renovation and liner replacement. It says it is a licensed commercial and residential contractor and a Better Business Bureau member, and references are available on request. Despite the name, the site does not describe spa work. It does not mention financing, warranties or a license number. Google Maps shows a 3.8 rating from 70 reviews, 45 of them five stars and 19 of them one star.
