About Splash Pools and Spas

Splash Pools and Spas is a retail chain, and this listing is its Orange Park showroom. The site lists stores in Orange Park, Jacksonville and several Louisiana cities. The shop menu covers inground and above ground pools, pool liners, cleaners, ladders, filters and pumps, along with patio furniture, umbrellas, fire pits and hot tubs, and it includes a hot tub builder tool. It has a financing page and says customers can apply online for hot tub financing. The site does not describe pool construction crews or installation, and it does not mention licenses or warranties, so treat it as a retail option. Google Maps shows a 4.1 rating from 93 reviews, 62 of them five stars and 15 of them one star.
