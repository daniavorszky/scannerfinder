About Stanley Pools

Stanley Pools, Inc. is a family pool company serving Northeast Florida, with the Orange Park listing and a Jacksonville history on its site. The site says full swimming pool construction began in the early 1990s after the father joined his sons. It offers inground pool construction, renovation and remodeling with brick paver decks and coping, tile and resurfacing, screened pool enclosures, service and repair, maintenance and a retail store. It says Greg Stanley is a state licensed residential and commercial pool contractor and Hank Stanley a state licensed screen enclosure contractor. The site does not mention financing, warranties or vinyl and fiberglass pools. Google Maps shows a 4.8 rating from 22 reviews, 21 of them five stars and 1 of them one star.
