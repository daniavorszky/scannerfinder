About W T Construction, Inc.

W T Construction, Inc. is a Middleburg builder of custom homes and pools serving Clay County and Northeast Florida. Its site says it specializes in new home construction and inground pool construction, with pool options including tanning shelves, spillover spas, custom paver decking, water features and upgraded lighting. It says the company is licensed as a general contractor under CGC1532802 rather than naming a pool contractor license. A financing section says it partners with Lyon Financial. It also offers commercial construction such as new builds and tenant build-outs, though the site does not say that includes pools. Google Maps shows 14 reviews and a 4.7 rating, with 13 at five stars and one at one star. The site does not mention a warranty or say which pool materials it builds with.
