About American Pools & Spas (A Cody Pools Company)

American Pools & Spas, a Cody Pools company, is an Orlando pool builder that says it specializes in construction, renovation and repair. The site lists inground pools, spas, water features, fire features, screen enclosures, pergolas, decks, outdoor kitchens and pool and patio deck pavers, along with residential and commercial pool construction and remodeling. Its menu includes a financing page, and one customer testimonial praises its warranty team, though it also criticizes screen enclosure work by a subcontractor. The site says it was named the number one pool builder for 14 years, which is its own claim. No license number appears in the material provided. On Google Maps it has a 4.3 rating from 457 reviews, 356 of them five stars and 61 of them one star.
