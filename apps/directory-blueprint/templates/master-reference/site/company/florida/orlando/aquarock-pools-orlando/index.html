About Aquarock Pools

Aquarock Pools is an Orlando specialist in rock features for swimming pools. Its site says it hand-carves pool waterfalls, grottos and rock slides, and it serves Orlando, Tampa and other parts of Florida. It offers a free design consult and has a plans and pricing page plus a learning center. The pages describe adding these features to a pool, not building the pool itself, so confirm by phone whether it takes on full pool construction. No license number, financing or warranty is mentioned. Google lists three reviews, all five stars, for a 5.0 rating, and no hours are posted.
