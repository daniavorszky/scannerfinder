About Artistic Pool Design Inc

Artistic Pool Design Inc is an Orlando pool company whose site presents projects named On the lake, Big splash, Tranquility and Renewal. It says the owner, Cary, has more than 25 years of experience in the design, construction and service of pools, and that the company builds custom spas to fit the swimmer. The team includes craftsmen and subcontractors, and the site offers a referral list of licensed and insured professionals on request. That refers to the referral group, not to a license held by the company, and no license number appears. Nothing is said about financing, warranties or remodeling. Google shows a 5.0 rating from four reviews, and no hours are posted. The site says it uses only quality finishes and equipment.
