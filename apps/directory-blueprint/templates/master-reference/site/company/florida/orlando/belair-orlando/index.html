About Belair

Belair is an Orlando HVAC and general contracting company that Google Maps also lists as a swimming pool contractor. Its site leads with air conditioning repair, installation and 24/7 service, and adds a general contractor division and a pool division, with service areas including Orlando, Kissimmee and St. Cloud. The pool section says it offers fiberglass and concrete pool construction, repairs and resurfacing, from new builds to emergency fixes, and it states CPC licensing. The warranties and financing described on the site relate to HVAC systems, so the record does not show them for pool work. Spas, decking, screen enclosures and commercial pool work go unmentioned. Google Maps lists a 5.0 rating from 22 reviews, all five stars, and the business identifies as Latino-owned.
