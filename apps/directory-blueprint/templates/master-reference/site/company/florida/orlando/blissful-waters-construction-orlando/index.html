About Blissful Waters Construction

Blissful Waters Construction is a family and veteran owned pool contractor in Orlando, and its site says it has more than 10 years of experience. Services listed include custom pool construction, concrete and fiberglass pool installation, hot tub installation, saltwater and indoor pools, and remodeling that updates surfaces. The installation process described covers excavation, plumbing, decking and finishing. It also takes commercial jobs for apartment complexes and community centers. The site says the company is fully licensed and insured, with warranties available, though no license number appears. Financing is not confirmed, and some headings on the site read like unfinished template text. Google Maps lists a 4.6 rating from 11 reviews, 10 of them five stars and one at one star.
