About Custom Orlando Pools

Custom Orlando Pools builds pools in Orlando, Winter Park, Windermere and Lake Nona, and the site says the team has 23 years of experience. Services listed include residential and commercial pool construction, in-ground concrete and gunite pools, gunite or vinyl shell options, custom spas, remodeling, decking, coping and tile, water and fire features, lighting and automation, and outdoor kitchens. It describes itself as a licensed and insured general contractor, and it says it works with financing partners for qualified homeowners. The site claims to be the number one builder in Orlando, which is its own claim. Pool enclosures are mentioned as an add-on without saying what type. Fiberglass and warranties are not mentioned. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 26 reviews, all five stars.
