About D&J The Pool Resurfacers

D&J The Pool Resurfacers works in Orlando, nearby areas and Florida's east coast, and its site says it has served Central Florida for more than a decade. It describes itself as specializing in pool construction, resurfacing and custom remodeling, and it says it builds custom swimming pools from design to final construction. The menu lists complete pool renovation, new pool construction, commercial pools, and outdoor living and decking, and the site is also offered in Spanish. It says it works with quartz and PebbleTec, a finish it describes as carrying a 25-year manufacturer warranty. The footer shows license CPC1459797. Financing and screen enclosures are not mentioned. Google Maps lists a 4.7 rating from 43 reviews, 38 of them five stars and 2 of them one star, and the business identifies as Latino-owned.
