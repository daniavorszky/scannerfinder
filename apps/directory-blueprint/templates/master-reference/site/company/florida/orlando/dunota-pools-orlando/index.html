About Dunota Pools

Dunota Pools offers swimming pool construction and service in Orlando and central Florida, and its site also lists pages for Tampa, Jacksonville, Lakeland, St. Augustine, Clearwater and St. Petersburg. The site says it builds new pools and spas with no subcontractors, and its services run from new construction and renovation to maintenance and pool supplies. It builds gunite and shotcrete pools and argues on its blog that shotcrete beats fiberglass, so fiberglass installation is not confirmed. A summer offer at $49,999 lists a 25 by 12 pool with a salt system, decorative coping, tile and 500 square feet of pavers. The site calls its team Certified Pool Contractors and has a Financing page. Google Maps lists 227 reviews at 4.3, with 185 five-star and 33 one-star, and the business identifies as Latino-owned. Screen enclosures, warranties and commercial work go unmentioned.
