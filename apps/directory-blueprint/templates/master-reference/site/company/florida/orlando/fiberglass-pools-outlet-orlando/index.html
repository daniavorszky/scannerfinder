About FIBERGLASS POOLS OUTLET

Fiberglass Pools Outlet in Orlando sells and installs fiberglass pools, according to its website, which says the pools come from manufacturers and carry a 15-year warranty. The site says its in-house team handles pool installation along with screen enclosures and fences, wood and composite decks, travertine paving, summer kitchens and fire pits, and it offers 3D design of a pool before installation. The page title calls the company the number one choice for fiberglass pools, which is its own claim. Financing, licensing details, spas and commercial work are not mentioned, and it does not say whether it works on gunite or vinyl liner pools. Google Maps lists a 4.9 rating from 37 reviews, 36 of them five stars and one at one star, with hours posted.
