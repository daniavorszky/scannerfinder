About InstaPools Florida LLC

InstaPools Florida LLC sells and builds fiberglass pools out of Orlando, according to its website. The site presents a lineup of named models, including Serafina, Neblina Grande, Coral Cove and Regal Plunge, sorted into small, medium and large pools, and it says the shells are made in the USA. It also shows a pool spa combination and tanning ledge designs. A financing page describes a soft credit pull and bank partners, and the company says banks finance nearly 60% of its pools. The site does not give a contractor license number, and it does not mention remodeling work or a warranty. Google lists six reviews, all five stars, for a 5.0 rating. The site says buyers pay as progress is made, and it groups its models as smart choice, most popular and ultimate luxury.
