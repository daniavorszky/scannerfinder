About Larry Bowles Pools

Larry Bowles Pools, which appears on its site as Larry Bowles Inc., builds swimming pools in Orlando, and the site walks through construction stages from excavation and steel forming to pouring, decking and coping. It also covers add-on features, landscaping, testimonials and a Financing page, and its FAQ says the company can help with financing and with maintaining the pool afterward. A financing service called Paramount Capital is described for customers of approved Paramount builders. The site even has pages for grilling recipes and pool party ideas. Spa building, warranties, licensing, remodeling and commercial work are not mentioned. Google Maps shows a 4.3 rating from 12 reviews, 10 of them five stars and 2 of them one star, with no posted hours, so call to confirm details.
