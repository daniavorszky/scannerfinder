About Leron Homes & Pools

Leron Homes & Pools is an Orlando company that builds inground concrete pools and new homes, according to its site, which cites more than 25 years of experience. It says it takes both residential and commercial pool construction, with commercial work for hotels, resorts, theme parks and community developments. The menu includes prices and contact, finishes, and a Financing page, and the site also has reviews and Instagram sections and a booking phone line. The record does not mention spas, screen enclosures, warranties, remodeling or a license number, and the site content is short. Google Maps lists a 5.0 rating from 25 reviews, all five stars, with hours posted. Call to confirm details beyond new construction, since the site text is thin on services.
