About Pacific Pools of Orlando

Pacific Pools of Orlando builds vinyl liner swimming pools and replaces liners, according to its site, which says the business has been established since 1986. It lists new pool construction, inground pools, vinyl liner remodeling and replacement, pool deck remodeling, new spas and hot tubs, and residential and commercial projects. The owner, Andy Coplin, is described as overseeing each project, and the site calls the company a Pacific Showcase Builder and Hayward equipment dealer. It displays state contractor license CPC035670 and says its builders offer warranties, though it does not detail them. Gunite and fiberglass appear only in comparisons with vinyl, so it is unclear whether it installs them. Financing is not mentioned. Google Maps shows a 4.7 rating from 23 reviews, 20 of them five stars.
