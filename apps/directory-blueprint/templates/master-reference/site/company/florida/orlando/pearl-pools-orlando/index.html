About Pearl Pools

Pearl Pools presents itself as a renovation specialist for homeowners, builders and property managers, with an Orlando office and others in Fort Myers and several other states. The site says it has renovated more than 200,000 pools since 1984 across residential and commercial projects, and its services cover resurfacing and remodeling, structural repairs and leak detection, tile, coping and deck renovation, sun shelves, spas and additions, water features, and equipment upgrades. License CPC1461299 is listed for Orlando and Fort Myers. Its search summary calls it a custom pool builder, but the page content centers on renovation, so new construction is unclear. It links to two financing pages, including Lyon Financial. Google Maps shows a 4.9 rating from 33 reviews, 30 of them five stars, with hours posted.
