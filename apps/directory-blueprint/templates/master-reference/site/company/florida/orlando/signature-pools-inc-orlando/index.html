About Signature Pools, Inc.

Signature Pools, Inc. is an Orlando design-build pool company that says it was founded in 1987 by Michael and Jennifer Malagian. The site describes 3D renderings and inground pool construction, with portfolio pages showing freeform and linear designs, fire and water features, swim-up bars and an overflow spa. It says it belongs to the Society of Watershape Designers, the International Watershape Institute, the Better Business Bureau and the Florida Spa and Pool Association, and it calls itself voted best pool builder in Orlando, which is its own claim. The footer shows license CPC039932. Financing, warranties, remodeling and commercial work are not mentioned. Google Maps shows a 3.4 rating from 18 reviews, 10 of them five stars and 7 of them one star.
