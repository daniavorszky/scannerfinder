About Simple To Sensational Pools, Inc.

Simple To Sensational Pools, Inc. is a Central Florida pool builder in Orlando that offers custom pool and spa construction, according to its site. Listed specialties are new pools and spas, water features, renovations, tiling, coping and decking, and interior finishes, and the site says renovations of older pools are part of the work. It describes owner Tom as preferring to build one-on-one with each customer, and it calls itself a certified pool builder, though no license number appears. Financing, warranties and commercial work are not mentioned. The menu links to a process page, a gallery and testimonials. Google Maps shows a 4.6 rating from 9 reviews, 8 of them five stars and one at one star, with no posted hours.
