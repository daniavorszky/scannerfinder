About Theisen Brothers Inc

Theisen Brothers Inc is a family owned Orlando contractor that builds pools and entry features for commercial and residential properties across Florida. The site describes design, engineering, construction and renovation of pools, along with cabanas, fencing, landscaping, irrigation, spas and fountains, plus equipment maintenance and required inspections. It says it uses its own employees on jobs, not subcontractors, and shows a state certified pool contractor number, CPC 057278, and a general contractor number as well. The pages include galleries and testimonials, but nothing on financing or a warranty. Google gives it 3.7 stars from six reviews, four at five stars and two at one star. The footer carries a 2014 copyright and an Orlando address in the 32839 zip code, so the pages may be dated.
