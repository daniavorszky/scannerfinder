About Unique Pools Spas & Billiards

Unique Pools Spas & Billiards is a showroom in Orlando with locations also listed for Jacksonville and Daytona Beach. According to its site, it sells hot tubs, swim spas, above-ground pools and semi-inground pools, and it describes itself as the largest and most award-winning dealer of those products in Florida, though the record names no award. The menu lists Cal Spas and other brands, heat pumps, cold plunge units, saunas, patio furniture and grills, along with a Financing page, and Maps adds billiards and pool table sales. The site frames the business as a retailer, so it reads as a place to buy spas and above-ground pools, not a builder of custom pools. Google Maps shows a 4.7 rating from 266 reviews, with 237 five-star and 17 one-star ratings. Screen enclosures, warranties, licensing and commercial work are not mentioned.
