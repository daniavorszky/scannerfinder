About Waterline Pools and Spas

Waterline Pools and Spas operates from Hansel Avenue in Orlando, and its site title describes custom pool and spa design. The site shows a timelapse of building a new pool and another of remodeling one, and a customer testimonial praises the pool design and help choosing travertine and tile. The company also promotes summer kitchens, pergolas and fire pits. The footer lists state license CPC044073, and a warranty section says the company warranties what it sells. The menu has a Financing page and a Pool Safety page, and the site also shows a contact form and a gallery. Commercial work and material types are not mentioned, and the footer carries a 2020 copyright date. Google Maps lists a 4.6 rating from 32 reviews, with 28 five-star and 3 one-star ratings.
