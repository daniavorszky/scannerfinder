About Above Code Pools Spas and Lagoons

Above Code Pools Spas and Lagoons is an Ormond Beach company that says it designs and builds custom pools, spas and lagoons across Central Florida, and it also mentions outdoor extras such as fireplaces and putting greens. Customer reviews published on the site describe an in-ground pool built in six weeks, a spa added to a new pool, an older pool remodeled, and new decking installed with a replacement patio. The company says it offers financing through Lyon Financial, is fully licensed and insured, and provides warranty options and ongoing support. It says it focuses on design and construction rather than maintenance. Commercial work and specific pool materials are not mentioned, and the build details above come from reviews the business posted, so ask what it does in each category.
