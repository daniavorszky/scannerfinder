About Affordable Pools

Affordable Pools builds in-ground gunite pools for homeowners in Volusia, Flagler, St. Johns and Seminole counties from an Ormond Beach base. The site says the team has more than three decades of pool construction experience and handles design, permits, decking, water features and equipment. It advertises a saltwater pool, spa and screen enclosure package priced at $85,000, and lists spa installation on its own or with a pool. Financing runs through Lyon Financial. The site describes a three-year warranty on pool equipment, lifetime coverage on concrete, ten years on plaster and one year on workmanship, and says its licensed and insured in-house team does the work. Remodeling and commercial work are not mentioned. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 49 reviews, 48 of them five stars and 1 of them four stars.
