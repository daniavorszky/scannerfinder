About C M Custom Pool Designs

C M Custom Pool Designs is a family owned pool company in Ormond Beach serving Volusia and Flagler counties, within roughly a 50 minute radius of its shop. The site says owner Coby Moore has worked in the pool industry since 1994 and describes custom inground pool construction, renovations and repairs, pool decks with pavers and other finishes, new screen enclosures, and hot tubs. It also offers monthly pool maintenance, outdoor kitchens and patio furniture, and lists a financing page. The business says it offers a warranty on what it builds and takes both residential and commercial work. No license number appears in the material found. Google Maps shows a 4.6 rating from 193 reviews, 166 of them five stars and 12 of them one star.
