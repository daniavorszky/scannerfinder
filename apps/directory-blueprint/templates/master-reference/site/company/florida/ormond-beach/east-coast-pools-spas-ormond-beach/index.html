About East Coast Pools & Spas

East Coast Pools & Spas builds pools from Ormond Beach for East Central Florida and says it is now serving St. Augustine too. Owner Tom Gilbert is described as having more than 24 years in the pool industry, and the site says the company has been a News Journal Readers' Choice pick each year since 2014. Menu pages cover new pool construction in gunite and fiberglass, remodels, service calls, monthly service, spas and financing. The site also mentions water and fire features, summer kitchens, screen enclosures and pool decks, and says commercial and residential work is offered. License numbers CPC1457246, CPC1461577 and CBC1259865 appear in the footer. No warranty is described. Google Maps shows a 4.5 rating from 68 reviews, 59 of them five stars and 7 of them one star.
