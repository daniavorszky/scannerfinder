About Serenity Waterscapes Pools | Spas

Serenity Waterscapes Pools | Spas is an Ormond Beach pool builder that offers custom pool and spa design on a 3D model so buyers can see a pool before it is built. The site says the company works one-on-one with designers to fit a buyer's budget and backyard. It lays out its build process from gunite shell and plumbing through decking, with required inspections and termite pretreatment before decks go in, and screen enclosures or fencing installed to engineered plans. It lists licenses CPC1461264 and CPC1461679. The site says every new pool is covered by a warranty program and has a page for warranty requests. Financing, remodeling and commercial work are not mentioned. Google Maps shows a 4.1 rating from 10 reviews, 7 of them five stars and 2 of them one star.
