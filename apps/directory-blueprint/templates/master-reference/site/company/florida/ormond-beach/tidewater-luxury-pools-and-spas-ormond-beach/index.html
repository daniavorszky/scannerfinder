About Tidewater Luxury Pools and Spas

Tidewater Luxury Pools and Spas is a veteran-owned Ormond Beach company that describes itself as an authorized Latham fiberglass pool dealer serving Volusia and Flagler counties. The site says the owner limits the number of pools built each year and stays involved personally. Services include custom fiberglass pools, spillover spas, and renovations covering resurfacing, waterline tile, coping, decks and equipment. It quotes fully installed fiberglass pools at $85,000 to $120,000, including a screen enclosure and travertine decking, and says most customers finance part or all of a project. It is listed as licensed and insured, and it mentions Latham's limited lifetime structural warranty. Concrete pools and commercial work are not described as services in the material found. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 2 reviews, both five stars.
