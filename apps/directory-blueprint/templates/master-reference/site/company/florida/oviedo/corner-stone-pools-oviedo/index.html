About Corner Stone Pools

Corner Stone Pools, Inc. is a family owned company in Oviedo that says it has worked since 1997, serving Oviedo, Winter Springs and Orlando. Its site says it specializes in weekly pool maintenance, renovations and new pool construction for residential and commercial clients, and it lists renovation upgrades such as waterfalls, underwater lighting, screened enclosures and custom decks. It also mentions built-in spas, and filter and pump replacements. The site states it is fully licensed and insured, with license CPC-1456748. Its menu separates renovations, new pools, maintenance services and equipment, and it describes an initial consultation for planning a project. It does not mention financing, warranties, or the pool types it builds. Google Maps shows a 4.3 rating from 12 reviews, 10 of them five stars and 2 of them one star.
