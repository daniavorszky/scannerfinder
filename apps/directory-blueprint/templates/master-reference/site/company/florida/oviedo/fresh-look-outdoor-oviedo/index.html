About Fresh Look Outdoor

Fresh Look Outdoor is a family owned paver and hardscape company in the Orlando area, listed on Google Maps as a swimming pool contractor along with carport and pergola builder and paving contractor. The site says it is ICPI certified and a Belgard authorized contractor, installs Belgard pavers, natural stone pool decks, driveways, pergolas, fire pits, fireplaces and outdoor kitchens, and does DriBond installation. It also says it installs a wide variety of pool renovations. The site's own pages center on decks and hardscape, and it does not say it builds new pools, so ask before assuming it takes on a full pool build. It does not mention financing, warranties or a license number. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 36 reviews, all five stars.
