About E Z Cool's Pool & Yardscape

E Z Cool's Pool & Yardscape is a Pace company that describes itself as Northwest Florida's affordable pool builder. The site says it installs above ground, semi-inground and fully inground pools, and its services list includes liner replacement, equipment replacement, pool repair, safety fencing and water features. It also does outdoor work beyond pools: paver installation, patios and decks, retaining walls, pergolas, gazebos, carports, and landscape design and build. The site pitches a design, plan and build process that starts with a plot plan and offers free quotes. It does not name gunite or fiberglass, and no license number, financing or warranty appears in the text read. Google Maps shows 16 reviews at 4.3, with 13 five-star and 3 one-star ratings, and it lists no posted hours, so call to confirm them.
