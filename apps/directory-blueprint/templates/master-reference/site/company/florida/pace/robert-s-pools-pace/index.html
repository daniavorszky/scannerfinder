About Robert's Pools

Robert's Pools is a family pool builder and retail store in Pace, in Northwest Florida. The site says the family has built pools in the area since the 1970s, that since 1979 it has built vinyl and gunite in-ground pools, and that it opened a retail store in 1993. Services listed include gunite pool installation, vinyl liner installation, hot tubs and spas, and pool chemicals. The site gives a license number, RP0041208, and cites a 2019 readers choice award from a local weekly, InWeekly. It does not spell out remodeling, screen enclosures, a warranty or commercial work in the text read. Google Maps shows 155 reviews with a 4.9 rating, 149 of them five stars and 1 at one star. Store hours run weekdays and Saturday mornings.
