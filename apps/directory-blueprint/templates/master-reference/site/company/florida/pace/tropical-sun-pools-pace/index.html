About Tropical Sun Pools

Tropical Sun Pools builds inground fiberglass pools for the Florida Panhandle and says it serves Pensacola, Pace, Milton and Crestview. The site describes itself as locally and veteran owned and sorts its pools into compact, medium and large fiberglass models, with rectangle, Roman, kidney and pool and spa combo designs. Other services on the site include vinyl liner replacement, resurfacing a fiberglass pool, and converting a chlorine pool to saltwater. It says it offers 100 percent financing through three lending partners and displays license CPC 1460806. The site promises satisfaction but does not state a warranty, and the pages read say nothing about gunite, screen enclosures, decking or commercial work. Google Maps shows 16 reviews, all five stars, and the profile notes veteran-owned, Latino-owned and disabled-owned.
