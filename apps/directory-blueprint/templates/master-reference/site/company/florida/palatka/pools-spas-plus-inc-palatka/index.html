About Pools & Spas Plus Inc

Pools & Spas Plus is a Palatka pool and hot tub store that also does service and repair. The site says it has served Palatka for over 30 years, and it reaches Palm Coast and St. Augustine as well. It sells Doughboy above ground pool packages, Aspen Spas and other hot tubs, pool chemicals and supplies, and grills, and its menu includes service and equipment repair pages and a Get Financing link. The Maps profile says the business identifies as women-owned. Nothing read says it builds inground pools, gives a license number or takes commercial work, so treat it mainly as a retail and service option. Google Maps shows 65 reviews and a 4.4 rating, with 47 five-star, 7 four-star, 4 three-star, 3 two-star and 4 one-star reviews.
