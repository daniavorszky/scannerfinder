About Selenity Pools

Selenity Pools builds pools in Keystone Heights and says it is a full-service residential builder working in gunite and fiberglass, with spa design offered on new construction. The menu lists custom concrete pool installation and San Juan fiberglass pool installation, and a heading says it is now an exclusive San Juan Fiberglass Pool Dealer. It also does replastering and renovation of existing pools. A financing page names two lenders, and the site shows contractor license number CPC1460922. One customer review on the site calls the crew polite and professional. Google Maps shows 15 reviews, all five stars. The site does not mention a warranty, decking, screen enclosures or commercial work, and it describes its builder work as residential. Confirm those points and the service area by phone.
