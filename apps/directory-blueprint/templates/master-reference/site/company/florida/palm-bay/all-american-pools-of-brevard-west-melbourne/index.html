About All American Pools of Brevard

All American Pools of Brevard is a West Melbourne pool builder serving Melbourne and Brevard County. The site says it designs and builds pools using many product options, calls its designers creative, and describes its pools as award winning without naming an award. It also calls itself a hot tub and spa builder, with a Spa page for custom spas. It offers a free consultation with one of its designers by phone or through a form. Warranties and financial security appear only in a checklist of factors for choosing a pool company, so neither counts as an offer. The site says nothing about licensing, financing, remodeling or commercial work. Google Maps shows 4.6 from 11 reviews, ten at five stars and one at one star.
