About Custom Pool Renovations, Inc

Custom Pool Renovations, Inc., known as CPR, is a Palm Bay company that calls itself a Brevard County pool remodeling business with a combined 40 years in the local pool trade. Its menu covers new pools, renovations, pool decks, waterfalls, tile, finishes, resurfacing, equipment, screen enclosures, LED lighting, spa additions, commercial pools and financing. The site says it is licensed and insured, that all work is guaranteed, and that it stands behind its work with a one-year warranty in addition to the manufacturers' warranty. It also provides pool equipment repair, including pumps, filters, automation and salt systems, and offers LED lighting and rock waterfalls. No license number is shown in the material.
