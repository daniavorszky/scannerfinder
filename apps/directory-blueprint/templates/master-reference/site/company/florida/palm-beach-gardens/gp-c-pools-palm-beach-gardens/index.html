About GP&C Pools

GP&C Pools is a family based pool contractor in Palm Beach Gardens that says it specializes in custom pool design and construction, with decades of combined experience. The site lists construction, pavers and coping, resurfacing and tiling, and outdoor features, and says the team designs and installs durable pavers and resurfaces aging pools. A quote form also lists pool enclosures as a choice, but no page text describes that work. The site gives no license number, financing, warranty or commercial details. Maps shows a 5.0 rating from just 2 reviews, both five stars, so there is little review history to go on. Call to confirm the scope of work and service area. The site was built on Wix and offers a consultation request form.
