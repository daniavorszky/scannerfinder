About Palm Coast Pools llc

Palm Coast Pools LLC is a Palm Coast builder of custom inground gunite pools across Flagler, St. Johns and Volusia counties, and it publishes its pricing. The site says a new inground gunite pool starts at $54,999 for a 12 by 24 pool with standard decking, including permitting, engineering and a written warranty. It says it builds spas, sun shelves and water features, and that a spa built with the pool costs far less than one added later. It states it builds gunite pools and nothing else, with no remodeling arm, and that screen enclosure footers are poured in its builds, with the enclosure installed by a specialist screen contractor. Financing runs through a lender it refers customers to. The site lists Florida license RP252555609. Google Maps shows a 4.2 rating from 5 reviews, 4 of them five stars and 1 of them one star.
