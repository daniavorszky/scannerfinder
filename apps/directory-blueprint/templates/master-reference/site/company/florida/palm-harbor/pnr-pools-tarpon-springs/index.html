About PNR Pools

PNR Pools is a family-owned and operated pool company in Tarpon Springs that says it has more than 25 years of experience. The site lists new pool construction, pool and spa resurfacing, remodels, new equipment, water and fire features and outdoor kitchens. Remodel work is described as new tile, coping and decking, and the equipment list names pumps, heaters, filters and salt systems. The company says it is licensed and insured under CPC1460521 and offers a free consultation. The site pitches a homeowner with a worn pool as easily as one starting from scratch, and it groups its work into construction and major upgrades, surface and finish work, and equipment installation. It does not say which pool materials it builds, and it does not mention financing, warranties, screen enclosures or commercial work. Google Maps shows a perfect 5.0 rating from 29 reviews, all five stars.
