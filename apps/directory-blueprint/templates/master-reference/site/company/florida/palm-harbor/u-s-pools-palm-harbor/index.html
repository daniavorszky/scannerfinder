About U.S. POOLS

U.S. Pools is a locally owned pool builder and remodeler serving Palm Harbor and Dunedin. Its site says it builds in-ground pools and handles remodeling and modernization, uses 3D design software, and assigns a project manager to each job. Past work shown includes indoor, enclosed and infinity pools, and a published review praises a patio with marble decking. A Financing page points to HFS for loans, and a Florida contractor license, CPC1457799, is shown. The site claims more than 1,500 five-star reviews, while Maps shows a 4.9 rating from 42 reviews, 41 of them five stars and 1 of them one star. It does not mention a warranty, spa work or commercial work. Because the text mentions Dunedin repeatedly, confirm that the Palm Harbor area is covered.
