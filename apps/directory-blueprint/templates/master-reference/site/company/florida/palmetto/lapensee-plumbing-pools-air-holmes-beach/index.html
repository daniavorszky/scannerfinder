About LaPensee Plumbing • Pools • Air

LaPensee Plumbing, Pools & Air is a Holmes Beach company that says it is family-owned and has served residential and commercial customers since 1985. Plumbing, air conditioning, water heaters and pools are its main service sections, and the site also has a showroom page. On the pool side, the text covers pumps, heaters, salt systems, routine maintenance, and the ability to upgrade or rehab an existing pool. New pool construction comes up only as a passing line, so whether it actually builds pools is unclear. The site says the company is licensed and insured and lists three license numbers, one of them a CPC number. Financing, warranties and specific pool materials are not mentioned. Google Maps shows 4.3 from 276 reviews, with 216 five-star ratings and 41 one-star ratings.
