About Trusted Pool & Spa

Trusted Pool & Spa is a Palmetto listing that says it has served residential and commercial clients in Sarasota and Manatee counties since 2010, and its site names Hillsborough and Pinellas counties as well. The family-owned company offers custom pool design and installation, including concrete and saltwater pools and spa installation. It also does remodeling, resurfacing, crack and tile repair, pool cage restoration, screen enclosures, decking, outdoor kitchens, fire features and leak detection, plus maintenance and equipment service. The site says it is fully licensed and insured and offers warranties on its work, though it gives no license number. A finance page is linked, and military and veteran discounts are mentioned. Google Maps shows a 4.8 rating from 337 reviews, with 320 five-star and 11 one-star ratings.
