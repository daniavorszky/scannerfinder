About Cox Building Corporation

Listed on Google Maps as Cox Building Corporation, this Panama City Beach entry points to a website branded Cox Pools, which says the family firm has built pools in Northwest Florida since 1958. The site covers custom residential pools with engineered gunite shells, commercial pools for resorts, HOAs and municipalities, remodeling and resurfacing, and outdoor living. It says its own crews handle plaster and paver decking, and it has a warranty department and a claims page. Lending partners are named for financing, and the license shown is CPC056661. The site also claims Certified Pool Builder status. The site also mentions the family's outdoor living studio. The Maps listing itself has a 4.9 rating from 11 reviews, 10 of them five stars and 1 of them four stars.
