About Palm Bay Pools LLC

Palm Bay Pools LLC builds and renovates pools from its base in Southport. The site says its founder, James, has over 30 years of experience in every type of concrete pool construction, and it describes designing custom pool and spa projects with clients after a consultation. Menu pages cover designs, renovations and construction, and a financing page points to Lyon Financial, which the site describes as a lender offering unsecured pool financing. The site lists Florida license CPC1458664. Warranties and commercial work are not mentioned. The site says it treats customers with honesty and integrity and puts them first, and the listing has posted hours. Google Maps shows 4.8 from 71 reviews, 65 of them five stars, 3 one star, 2 four stars and 1 two star.
