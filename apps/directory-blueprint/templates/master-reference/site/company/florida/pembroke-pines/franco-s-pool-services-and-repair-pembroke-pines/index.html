About Franco's Pool Services and repair

Franco's Pool Services and Repair is listed in Pembroke Pines and covers Miami-Dade and Broward counties. The site describes a locally family-owned company that is certified and insured and cites CPC and CPO certification. Its core work is cleaning, maintenance and repair of pools and spas, including tile cleaning, acid washing and plastering, for commercial and residential customers. The site also invites visitors to renovate a pool or add new equipment and says it offers a guarantee on all its work. A menu item and a text line refer to new pool construction, so the company appears to take new builds, though the record gives no detail on pool types or materials. Google Maps lists it as Latino-owned, and its rating is 5.0 from 29 reviews, all five stars.
