About Paramount Pool Services Company

Paramount Pool Services Company serves southwest Broward County from Pembroke Pines, with a focus on Southwest Ranches, Davie and Weston as well. The site says the company has worked in the pool industry since 1985 and is licensed and insured by the State of Florida as a certified contractor and service provider. Its menu covers weekly pool service and management, leak detection, inspections, remodeling with pool finishes, tile and coping, equipment installs, repairs, portable spas, BBQ islands and a financing page. It also sells pumps, filters, heaters, automation and robotic cleaners. Nothing in the record says it builds brand new pools, so treat it mainly as a service, remodeling and equipment option. A Warranty item appears in the menu, but the record does not say what it covers, and one page notes the company can process manufacturer warranty claims on equipment. Google Maps shows 4.9 from 29 reviews, 28 of them five stars.
