About Fagan's Custom Pools

Fagan's Custom Pools is a Pensacola and Navarre contractor that describes itself as a certified new pool contractor. The site says it installs custom in-ground pools, repairs pools and replaces liners, and mentions salt water systems. It says it has served the area for over 30 years, that pool repair service goes back to 1985, and that the owner personally oversees construction and installation. The footer gives state license RP0049993. The site does not mention financing, a warranty, spas, decks or commercial work, and it does not name the pool materials it builds. Google shows a 4.3 rating from 23 reviews: 19 at five stars and 4 at one star. Call to confirm which pool types it builds. Its galleries show start to finish builds and new liners.
