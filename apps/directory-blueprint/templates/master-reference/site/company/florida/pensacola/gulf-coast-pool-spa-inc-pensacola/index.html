About Gulf Coast Pool & Spa Inc.

Gulf Coast Pool & Spa Inc. is a Pensacola company that says it has provided pool services, new pools and liner replacements since 1987, though another heading on its site says 1997. The site describes new in-ground pool construction, with a pool possible in as little as 10 days, and its menu covers liner replacement and patterns, pool repairs, openings and closings. Owners named on the site include Gary Jensen and Thomas Jensen, and a menu page covers its work with veterans. The footer shows Florida license RP0054511 and an Alabama number. Financing, a warranty, decking and commercial work are not mentioned. Google shows a 4.3 rating from 49 reviews: 36 at five stars, 6 at one star and 7 in between.
