About Johnson Pools Inc

Johnson Pools Inc. is a family-owned pool company on Massachusetts Avenue in Pensacola that also names Foley, Alabama in its page title. The site says it installs new in-ground pools and provides renovations such as vinyl liner replacements, and its menu lists gunite resurfacing, pool repairs, pool removal, Latham liners, financing and a Trilogy Pools page. Gunite resurfacing is described as work on existing pools, so the record does not show it building gunite pools. The footer lists license numbers RP25254818 and CPC1458622. No warranty, spa, deck or commercial work is described. Google shows a 3.8 rating from 165 reviews: 103 at five stars, 40 at one star and 22 in between. The site describes its service area as the Florida Panhandle plus nearby Alabama towns, so confirm coverage for your address.
