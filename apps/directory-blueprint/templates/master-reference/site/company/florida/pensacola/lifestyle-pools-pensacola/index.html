About Lifestyle pools

Lifestyle Pools sells and installs fiberglass swimming pools, and its site says the company has over 30 years in the fiberglass pool business. Its pages describe in-ground fiberglass pools, plunge pools, lap pools, reflection pools and spas, and it promotes a patented composite technology it calls Composite Armour. The site says the pools carry a lifetime structural warranty and a lifetime structural osmosis warranty, and it has a financing page with lender partners. It also mentions more than 1,500 fiberglass pools installed in the USA and describes itself as an award winning builder. The site compares fiberglass with concrete pools but does not say the company builds concrete or vinyl liner pools, and it does not mention remodeling, screen enclosures, decking, commercial work or a license number. The site mentions Florida and Texas but does not name Pensacola or a service area, so confirm that it works in your area. On Google Maps it has a 5.0 rating from two reviews, both at five stars.
