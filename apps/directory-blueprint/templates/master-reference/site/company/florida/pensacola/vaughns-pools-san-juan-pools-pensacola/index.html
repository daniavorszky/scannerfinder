About Vaughns Pools - San Juan Pools

Vaughn's Pools, also listed as San Juan Pools, is a family owned Pensacola business that says it has been in the pool industry since 1979 and builds pools in Northwest Florida and Southeast Alabama. The site says it specializes in in-ground vinyl liner and fiberglass pools, and it also runs a retail store with accessories and chemicals. Menu pages cover pool repair and liner replacement, pool enclosures and financing, and the footer lists Florida license CPC 057331 plus an Alabama number. It calls itself a top rated dealer, which is its own claim. Gunite, warranty and commercial work are not mentioned. Google shows a 4.1 rating from 44 reviews: 33 at five stars, 8 at one star and 3 in between.
