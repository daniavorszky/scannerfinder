About Carlos Pavers, Pools and Spas

Carlos Pavers, Pools and Spas serves Pinellas County and the Tampa Bay area from Pinellas Park. The site says the company was established in 2001 and describes it as family owned with more than 30 years of experience. It offers new swimming pools and spas, remodels and renovations, pool decks, driveways, walkways, custom waterfalls, retaining walls and commercial paver projects. The site advertises 100 percent financing and a one-year warranty on all services, and says the company is fully licensed and insured, though no license number appears in the record. A commercial page is on the menu. The site also says showroom visits are welcome and claims an A+ Better Business Bureau rating. Google Maps shows a 4.8 rating from 102 reviews, 94 of them five stars and 4 of them one star.
