About Kingdom Pavers

Kingdom Pavers is listed in Pinellas Park, and its site says it serves Tampa, Pasco, Pinellas, Sarasota and nearby areas. The focus is paver and hardscape installation for commercial and residential customers, including driveways, walkways, pool decks, patios, natural stone, artificial turf, gutters, fences, pergolas and stucco. Pool related pages cover remodeling and refinishing, waterline tile, and screen enclosure repair or rescreening. The site does not say it builds new pools, so treat it as a hardscape and pool finish contractor rather than a pool builder. The site offers free quotes and a design consultation with 3D renderings. Financing, warranties and licensing are not mentioned. Google Maps shows a 4.4 rating from 97 reviews, 80 of them five stars and 14 of them one star, with no hours posted.
