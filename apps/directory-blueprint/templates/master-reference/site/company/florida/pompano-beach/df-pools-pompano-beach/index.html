About DF Pools

DF Pools is a family-owned Pompano Beach company working across Broward, Palm Beach and Miami-Dade counties. The site says it has offered pool construction, remodeling and renovation for over two decades, using its own crews. Remodeling is the deepest part: resurfacing, tile, coping, mosaics, acid washes, leak detection and waterfalls, plus pool deck pavers in travertine, marble, brick and stamped concrete, and driveway and patio pavers and outdoor kitchens. It says it resurfaces commercial and residential pools and spas, offers flexible financing with a Finance page, and that customers get a warranty with the work, though no terms are given. It calls its people fully insured but shows no license number. Google Maps shows a 4.2 rating from 42 reviews, with 33 five-star and 8 one-star ratings.
