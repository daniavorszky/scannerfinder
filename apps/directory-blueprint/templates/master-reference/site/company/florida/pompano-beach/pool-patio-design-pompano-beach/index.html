About Pool & Patio Design

Pool & Patio Design builds custom pools for homeowners in Pompano Beach, Fort Lauderdale, Boca Raton, Coral Springs, Deerfield Beach and Parkland. The site says it specializes exclusively in inground, concrete pool construction and states that it does not offer vinyl-liner pools. Services run from custom design with 3D renderings to remodeling, resurfacing and refinishing, custom spas and hot tubs, deck installation, water and fire features, outdoor kitchens, pergolas and artificial turf. A menu page describes a licensed certified pool contractor, but no license number appears in the record. Financing and warranties are not mentioned, and neither is commercial work. The site's reviews praise pavers, coping and a salt system conversion. On Google Maps it has a 4.9 rating from 123 reviews, with 115 five-star and 2 one-star ratings.
