About Seacrest Pools, Inc.

Seacrest Pools, Inc. builds custom luxury pools across South Florida from Pompano Beach, with Fort Lauderdale and Lighthouse Point on its site. It cites over 40 years of combined experience and walks through its build in stages, including inspections of the steel and bonding before a shotcrete application. The company also says it builds swim and spa systems and renovates existing pools with sun shelves, lighting, water features and spa additions, along with tile, coping and deck repair. For new construction it describes a three-year guarantee and a lifetime structural warranty on the pool shell, and it says manufacturers add their own coverage. No license number or financing page appears in the record. Google Maps shows a 4.9 rating from 40 reviews, 39 at five stars.
