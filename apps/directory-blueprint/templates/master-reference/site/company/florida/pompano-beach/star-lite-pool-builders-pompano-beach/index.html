About Star-Lite Pool Builders

Star-Lite Pool Builders is a Pompano Beach pool company that says it has served South Florida since 1976. The site shows a contractor license, CPC#1458513, and lays out a process that runs from consultation and design through construction and project management, with most pools taking 8 to 10 weeks to build. It says the owner also acts as construction manager. The build steps name shotcrete for the pool shell and include deck installation and equipment installation near the end. The company describes itself as a warranty center for Hayward and Jandy equipment, which is a repair-center status and not a warranty on its own work. Nothing in the record covers spas, remodeling, screen enclosures, financing or commercial work. On Google Maps it has a 3.9 rating from 7 reviews, five of them five stars and two of them one star.
