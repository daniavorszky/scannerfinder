About THCS Pavers Driveways & Pool Installation

THCS Pavers Driveways & Pool Installation is a Pompano Beach contractor that mixes hardscape and pool work. Its menu has pages for new swimming pool construction, pool installation, driveways, patios and pool decks, paver installation and general contracting, plus area pages for Fort Lauderdale, Boca Raton, Lighthouse Point, Oakland Park and nearby cities. The site describes a build process from excavation and steel reinforcement through plumbing, electrical and shotcrete, and says it favors pavers that stay cool underfoot and resist slipping when wet. High-end driveways in concrete pavers, travertine and marble are also offered. It does not mention financing, a license number or a warranty. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 65 reviews, with 64 five-star and one two-star rating.
