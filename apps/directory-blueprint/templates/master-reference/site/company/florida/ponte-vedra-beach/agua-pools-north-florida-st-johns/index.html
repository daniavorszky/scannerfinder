About Agua Pools North Florida

Agua Pools North Florida says it designs and builds custom inground pools, spas, screen enclosures and outdoor living spaces across North and Central Florida with one in-house team. Its site lists new pool construction, remodeling and resurfacing, spas and water features, automation, pavers, pergolas, outdoor kitchens, fire features, 3D design previews and showrooms. It advertises financing with a loan calculator, describes itself as licensed and insured, and discusses warranty protection on its workmanship and installed products. It claims more than 1,000 pools built and over 30 years of experience. Commercial work is not mentioned, and the record names no license number. The site also advertises referral programs for friends, family and realtors. Google Maps shows a 4.3 rating from 100 reviews, with 79 five-star ratings and 15 one-star ratings.
