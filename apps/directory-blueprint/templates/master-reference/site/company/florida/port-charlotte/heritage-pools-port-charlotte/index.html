About Heritage Pools

Heritage Pools is a Port Charlotte pool service and renovation company. Its site says the business has been operating since 1978 and is fully licensed and certified, though it gives no license number. Services cover regular maintenance programs, repairs, and renovations such as replacing old tile and re-plastering the pool interior, along with tile and brick coping, resurfacing and remodeling work. The company also installs and renovates pool decks, including textured and custom decking, and it lists rescreening and pool cages, with screen repairs and enclosures named in the renovation copy. The site does not describe building new pools, financing or a warranty, so treat it as a service, repair and renovation option and confirm anything else by phone. On Google Maps it holds a 3.3 rating from 7 reviews, four at five stars and three at one star.
