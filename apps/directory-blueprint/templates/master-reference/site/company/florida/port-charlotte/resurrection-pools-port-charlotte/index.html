About Resurrection Pools

Resurrection Pools focuses on pool restoration in Port Charlotte and across Southwest Florida. Its site covers resurfacing, tile and coping, deck resurfacing with pavers and travertine, equipment upgrades, structural repairs and leak detection, water features and lighting, and sun shelves and spas. It says a full restoration comes with a single warranty and one point of accountability, and it has a financing page. It also offers pool inspections for homeowners, commercial properties and real estate transactions. Restoration is the site's main message, though one customer review published there says the company installed a new pool, so confirm new construction by phone. No license number appears in the record. Google Maps shows a 4.6 rating from 215 reviews, with 183 five-star ratings and 16 one-star ratings.
