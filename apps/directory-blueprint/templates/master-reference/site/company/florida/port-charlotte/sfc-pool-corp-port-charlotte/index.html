About SFC Pool Corp

SFC Pool Corp is a custom inground pool builder based in Port Charlotte that also serves Lee, Desoto, Sarasota, Broward and Palm Beach counties. The site says the business grew out of a family trade into design, construction and renovation, and that permitting is handled for customers. Add-ons named include spas, water features, sun shelves, lighting and fire features. It states that most custom pools run from $45,000 to $100,000 or more, depending on size, depth, finishes and add-ons, and that financing comes through a lender it partners with, Lyon Financial, which the site describes as a pool financing specialist since 1979. No license number, warranty, decking detail or commercial work appears in the record. Google Maps shows a 4.9 rating from 28 reviews, with 27 five-star ratings and 1 one-star rating.
