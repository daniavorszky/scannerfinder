About Superior Pools of Southwest Florida Inc.

Superior Pools of Southwest Florida is a family-owned custom pool builder with offices in Fort Myers and Port Charlotte, serving Charlotte, Lee and Collier counties and nearby cities. The site says it has built over 10,000 pools and lists Pool & Spa News Top 50 recognition. It describes a shotcrete shell process with rebar, tile and coping, then a deck poured or paved, and a screen enclosure erected where applicable. Renovations and equipment upgrades are a separate service. The site says the lifetime warranty is backed by the company's labor, and its financing page lists partner lenders. It shows Florida license CPC-1459983. The record does not mention commercial work. Google Maps shows a 4.8 rating from 354 reviews, with 334 five-star ratings and 15 one-star ratings.
