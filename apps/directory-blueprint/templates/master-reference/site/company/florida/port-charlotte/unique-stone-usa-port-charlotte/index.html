About Unique Stone USA

Unique Stone USA started as a concrete resurfacing company and now also builds custom pools across Southwest Florida. Its site says the family owned business has worked since 2001, offers concrete overlays for driveways, patios and pool decks, epoxy floors and paver installation and sealing, and has resurfaced hundreds of pools before moving into building them from the ground up. Pool renovation covers new plaster finishes and heated or cooled spa additions. Financing, a license number and warranties do not appear in the record. The overlay side of the business is the older and larger part of the site, so ask about pool building experience directly. Google Maps shows a 4.6 rating from 56 reviews, with 49 five-star ratings and 4 one-star ratings.
