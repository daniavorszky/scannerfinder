About East Coast Pools-Licensed

East Coast Pools & Spas, listed in Port Orange, presents itself as a family business run by owner Tom Gilbert, who is credited on the site with over 24 years in the pool industry. The site covers new pool construction in gunite and fiberglass, remodels, service calls, monthly pool service and spas, and it says the company designs and builds pools, spas, water and fire features, summer kitchens and screen enclosures. It also says commercial and residential pools, spas, water features and pool decks are offered, and it has a financing page. Three contractor numbers appear in the footer: CPC1457246, CPC1461577 and CBC1259865. The site says local newspaper readers chose it every year since 2014. Its pages name Ormond Beach and St. Augustine, so confirm that Port Orange is inside the service area. On Google Maps it has a 4.2 rating from 15 reviews, 12 of them five stars and 3 of them one star. A warranty is not mentioned.
