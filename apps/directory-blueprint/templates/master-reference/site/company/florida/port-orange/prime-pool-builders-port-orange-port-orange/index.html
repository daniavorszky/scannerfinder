About Prime Pool Builders Port Orange

Prime Pool Builders Port Orange says it has served Port Orange since 2015, building pools from design through start-up. Its site lists inground pool construction along with gunite and shotcrete, fiberglass installation and repair, and vinyl liner installation and replacement, plus remodeling, resurfacing and replastering, pool decks, water features and heaters. Concrete pavers and coping are part of the work it describes. It says it is licensed and insured for pool construction and will provide documentation on request, though no license number appears. The site explains workmanship warranty coverage and manufacturer equipment warranties, and it says it takes commercial jobs for HOAs, hotels and other facilities. Nearby areas named include South Daytona, Daytona Beach Shores, Ponce Inlet and Holly Hill. Google Maps shows a single five-star review and no posted hours, so the track record is thin.
