About Danley's Pool Service, Inc.

Danley's Pool Service, Inc. serves Port St. Lucie and nearby areas with pool building, renovation and upkeep. The site says it handles custom pool construction, including brand-new installs, with inground pools and spa combos as its focus, and it lists plaster, quartz, pebble and tile finishes with options such as waterfalls and lighting. Renovation and resurfacing get their own pages, along with equipment and automation work, weekly maintenance and tile. It describes its team as fully licensed and insured, though no license number appears in the record. The site speaks to residential clients and does not mention financing or a warranty. Google Maps shows a 4.4 rating from 11 reviews, with 8 five-star and 1 one-star ratings. Its testimonials mention a new motor, a salt system conversion and spa jet repairs.
