About Platinum Blu Pools

Platinum Blu Pools works out of an office on NW Enterprise Drive in Port St. Lucie and presents itself as a local pool builder. The site says the team develops swimming pools, builds spas of any size, and renovates or remodels existing pools, including adding technology features. The footer gives contractor license CPC1460148. A Financing page in the menu links to two lender names, and the site adds that it also does residential and commercial pool service such as lights, heaters, salt systems, pumps and automation. Free estimates are offered. The record does not say which construction materials it uses, and it does not mention a warranty. On Google Maps the company has a 4.7 rating from 35 reviews, 32 of them five stars and two at one star.
