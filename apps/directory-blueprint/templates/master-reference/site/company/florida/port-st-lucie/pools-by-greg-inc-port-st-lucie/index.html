About Pools By Greg Inc

Pools By Greg Inc is a Port St. Lucie pool builder and renovator serving the Treasure Coast, including Vero Beach, Hobe Sound, Fort Pierce, Okeechobee and Stuart. The site says it is locally owned and family-run, has over 40 years of experience, and specializes in custom in-ground pools, renovations and custom features for residential properties. It describes itself as licensed and insured, mentions financing options, and offers custom decking and tile finishes. A customer review on the site says the company repainted a deck after the 12-month warranty had ended, though the site gives no other warranty terms. Commercial work is not offered, since the site describes a residential focus. Google Maps shows a 3.8 rating from 119 reviews, 76 five-star, 9 four-star, 3 three-star, 2 two-star and 29 one-star.
