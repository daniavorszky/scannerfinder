About Pools USA

Pools USA is a Port St. Lucie pool service company that also lists pool remodeling, renovation and pool building among its services, and Google Maps files it as a pool supply store as well. The site describes weekly cleaning, repairs and equipment installation, and commercial pool service for apartment complexes, hotels and homeowner associations. It says it serves St. Lucie, Martin, Indian River and Okeechobee counties, and that it is fully licensed and insured, though no license number appears in the material. A pool building menu item is the only sign it builds new pools, and the site gives no detail on materials, financing or warranties. Google Maps shows a 4.5 rating from 122 reviews, 105 of them five stars, 2 four-star, 1 two-star and 14 one-star.
