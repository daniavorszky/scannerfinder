About Flamingo Pool & Spa Inc

Flamingo Pool & Spa says it specializes in the design, construction, renovation and refinishing of commercial swimming pools, and its gallery shows RV resorts, an inn and other business projects around Southwest Florida. It also has residential pages for construction, remodels, service and repairs, and heating and automation. A customer review on the site mentions adding a sunshelf and a spa during a remodel. The menu says financing is available, and the site lists contractor license numbers CPC056940 and CPC1457362 for its principals. Google Maps identifies it as veteran-owned, and shows a 4.3 rating from 25 reviews, 20 at five stars and 3 at one star. Warranties are not mentioned in the record. A showroom in Punta Gorda is open by appointment only.
