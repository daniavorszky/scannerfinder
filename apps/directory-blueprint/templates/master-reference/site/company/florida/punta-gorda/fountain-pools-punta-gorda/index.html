About Fountain Pools

Fountain Pools builds residential and commercial pools and in-ground spas across Southwest Florida, with the site naming Cape Coral, Venice, Sarasota, Lakewood Ranch and Punta Gorda. It says concrete pools offer complete personalization and describes itself as the commercial pool builder that architects and developers trust most. Other pages cover renovations, repairs, equipment upgrades, fountains, outdoor living, railings and full screen enclosures, plus tile, decking and coping. The menu has financing and warranty pages, including a way to submit a warranty ticket, and the footer lists CPC #1458460. Nothing on the site says whether it installs fiberglass or vinyl liner pools. Google Maps shows a 4.4 rating from 252 reviews, 211 at five stars and 33 at one star. The footer address is on Tamiami Trail South in Punta Gorda.
