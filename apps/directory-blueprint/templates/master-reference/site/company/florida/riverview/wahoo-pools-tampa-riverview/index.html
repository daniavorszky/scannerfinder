About Wahoo Pools - Tampa

Wahoo Pools' Tampa listing sits in Riverview and describes custom-designed, built and serviced pools across Tampa Bay. The site says it holds Florida license CPC1458680 and that every pool is backed by a structural warranty. Project write-ups show raised spas, travertine decking, paver fire-pit lounges and screen-enclosed pools, and a customer quote describes a remodel with a new finish, tile and decking. It offers pool financing with monthly payments, an online instant estimate and an AI design tool, plus residential and commercial pool service for HOAs, condos and resorts. It cites 1,236 or more pools built. The pages read do not name gunite, fiberglass or vinyl. Google Maps shows 114 reviews at a 4.8 rating, with 101 five-star and 4 one-star.
