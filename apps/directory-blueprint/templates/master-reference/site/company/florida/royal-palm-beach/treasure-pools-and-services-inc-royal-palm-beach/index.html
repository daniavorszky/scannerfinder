About Treasure Pools and Services Inc

Treasure Pools & Services says it has been transforming Palm Beach County backyards since 1985. The site lists new pool construction, pool renovation and remodeling, spas, outdoor kitchens, landscape design and installation, vanishing edge pools, waterfalls, and fire and water features, and it says it designs commercial and residential pools. It mentions inground pool designs, and it has a pay-as-you-go financing page. A customer testimonial published on the site says the owner had every paver replaced after a walkway problem, and another describes rebuilding pool deck areas. The site names Royal Palm Beach, Wellington, West Palm Beach and Stuart among its service cities. It does not show a contractor license number or warranty terms. Google Maps shows a 4.5 rating from 100 reviews, with 84 five-star and 11 one-star ratings.
