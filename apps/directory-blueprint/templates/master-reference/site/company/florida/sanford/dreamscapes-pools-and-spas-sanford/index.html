About Dreamscapes Pools and Spas

Dreamscapes Pools and Spas builds custom pools and spas in the Sanford and Orlando area, according to its site, which also names Lake Nona and Winter Garden. It says its spas are built with durable gunite, just like its swimming pools. The site has separate sections for screen enclosures, pavers, tile, fire features, lighting and outdoor living, and it describes classic, dome, mansard and panoramic enclosure styles. A Financing page is in the menu, and a contractor license, CPC1457433, appears in the footer. The site says nothing about a warranty or commercial work, and Google Maps lists no hours. The listing has a 4.8 rating from 244 reviews, with 231 at five stars and 8 at one star. Google Maps files it as a swimming pool contractor.
