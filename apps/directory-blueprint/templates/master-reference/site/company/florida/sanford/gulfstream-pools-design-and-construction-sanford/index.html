About Gulfstream Pools Design and Construction

Gulfstream Pools Design and Construction describes itself as a locally owned pool builder serving Central Florida, with a Sanford page among its service areas. Owners Jim Dowling and Jeromy Anderson are said to have over 40 years of combined residential and commercial pool experience. The site covers custom pool design and construction, a page for commercial projects, and a banner saying financing is available. It lists many area pages, from Brevard and Volusia counties to Lake Mary and Windermere. No contractor license number, warranty or specific pool material appears in the record.
