About Seminole Pool Supply

Seminole Pool Supply, in Sanford, sells pool supplies and services pools for customers in Seminole, Volusia and Orange counties, and says it has done so for more than 35 years. Google Maps also files it as a hot tub store and hot tub repair service. The site advertises financing with an online application and shows a contractor license number, CPC1460622. It says it is a warranty service provider for Jandy and Pentair equipment, which is repair authorization rather than a warranty of its own, but a customer review posted on the site says a replacement pump came with a one-year warranty. Nothing on the site says it builds pools. Google Maps shows a 4.5 rating from 120 reviews, 102 at five stars and 13 at one star.
