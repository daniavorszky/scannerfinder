About POOL COMMANDER

Pool Commander works out of Santa Rosa Beach on the Emerald Coast and describes a track record of more than 25 years in pools. The site lists new custom pool construction for homes and for commercial properties, along with 3D design, plastering, tile and coping, decking, hardscapes, water features and outdoor kitchens. Its founder's story centers on demand for inground concrete swimming pools, which points to concrete construction. Spas are part of the residential and commercial offering, and remodeling is covered too, including resurfacing the interior and replacing lighting, tile, coping and decking. There is a financing page with an online application. The footer shows two Florida certified commercial pool and spa contractor license numbers, CPC1460152 and CPC1460041. Repairs, inspections and equipment pages round out the menu. Screen enclosures and warranty terms are not mentioned in the material reviewed. On Google Maps it holds a 4.9 rating from 60 reviews, with 58 at five stars and one each at four stars and one star.
