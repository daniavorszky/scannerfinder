About Bulldog Pools

Bulldog Pools is a family-owned pool builder serving Sarasota and Manatee County. The site says it specializes in concrete gunite pools and designs and builds pools and spas. Its custom solutions list adds pool decks, water features, fire features and screen cages, and it offers a remodel service for existing pools. A financing page describes pool loans through Lyon Financial. The site also promises custom designs and free estimates, and its FAQ covers permit timelines. The record does not show a license number, warranty or commercial work. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 22 reviews, all five stars. The site lists a phone number, (941) 377-7665, and a quote request form. It describes premium materials and a process that runs from vision to finished pool.
