About Coast to Coast Pools

Coast to Coast Pools is a Sarasota builder that says it specializes in custom-designed concrete pools and has more than 45 years of experience. Its menu covers custom pool design, new pool construction, maintenance, remodeling, a construction process page, financing, awards, a scholarship program and a design tool. Feature options include water and fire features, lighting, custom decking and coping, energy-efficient equipment and automation. The site lists warranty protection among its selling points, gives license number CPC1457311, and calls itself Florida's number one pool builder, a claim the record does not support with a ranking. It lists weekday hours of 8:00 to 4:30. Google Maps also tags it as a pool supply store. It shows a 4.4 rating from 591 reviews, with 491 at five stars and 68 at one star. Commercial work is not mentioned.
