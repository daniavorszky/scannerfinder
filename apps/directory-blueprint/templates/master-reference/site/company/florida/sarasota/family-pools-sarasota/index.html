About Family Pools

Family Pools, which brands itself Florida Family Pools, is a Sarasota company with a sales center on Lakefront Drive open weekdays from 9 to 5. Its site covers new construction, pool remodeling, resurfacing and maintenance, plus a pool school, a financing page and a referral program that pays a set amount when a referral signs a contract. It says it offers comprehensive warranties on new pool construction, that it provides financing through trusted partners, and that it has done commercial and residential work since 2022. The meta text also cites 40 years of award-winning craftsmanship, an award the record does not name. License number CPC1460750 appears in the footer. Long keyword lists on the site touch on spas, screen enclosures and pavers, but no sentence confirms them. Google Maps shows a 4.8 rating from 187 reviews, with 172 at five stars and 7 at one star.
