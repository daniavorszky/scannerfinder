About Jackson Pools, Inc - Sarasota

Jackson Pools, Inc. runs a Sarasota office that its site menu places alongside locations in Fort Myers, St. Petersburg and Naples. The site says the team has more than 40 years of residential and commercial pool construction experience, and it lists custom residential pools, commercial pools, renovations, waterfalls, fountains and infinity edge designs. Its construction stages include a gunite step, and a review on the site describes a new pool built with a spa. The pool deck is handled by the crew, but the FAQ says the company holds a CPC license and does not build screen enclosures itself, using a trusted licensed partner instead. It shows license CPC022504. Financing runs through Lyon Financial and Paramount Capital. The Sarasota listing has one review, a five star, for a 5.0 rating. Warranty terms are not mentioned in the record.
