About Keystone Pool & Patio

Keystone Pool & Patio builds custom inground concrete pools in Sarasota and nearby areas, including Bradenton, Nokomis, Siesta Key, Longboat Key, Venice and Osprey. The site describes freeform and infinity edge shapes, with sun shelves, waterfalls, spas and lighting as options. It also builds patios in pavers, stamped concrete or natural stone, and lists outdoor kitchens and renovation work for short-term rentals. It offers flexible financing and shows a lifetime warranty. A customer quote mentions a marble tile pool deck. The record gives no license number and does not describe remodeling of existing pools. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 7 reviews, all five stars, so the review record is short. The Maps listing notes online estimates and onsite services, and the site sells outdoor products from several brands.
