About Sunrise Contractors of Florida LLC

Sunrise Contractors of Florida LLC is a Sarasota general contractor that covers new construction, remodeling, roofing, air conditioning and plumbing, with a Pools, Spas and Pool Cage service line among them. The site says it delivers pool construction, spa installation, pool deck renovation and screen enclosure work for residential properties, and it describes pool shells built with gunite or shotcrete. It reports more than 1500 projects across five licensed trades and shows general contractor license CGC1525605. Maps also tags it as a deck builder, painter, plumber, roofing contractor and pool repair service, so pools are one part of a wider business. The listing has a 5.0 rating from 5 reviews, all five stars, and the Maps profile lists Spanish language assistance and a wheelchair accessible entrance. Financing, warranty terms and commercial pool work are not mentioned in the record.
