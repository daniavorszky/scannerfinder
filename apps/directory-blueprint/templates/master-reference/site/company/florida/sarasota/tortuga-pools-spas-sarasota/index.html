About Tortuga Pools & Spas

Tortuga Pools & Spas is a Sarasota pool builder that says it has over 30 years of experience. The site covers custom pool design and installation, spas and water features, cold plunge and swim jet pools, renovations and remodels. It also lists pergolas, outdoor kitchens, fire features, lighting and audio, and pavers and hardscapes through a partnership with Hardscape.com. It shows Certified Pool Contractor license CPC1460102 and links to financing. The site describes a one-year labor warranty plus a one-year manufacturer warranty on equipment. It serves Sarasota and Manatee counties. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 19 reviews, all five stars. The site menu also has a repair request page and a warranty claim page. The record does not mention screen enclosures or commercial jobs.
