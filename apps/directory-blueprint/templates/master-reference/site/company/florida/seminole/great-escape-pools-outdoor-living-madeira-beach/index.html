About Great Escape Pools & Outdoor Living

Great Escape Pools & Outdoor Living, based in Madeira Beach, says it builds custom pools on Florida's west coast and describes work in Pinellas County. Its menu lists new pool builds, renovation of existing pools, repairs, maintenance, and pool cages and shade solutions, along with outdoor kitchens, furniture, lighting and landscape. The site discusses pool deck resurfacing and pavers, says it asks for a 10% deposit, and describes lump sum and construction loan options with a financing page. It also advertises 3D backyard design and current promotions. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 15 reviews, all at five stars. The site gives no license number or warranty in the record, and it does not clearly say whether it takes commercial work or installs spas. Call to confirm those points.
