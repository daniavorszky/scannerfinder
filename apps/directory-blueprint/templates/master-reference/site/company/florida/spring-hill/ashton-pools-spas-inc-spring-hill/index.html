About Ashton Pools & Spas Inc

Ashton Pools & Spas builds concrete, gunite, fiberglass and vinyl liner pools, according to its Spring Hill site, which claims over 60 years of combined crew experience and Tampa Bay work since 1998. It generally works in Hernando, Pasco and Citrus counties and says it has built commercial pools in several other counties. The service list includes new and existing pool renovations, acrylic spas, screen enclosures, liner replacements, pool demolition, pump and filter replacement, weekly service and ADA pool lifts. It lists state certification CPC057026 and advertises lifetime warranties on its Sun Fiberglass and Kafko pools. Financing is not mentioned. Google Maps shows a 3.5 rating from 19 reviews, 11 at five stars and 7 at one star. It says well trained in-house crews do the work.
