About Trinity Pools Inc.

Trinity Pools Inc. says it has done owner-supervised onsite construction for over 30 years and builds and remodels custom pools in Hernando, Citrus and Sumter counties. The site lists new pool construction, renovation and remodeling, pool deck remodeling, resurfacing, stone, pavers and tile, and equipment. It shows a spa-attached design, a homeowner review of an in-ground pool it completed, and a financing page naming Lyon Financial as a partner. The footer lists certified pool and spa contractor license CPC1458927, and the site says it installs only warrantied equipment brands. Commercial work is not mentioned. Google Maps shows a 4.3 rating from 98 reviews, 78 at five stars and 17 at one star. Its contact details show hours Monday to Saturday, 9AM to 5PM, and a Citrus County project page.
