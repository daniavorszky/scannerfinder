About Florida Coastal Contractors, LLC

Florida Coastal Contractors, LLC is a St. Augustine outdoor living contractor serving St. Johns County, with service pages for Nocatee and World Golf Village. Its pool work goes by the name Island Breeze Luxury Pools, and the site describes inground pools with waterfalls, tanning ledges and integrated spas, handled from 3D design and permitting through construction. It also builds paver patios and driveways, pergolas, pavilions, outdoor kitchens, fire pits and screen enclosures. The footer lists a state certified pool contractor license, CPC-056960, and a certified building contractor license. The site says it is a preferred builder for Lyon Financial, which offers loans to customers. Google Maps marks it women-owned and shows a 4.8 rating from 101 reviews, with 89 at five stars and 2 at one star. The record does not mention a warranty.
