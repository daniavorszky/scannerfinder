About Southern Pool Designs – Luxury Custom Pools St. Augustine

Southern Pool Designs runs a luxury custom pool practice that its site says has been designing and building gunite and shotcrete pools in house since 1997. The site has a St. Augustine page but a 407 phone number, and it describes itself as Central Florida based. It designs and engineers new pools with integrated spas and water features, and its menu covers residential and commercial work, a Rip & Replace page, water features and pool automation. It shows a certified pool contractor license, CPC1461548, marked for commercial and residential. The site claims more than $100 million in pools built and offers a custom pool cost guide. Financing, a warranty, fiberglass and vinyl liner work are not mentioned. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from one review.
