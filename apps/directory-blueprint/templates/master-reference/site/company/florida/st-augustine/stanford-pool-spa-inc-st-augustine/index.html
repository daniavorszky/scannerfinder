About Stanford Pool & Spa, Inc.

Stanford Pool & Spa, which brands its site as Stanford Custom Pools, describes itself as a family-owned design-build contractor serving St. Johns, Putnam and Flagler counties. The site explains that shotcrete is sprayed into the excavation to form the pool shell, and it says a build should take about 12 to 16 weeks. Along with pools it lists spas, waterfalls, fire features, screen enclosures, pergolas, gazebos, summer kitchens, artificial turf, paver and concrete decking, and pool renovations for existing pools. It says it is fully state licensed and insured, without giving a number, and it partners with Lyon Financial for pool financing. A warranty and commercial work are not mentioned. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 20 reviews, all at five stars.
