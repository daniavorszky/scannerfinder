About Tier 1 Pools and Spas

Tier 1 Pools and Spas presents itself as a custom pool design and build firm serving Jacksonville and the wider North Florida area, with a St. Augustine listing. Its site describes a process meant to be smooth and stress-free, ending with a full walkthrough of the finished features, and notes that new construction homes need a full set of the home's plans. Add-ons it says it offers include outdoor kitchens, pergolas, fire pits, water features, screen enclosures and outdoor fireplaces. It has a Financing page that points to three lending partners, Lyon Financial, HFS Financial and Viking Capital. The record does not name pool materials and does not mention a license number, warranty or commercial work. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 19 reviews, all at five stars.
