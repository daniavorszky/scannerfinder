About AQUA Custom Pools and Spas

AQUA Custom Pools and Spas is based in St. Cloud and describes a design and coordination approach to backyard projects: free 3D design, project management, pool construction coordination, and renovation and repair coordination through licensed Florida contractors. Its site lists custom pools and spas, lap and saltwater pools, rock waterfalls, pool decks, outdoor kitchens, pergolas, screen enclosures and artificial turf, and it has a pool financing page. The page title promises free 3D designs and a lifetime warranty. Because the site says the work goes through licensed contractors it coordinates, no license number of its own is shown. Google Maps notes Latino ownership and women ownership, online estimates, and language help in English, Polish, Portuguese and Spanish. It has a 4.7 rating from 17 reviews, 15 of them five stars.
