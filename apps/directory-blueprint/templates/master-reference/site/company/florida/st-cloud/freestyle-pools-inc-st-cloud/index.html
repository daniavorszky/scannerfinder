About Freestyle Pools Inc

Freestyle Pools and Plastering says it has been building and restoring pools in Osceola County since 1995. The site describes design, supply, installation and maintenance of pools and spas, and a blog post explains the advantages of gunite for in-ground pools. It lists refurbishing existing pools with a new interior finish, tile and decking, screen enclosures and re-screening, spa covers and safety fencing. It says it can complete the entire job, including excavation, equipment, pool deck, stamped concrete, pavers, brick, fencing and landscaping, and that it has a team for commercial and residential swimming pools. No license number, financing or warranty terms appear in the record. Google Maps shows 4 reviews, all at five stars, and no posted hours, so the public track record is short.
