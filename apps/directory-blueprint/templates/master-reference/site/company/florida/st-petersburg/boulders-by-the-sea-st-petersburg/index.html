About Boulders By The SEA

Boulders By The SEA is a St. Petersburg listing whose website is built around custom waterfalls and pool decking, with a pitch about turning a backyard into a resort. The site also shows pizza ovens and pool estates among its subjects. Its keyword text names paver decks, travertine decks and pool coping, but a keyword list is not proof, so the tagline about pool decking is the clearest service statement. Terms such as pool building and Florida pool builder appear only in that same list, and the one full sentence on pool construction is a general remark about natural rock versus artificial rock. That leaves new pool construction, spas, financing, licensing and warranty unconfirmed. Google Maps files it as a swimming pool contractor with a single review, five stars, for a 5.0 rating. Call to confirm whether it builds full pools or focuses on rock features, waterfalls and decks.
