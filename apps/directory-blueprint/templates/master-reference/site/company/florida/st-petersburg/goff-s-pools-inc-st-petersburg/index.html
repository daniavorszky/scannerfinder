About Goff's Pools, Inc.

Goff's Pools, Inc. says it has built and remodeled swimming pools and spas since 1971, working in Pinellas, Hillsborough and Pasco counties, including St. Petersburg and Clearwater. The site describes the company as a designer and builder of custom residential and commercial pools and spas, with structural renovations for existing pools and spas. It shows license CPC1457273 as a State of Florida licensed residential and commercial certified pool contractor and says it is fully insured. It offers a free consultation and says it aims to finish on time or ahead of schedule and within budget. The record does not mention financing, a warranty, decking or screen enclosures. Google Maps shows 4.3 from six reviews, five at five stars and one at one star.
