About Oasis Pavers and Pools

Oasis Pavers and Pools builds custom pools, spas and hardscape in St. Petersburg from a base on Anvil Street North. Its site says the company began with paver patios and driveways and later added paver pool decks and remodeling, and it now offers new in-ground pools, pool remodeling and paver work. The FAQ describes the shell stage as shotcrete, a concrete application, taking one to two weeks, and admits that some crews are subcontracted. The site says it has no in-house financing but partners with Lyon Financial for loans. It shows license CPC1458930 and says it was honored with five awards, including Pool & Hot Tub Alliance awards of excellence. Google Maps shows 4.9 from 343 reviews, 331 at five stars and seven at one star. No warranty or commercial work is mentioned.
