About Sunshine City Pools

Sunshine City Pools says it is locally owned and based in St. Petersburg, and that it specializes in custom pool builds, renovations and resurfacing. The site groups its work into custom builds and designs, renovations, and pool cleaning and repair, and it lists paver decks and artificial turf among its outdoor features. A customer comment on the site praises the office staff and the renovation and decking work. The site says its technicians handle everything from water balancing to pump overhauls, and it offers a free estimate. The record shows no license number, financing terms, warranty or commercial work. Google Maps shows 4.4 from 51 reviews, 40 at five stars and six at one star, with online estimates and onsite services.
