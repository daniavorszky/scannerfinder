About Tilney Pools

Tilney Pools designs and builds custom swimming pools in the Tampa Bay area, and its site claims more than 25 years of experience. The construction process it describes runs from layout through fencing and excavation, a rebar structure and a hand-formed shotcrete shell, then plumbing, equipment, coping and decking. Separate pages cover residential pools, commercial pools for hotels, communities and businesses, renovation, and financing. The renovation copy mentions resurfacing, new tile, decking and layout changes, and a customer review published on the site says a pool remodel was finished in just a few weeks. Google Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 132 reviews, 129 at five stars and three at four stars. The record gives no license number and mentions no warranty or spa work.
