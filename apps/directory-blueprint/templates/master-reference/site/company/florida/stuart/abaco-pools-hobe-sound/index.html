About Abaco Pools

Abaco Pools builds custom gunite pools in Palm Beach County and south Martin County, and Hobe Sound is one of the communities it names. The site walks through a build sequence that includes spa pre-plumbing and a shell step where a sand and cement mix is sprayed on. Its cost notes list spas among the features that raise the price of a custom pool. Renovations cover resurfacing, tile, coping and new decking, and the company also lists maintenance and repairs. Carl Keever is described as a Florida Certified Pool Contractor who handles permitting, engineering and inspections, and the site says he gives detailed estimates during a free in-home consultation. Nothing in the record covers financing, warranties, commercial work or screen enclosures. Google Maps shows a 4.9 rating from 110 reviews, with 105 five-star ratings and two one-star ratings.
