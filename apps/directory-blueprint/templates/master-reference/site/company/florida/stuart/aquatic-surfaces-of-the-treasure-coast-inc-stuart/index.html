About Aquatic Surfaces of the Treasure Coast, Inc.

Aquatic Surfaces of the Treasure Coast is a Stuart pool resurfacing company that describes itself as a Florida state certified pool contractor. The site focuses on plaster, retile and refinish work for pools and spas, restoration of concrete, steel and fiberglass pools, and finishes such as Diamond Brite and Florida Gem. It also lists deck and paver installation, equipment, and hotel and military pool work, and it says it takes residential, commercial and government jobs nationwide and abroad. Financing is offered through Lyon Financial. One line mentions construction among its skills, but its services center on resurfacing and renovation, so confirm before hiring it to build a new pool. It calls itself the top rated resurfacing company in the country, which is its own claim. On Google Maps it has a 4.7 rating from 39 reviews, 36 of them five stars and 2 of them one star.
