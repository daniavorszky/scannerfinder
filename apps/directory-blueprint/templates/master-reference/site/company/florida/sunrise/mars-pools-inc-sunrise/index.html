About Mars Pools Inc

Mars Pools Inc. presents itself as a pool and spa builder that has been designing pools, spas and water features since 2001. Its website title gives an address in Oakland Park, near the Sunrise listing, so confirm the service area. The site is mostly a photo gallery, showing an infinity pool with planters, a pool and spa with a rock walkway and spillover, and a Grecian pool with deck jets. It says it can supply tile, coping, travertine and marble, and it offers pool plastering finishes such as Pebble Tec. One passage names an owner, Michael R. Stewart, who guides customers through each step. The site does not mention financing, a license, a warranty or commercial work. Google Maps shows a 3.0 rating from only six reviews, three at five stars and three at one star.
