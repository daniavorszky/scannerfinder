About Pools by Design Build & Construct

Pools by Design Build & Construct, which goes by Pools by DBC, is a Sunrise pool company that says it designs luxury in-ground pools and spas and describes its crew as builders of outdoor living environments. The site also offers outdoor kitchens, pavers and hardscape, and aluminum pergolas, and it lists a pool store and a design consulting service. It says it works with financing partners, and its footer shows a CPC license number and a CGC number tied to Morales Construction. Google Maps notes online estimates, onsite services and English and Spanish assistance. The site does not say which shell materials it builds, and it does not mention warranties or commercial jobs. The listing has a 4.8 rating from 39 reviews, with 36 at five stars, one at one star, one at three stars and one at four stars.
