About BlueLaguna Pool Service

BlueLaguna Pool Service is a family owned and operated Tampa company that leans toward cleaning, maintenance and repair, with remodeling as a second line. Its services list covers hurricane relief, leak detection, equipment upgrades, lighting and automation, resurfacing and plastering, tile and coping work, and saltwater conversions. The remodeling page mentions new tile, modern decking and water features, and says it serves Tampa, Clearwater and St. Petersburg. The site says repairs under its Contractor Pro Package are handled by licensed and insured technicians. Building a new pool is not mentioned, so ask before assuming it takes construction work. Financing and warranties are not mentioned. Google Maps shows a 4.6 rating from 70 reviews, with 63 five-star ratings and 7 one-star ratings.
