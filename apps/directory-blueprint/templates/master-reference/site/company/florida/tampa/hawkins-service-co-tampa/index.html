About Hawkins Service Co.

Hawkins Service Co. is a Tampa home services company where pool work sits alongside air conditioning, heating, plumbing and electrical. The site describes a family owned and operated business that has served the area since 1993, with a second market in Fort Myers. Its pool menu covers heaters, automation systems, salt water chlorinators, pool wiring, filters, filter repair, commercial pool repair, commercial pool safety inspections and pool remodeling. The record says nothing about building new pools, so treat it mainly as a repair, equipment and electrical option and confirm by phone if you want construction. The site advertises flexible financing plans and says its plumbers and technicians are licensed and insured. The warranties it mentions apply to heating and air conditioning work, not to pool work. Google Maps shows a 4.9 rating from 5584 reviews, with 5317 five-star ratings and 149 one-star ratings.
