About Patio Pools, Inc.

Patio Pools, Inc. has built custom residential inground pools around Tampa Bay since 1979, according to its site, which says three generations of the family are involved. The site lists new pool and spa construction and remodeling of pools and spas, and it offers 3D AutoCad renderings. A retail store is also part of the business, and the site notes an appearance on a Tampa Bay morning show. A license number, CPC1458992, appears on the site. It does not mention financing, warranties, pool materials, screen enclosures or commercial work. Google Maps lists the company in Carrollwood with a 4.1 rating from 158 reviews: 113 five stars, 10 four stars, 3 three stars, 4 two stars and 28 one star, a wider spread than the rating alone suggests.
