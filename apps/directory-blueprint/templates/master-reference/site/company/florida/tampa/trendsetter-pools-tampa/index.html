About Trendsetter Pools

Trendsetter Pools is a custom pool builder working from Tampa across Hillsborough and Pasco counties, with pages for Wesley Chapel, Riverview, Lutz, Land O' Lakes and Apollo Beach. The site says it designs and builds fully customized inground pools and also handles remodeling, renovation and resurfacing. It says it can take on commercial pool, spa, fountain, renovation and service needs, and it describes a design process that starts with the homeowner's vision. The site names project pages for outdoor living transformations as well, and its menu links to a page of custom pools. The record does not name a construction material and does not mention financing, a license number or a warranty, so confirm those by phone. Google Maps shows a 4.2 rating from 55 reviews, with 42 five-star ratings and 9 one-star ratings.
