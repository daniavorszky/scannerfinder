About Ultra Custom Group

Ultra Custom Group is a Tampa home remodeling and outdoor living company that also runs a pool design and build division. The site describes kitchen and bathroom remodeling, a showroom, outdoor kitchens and fireplaces, and pool, spa and waterfall design, and says it specializes in residential homes. It lists licenses CPC1457644 and CRC1331943. A service section explains that pool equipment, surfaces, pavers and tiles carry manufacturer warranties, and that Pentair and Jandy equipment warranty calls go through those brands. Whether it remodels existing pools, offers financing or takes commercial work is not stated. The site itself looks dated, with a 2020 copyright, and it calls the company a premier builder, which is its own claim. Google Maps shows a 4.6 rating from 34 reviews, with 31 five-star ratings and 3 one-star ratings.
