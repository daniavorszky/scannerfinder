About Graco Construction

Graco Construction is a Lithia contractor whose site describes custom pools, patios and enclosures for Central Florida and the Tampa Bay area. It says it builds custom concrete inground pools, with a process that runs from design through a final inspection, and it also lists pool renovation with new finishes and lighting. The patio menu includes pavers, travertine, tile installation and colored or basic concrete. Enclosure pages cover screen enclosures, pool enclosures, hybrid and two-story designs, screen rooms and sunrooms. Two contractor license numbers appear in the footer, and a free quote is offered. The record does not mention financing, warranties or commercial work. Google Maps shows a 4.9 rating from 81 reviews, with 79 five-star ratings and one one-star rating.
