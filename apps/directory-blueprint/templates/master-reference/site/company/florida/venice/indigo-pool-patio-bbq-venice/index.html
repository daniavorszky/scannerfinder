About Indigo Pool Patio BBQ

Indigo Pool Patio BBQ builds pools and runs a retail store under one roof in Venice, at 620 Cypress Ave. Its site describes a swimming pool builder and outdoor living retail center serving Venice, North Port, Sarasota and Englewood, with grills, smokers, patio furniture and outdoor kitchens alongside pools. The menu covers new pool construction, water features, custom spas, pool service, equipment, financing, and renovations and remodels, and the text mentions resurfacing existing pools. It says it is currently building inground spas, which can be paired with a pool or built standalone. The site shows license CPC1460829. It does not mention a warranty, and the record has no gunite, fiberglass or commercial claims. On Google Maps it holds a 4.7 rating from 345 reviews, 309 of them five stars and 15 of them one star.
