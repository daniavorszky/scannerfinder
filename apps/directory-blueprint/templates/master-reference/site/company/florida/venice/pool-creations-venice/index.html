About Pool Creations

Pool Creations describes itself as Venice's licensed custom pool builder, serving Sarasota, Manatee and Charlotte counties since 2013. The site says the company designs and builds new pools, remodels and resurfaces existing ones, handles repairs and runs weekly service, and it takes commercial pool construction and renovation. It lists license CPC1458354 and states that Pool Creations is a DBA of R.A.C.E. Pool & Spa Services, LLC. It works with lending partners to help homeowners finance new pools, remodels and equipment, and it claims more than 5,200 projects. The site also mentions Pentair-certified installation and warranty service, which is a repair credential and not a warranty on its own work. On Google Maps it has a 4.8 rating from 78 reviews, 72 of them five stars and 4 of them one star.
