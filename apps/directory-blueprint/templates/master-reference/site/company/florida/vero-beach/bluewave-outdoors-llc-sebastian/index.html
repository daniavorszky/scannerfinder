About Bluewave Outdoors, LLC

Bluewave Outdoors, LLC in Sebastian shows its Florida license, CPC1460953, at the top of its site, and its menu covers pool building, remodeling, cleaning, repair, inspections, pool school, pool start ups and backyard upgrades. The site says it designs and builds custom in-ground pools and describes a vinyl liner pool with a 10-inch rebar-reinforced concrete wall. It says the surface of its custom pools is backed by an extended warranty. It also mentions decking, kitchens and pergolas, paver sealing and heater cleanouts. The concrete wall is the only concrete construction described, so gunite is unconfirmed, and fiberglass and hot tub words appear only in a keyword list. No financing or commercial work is mentioned. Google Maps shows 57 reviews and a 4.9 rating, with 55 at five stars, and identifies the business as women-owned.
