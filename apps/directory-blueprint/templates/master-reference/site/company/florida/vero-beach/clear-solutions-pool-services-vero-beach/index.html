About Clear Solutions Pool Services

Clear Solutions Pool Services works out of Vero Beach and serves Indian River County. Its site describes pool repair, remodeling and upgrades, with service sections headed Construction, Update or Upgrade, and Decks & Hardscapes. The company says it has operated for more than two decades and is led by co-owners Michael Auld, described as a certified pool/spa contractor and operator, and Zach Martin, who directs projects and sales. The site says Martin has experience with commercial pools and spas as well as residential ones, though commercial work is not presented as a service line. Remodel copy talks about updating outdated finishes and modern upgrades. The site does not mention financing or a warranty, and it stops short of saying the company builds new pools from scratch. On Google Maps it has a 4.9 rating from 115 reviews, 111 of them five stars and 3 of them one star.
