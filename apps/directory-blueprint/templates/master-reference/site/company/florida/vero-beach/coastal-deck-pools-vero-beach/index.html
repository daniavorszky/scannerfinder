About Coastal Deck & Pools

Coastal Deck & Pools is a Vero Beach company run by Lynn and Barb Spilman, who the site says have lived in the area since 1986. It says its professionals design and build in-ground pools and spas, remodel existing pools, and install pavers on driveways, decks and patios in brick, marble or travertine. The site says the company is licensed and insured but gives no number. It also points visitors to tile distributors named NPT, Luv Tile, Aquabella and USA Marble, and it mentions a showroom with posted weekday hours. Financing, warranty and commercial work go unmentioned. Google Maps lists it as a pool contractor, concrete contractor and paving contractor, with a 3.4 rating from 13 reviews, 7 five stars and 5 one star.
