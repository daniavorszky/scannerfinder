About Odom Custom Pools

Odom Custom Pools is a Vero Beach builder that says it specializes in custom swimming pool construction and is a division of a family owned company. Its service area runs from Vero Beach to Fort Pierce, Sebastian and Port St. Lucie, and the site lists new construction, remodeling, repairs, leak detection and accessories. A step-by-step construction page describes rebar shells, gunite sprayed over the steel cage, soil compaction testing by a geotechnical engineer before the deck, and waterline tile, decking and coping. It shows license CPC1458195 and names Jared Odom as the person available during and after construction. Financing, warranty and commercial work go unmentioned. Google Maps shows 4.6 from 9 reviews, 8 five stars and 1 one star. Google Maps files it only under swimming pool contractor.
