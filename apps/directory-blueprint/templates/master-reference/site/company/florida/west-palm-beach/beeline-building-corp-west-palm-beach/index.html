About Beeline Building Corp

A family-owned general contractor serving Palm Beach and Martin counties, Beeline Building Corp names a sister company, Beeline Pools and Roofing Corp, on its menu. The home page lists custom home building, shell construction, remodels, dirtwork, concrete and demolition, and it puts pool design, pool construction and pool installation next to roof installation and replacement, along with hurricane windows and additions in its wider work. The site says it is licensed and insured and shows four license numbers, one of them CPC-1460768. Its remodel and commercial wording describes homes and buildings in general, and the site says nothing about pool remodeling, financing or a warranty. Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 18 reviews, all five stars, and lists French and Ukrainian language assistance.
