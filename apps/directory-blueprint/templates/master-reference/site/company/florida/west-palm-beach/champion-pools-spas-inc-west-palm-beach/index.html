About Champion Pools & Spas, Inc.

Champion Pools & Spas is a West Palm Beach pool builder that says it has worked for over 30 years across Palm Beach County and South Florida. The site describes custom swimming pools, spas and outdoor living projects for homeowners, developers and commercial clients such as hotels, resorts and community developments. Its menu has a financing page and it lists license CPC057289. The build steps it describes end with finishing touches such as decking, tile and interior finish. Customer reviews published on the site mention a renovation with a full resurface, and one describes a warranty repair the company handled. The record does not say which pool materials it builds. Google Maps shows a 4.6 rating from 199 reviews, with 177 five-star and 17 one-star ratings.
