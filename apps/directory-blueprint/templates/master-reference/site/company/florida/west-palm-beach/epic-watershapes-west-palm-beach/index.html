About Epic Watershapes

Epic Watershapes presents itself as a custom pool builder for West Palm Beach, though the license line on its site gives a Boca Raton office address. The site title promises inground pools and remodeling, and the text says its crews design, permit and build gunite pools, with 8 to 12 weeks of construction after approvals. Its menu lists pool and spa construction, remodeling, resurfacing, repair, pool decks and driveways, outdoor kitchens, pergolas, 3D design, commercial pools and fountains, and a financing page. The site states license CPC1460900. It describes a 10-year warranty on quartz finishes and a lifetime warranty on PebbleTec, and says Epic holds the warranty on the whole project. Fiberglass, vinyl liner and screen enclosures are not mentioned. Maps shows 5.0 from 55 reviews, all five stars, with English, Portuguese and Spanish assistance.
