About FDS Pools

Based in Loxahatchee according to its page title, FDS Pools says it builds and services pools, fountains and water features from Palm Beach County to Miami. The site runs in English and Spanish and lists custom pool construction, remodeling, service and repairs, weekly maintenance, and patio and driveway work with pavers. Weekly maintenance is described as chemicals, brushing, vacuuming and filter service. Its construction text covers engineering, permitting, spas, sun shelves and lighting, while remodeling includes Diamond Brite resurfacing, waterline tile, coping and deck work. The site description states license CPC1460866 and says it takes commercial projects for hotels and restaurants alongside residential ones. Financing and a warranty are not mentioned. Maps shows a 5.0 rating from 20 reviews, all five stars.
