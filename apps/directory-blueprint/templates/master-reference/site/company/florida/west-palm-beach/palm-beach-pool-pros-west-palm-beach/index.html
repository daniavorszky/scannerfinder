About Palm Beach Pool Pros

Repair and resurfacing lead the pitch at Palm Beach Pool Pros, a West Palm Beach listing whose menu also includes remodeling, coping repair, fountains, saltwater pools, equipment and new pool construction. The site gives Florida contractor license CPC1460269 under the name Cloud Lake Sunking Partners LLC. Its quote form lists options such as liner replacement and adding a spa, but the service pages do not confirm them. Some wording says the site has found the county's best contractors and refers to providers, which suggests it may match customers with contractors, so ask who will do the work. Financing and a warranty are not mentioned. Maps shows a 4.5 rating from 15 reviews, 13 of them five stars and two at one star.
