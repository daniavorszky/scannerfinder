About Palmer Marble & Tile, Inc.

Palmer Marble & Tile is a stone and tile fabrication company that Google Maps also files as a swimming pool contractor in West Palm Beach. Its site says it began in a small backyard shop with three employees and now does high-end residential and commercial work in Florida, and it also mentions Japan, Canada, the Caribbean and Saudi Arabia. One project it describes was an 11,000 square foot home with bathrooms, walls, staircases, a pool and a pool deck. The menu covers tools and materials and waterjet cutting.
