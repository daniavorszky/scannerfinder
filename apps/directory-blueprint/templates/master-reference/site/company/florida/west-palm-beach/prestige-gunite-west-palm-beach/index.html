About Prestige Gunite

Prestige Gunite & Shotcrete describes gunite and shotcrete application for pools and for other structures such as walls, dams, culverts and tunnels, plus concrete structure rehabilitation and infrastructure reinforcement. It describes its reputation as built on customer service. Its residential services page lists pool construction and repair, and the site says the method suits curved pools because concrete is shot against excavated soil. The site links to CRH, a building materials group, and has a locations page, but nothing captured names West Palm Beach as a service area, so confirm that before you count on it. Remodeling, financing, a license number and a warranty are not mentioned. Maps shows a 4.3 rating from 12 reviews, nine of them five stars, two at one star and one at four stars.
