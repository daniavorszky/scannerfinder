About RPD Custom Pools

RPD Custom Pools builds custom pools, spas, water features and stonework in West Palm Beach, with remodeling on its menu as well. The site describes a process that runs from consultation to a building plan and quote, and it breaks payment into six draws that include gunite, plumbing, hardscape and plaster stages. Marble and travertine samples and glass waterline tile are among the finishes it mentions. It says it partners with Buildertrend on financing with no down payment, and it states license CPC1459061. Warranties, the site says, come through the manufacturers of the equipment, plaster and setting materials. Commercial work is not mentioned. Maps shows a 4.3 rating from 29 reviews, 24 of them five stars and five at one star.
