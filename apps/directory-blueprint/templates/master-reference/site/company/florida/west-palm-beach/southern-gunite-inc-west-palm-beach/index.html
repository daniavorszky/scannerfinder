About Southern Gunite Inc

Gunite shells are the specialty at Southern Gunite Inc, a West Palm Beach listing that says it builds custom pool shells and fountains. According to the site, it works directly with homeowners and builders on new pool construction, additions and upgrades, and it takes large commercial jobs such as community pools and subdivision developments. The site says it has served Florida since 2011 and completed more than 25,000 projects. Its content centers on the shell, so it does not say whether it also finishes pools with tile, decking or equipment, and it does not mention financing, a license number or a warranty. Maps shows a 3.9 rating from 16 reviews, 11 of them five stars and four at one star.
