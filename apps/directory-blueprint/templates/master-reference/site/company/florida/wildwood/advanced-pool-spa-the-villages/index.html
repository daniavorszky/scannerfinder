About Advanced Pool & Spa

Advanced Pool & Spa, a division of Advanced Construction & Remodeling, LLC, builds pools around The Villages and Central Florida. The site says it is family and veteran owned, has worked in the area for over 20 years, and specializes in custom in-ground concrete pools and in-ground spas, with lanais and summer kitchens among the outdoor work. It also renovates existing pools. It describes itself as a state licensed and insured pool builder and general contractor, without a number in the text read. The site lays out a warranty of a lifetime shell, five years on components and three years on equipment, and menu pages cover pricing and a $50K pool package. No financing is mentioned. Google Maps shows 16 reviews, all five stars.
