About Blue Haven Pools & Spas

Blue Haven Pools & Spas in Wildwood is the Villages location of Blue Haven Pools on the Gulf, a builder the site says is based in Florida and Alabama, with many Florida locations plus Mobile. It offers inground pool design and construction, renovations, resurfacing, equipment and repairs. The pool construction steps include deck installation with pavers, natural stone or stamped concrete, and customer reviews on the site praise a gunite pool build and the renovation of an older pool. The site says its pool designers can explain financing and describes an in-house warranty team, with a Warranty page in the menu. No license number appears in the text read. Google Maps shows 37 reviews, all five stars, and lists onsite services.
