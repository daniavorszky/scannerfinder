About T & D Pool Construction

T & D Pool Construction is a Wildwood builder serving The Villages, Sumter County and nearby towns. The site says the company builds custom pools and spas and has been at it since 1983, calling out 43 years of experience. Its services list includes custom concrete pools and spas, fiberglass pools, renovations of pools, pavers and acrylic texture, water features, summer kitchens, fireplaces and firepits, stone work and electrical work, and it publishes pool and spa plans. It notes it may keep a waiting list for new construction rather than overbook. The footer shows several license numbers, including CPC1458794 and CPC1458777. Financing and warranty are not mentioned in the text read. Google Maps shows 53 reviews at 4.3, with 39 five-star, 6 four-star and 7 one-star ratings.
