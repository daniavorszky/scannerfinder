About Premier Pools of Central Florida

Premier Pools of Central Florida describes itself as a family owned and operated Orlando pool builder that makes every pool fully custom. Its site lists modern geometric, infinity, lap, plunge and freeform pools, hot tubs and spas, fire features, grottos, lighting, waterfalls, sun shelves and pool bars, plus renovations of existing pools. It names Orlando, New Smyrna Beach, The Villages, Winter Park, Windermere and Winter Garden as locations and has a financing options page. The site says the company is licensed and insured, but no license number appears in the record. Warranty terms, commercial work and paver decking are not mentioned. Google Maps shows a 4.7 rating from 268 reviews, with 238 five-star, 13 one-star, 6 two-star, 9 four-star and 2 three-star ratings.
