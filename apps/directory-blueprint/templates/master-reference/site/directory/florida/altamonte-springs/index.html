Altamonte Springs splits its pool trade between backyard builders and commercial firms, and homeowners looking for pool builders here should know which is which. Ten businesses are filed under Altamonte Springs. Eight are in Seminole County and two are in Orange County, based in Apopka. Most of the residential builders name Orlando as their market, and a few add Longwood, Sanford and Oviedo.

Paradise Pools By Design says it has designed and built luxury pools and spas across Central Florida for nearly two decades. Its site sets out a step by step construction process that includes a gunite shell and decking finished with brick pavers, travertine or concrete. It also has a commercial division, which it says builds pools and spas for hotels, resorts and multifamily developments, and its menu adds splash pads. It lists financing and a warranty, and it has a 4.5 rating from 62 reviews.

Magic Pools is an owner-operated Central Florida builder run by Petar, a civil engineer with more than 25 years of experience according to its site. It says it builds inground gunite pools using shotcrete, in knife-edge, geometric and freeform designs, and it adds spas, water features, pavers, decks, screen enclosures, pergolas and outdoor kitchens. Resurfacing and remodeling are named too. It states a contractor license and has a 4.9 rating from 36 reviews. Inspire Pools LLC describes itself as an Orlando area pool builder and says its team has designed and built hundreds of pools. A heading names inground gunite swimming pool building, and the pages add a spa collection, renovations and repairs and an easy financing page. It has a 5.0 rating from 7 reviews.

The Apopka businesses on this page are different from each other. Weller Pools LLC positions itself as a commercial aquatic builder working across the U.S. and the Caribbean since 1974, and its menu groups projects into water theme parks, community developments, municipal pools, competition pools, hotels and resorts, fitness and wellness, international work and splash pads. It is not a backyard pool company, and it has a 4.7 rating from 6 reviews. 3 ABN Pools describes itself as a family business serving Central Florida with more than 20 years of hands-on experience. It lists new pool construction from layout and excavation through plumbing, tile and finish, plus remodeling, resurfacing, repair, equipment replacement and routine service. It has a 5.0 rating from 1 review.
