Pool builders in Boca Raton tend to sell more than a pool. Read how the local trade describes itself and the same items keep appearing beside the pool: paver decks, driveways, pergolas, outdoor kitchens, turf and lighting. Romance Pools, AP Outdoor Design, Master Touch Outdoor Living, M&M Pool Builders and Dream Pools of Florida LLC all present the work as a whole backyard, not a shell in the ground. For a homeowner the first decision is often scope: a pool on its own, or a finished outdoor space with a pool in it. The Boca Raton page carries 26 pool builders and contractors, and almost all of them sit in Palm Beach County by ZIP code.

New construction and remodeling run side by side here. Many of the builders take both jobs: they will dig a new inground pool, and they will also replaster, retile, reshape or rebuild an older one. Romance Pools says on its site that it covers everything from new inground pools to complete remodels, and adds decking, tile replacement, replastering and equipment upgrades. Fort Pools, Hydroline pools and Excel Pool & Patio Solutions describe similar mixes of new pools, renovations and repairs. If your pool is already in the ground and tired, this is a market with several companies used to that job.

Concrete is the material most builders here name. M&M Pool Builders, a family-owned company, says it builds custom gunite pools in Boca Raton and Palm Beach County. Master Touch Outdoor Living describes a gunite shell in its build steps. Epic Watershapes lists inground construction with reinforced gunite and calls itself a certified PebbleTec Elite builder. EDX Pools says its new pools start as gunite and shotcrete shells, and it lists infinity edges and resort-style designs. Right Choice Pools & Spas builds and renovates custom inground pools across South Florida, and Excel Pool & Patio Solutions describes fiberglass as an option beside gunite.

Builders based here work well beyond the city. Their own sites name Palm Beach, Fort Lauderdale, West Palm Beach, Delray Beach and Jupiter, and some name Palm Beach County as a whole. Liberty Pools is based in Boca Raton and also serves Palm Beach County and the Port St. Lucie area. Epic Watershapes names West Palm Beach and Jupiter. MBG&A Complete Pool Service and Backyard Remodeling says it has built custom inground pools across Palm Beach and Broward counties for over 15 years. Master Touch Outdoor Living shows an address in Coral Springs and says it also serves Delray Beach, Boynton Beach and Highland Beach.

Common questions about pool builders in Boca Raton

Which Boca Raton pool builders work in gunite or concrete?

M&M Pool Builders, Master Touch Outdoor Living and Epic Watershapes each describe gunite construction on their sites. Right Choice Pools & Spas lists gunite pool construction in its menu, and EDX Pools says its new pools start as gunite and shotcrete shells.

Who in Boca Raton builds the deck, pergola or outdoor kitchen along with the pool?

AP Outdoor Design began as a paver contractor and now lists driveways, pergolas, outdoor kitchens, stone pavers and landscaping beside new pools and remodels. Dream Pools of Florida LLC lists pavers, decks, pergolas, outdoor kitchens, turf and putting greens. Epic Watershapes lists outdoor kitchens, pergolas and pool decks.

Where can I find a Boca Raton company to remodel an older pool?

Romance Pools lists complete remodels, tile replacement, replastering and equipment upgrades. Fort Pools says it will build or renovate a pool, spa, sun shelf, water feature or outdoor kitchen. Summer Pool Solutions presents itself as a remodeling, repair and maintenance company.

Do Boca Raton builders also work in Broward County or farther up the coast?

MBG&A Complete Pool Service and Backyard Remodeling says it has built pools across Palm Beach and Broward counties. Liberty Pools also serves Palm Beach County and the Port St. Lucie area. Master Touch Outdoor Living says it serves Delray Beach, Boynton Beach and Highland Beach as well as Boca Raton.

Who takes commercial pool work around Boca Raton?

Romance Pools, Dream Pools of Florida LLC and Epic Watershapes each say on their sites that they take commercial work along with residential projects. The Pool Scientist also describes serving residential and commercial clients, with design, construction, maintenance and repair.
