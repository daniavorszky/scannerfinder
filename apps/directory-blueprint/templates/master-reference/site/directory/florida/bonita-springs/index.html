Pool builders in Bonita Springs work in Lee County, and the names they give for where they work reach into Estero, Naples and Fort Myers. Nine businesses share this page. Two of them, Azure Pool and Deck Design, Inc. and McDaniel Construction of SW FL, Inc, are based in Estero, which is the smaller town that shares the page with Bonita Springs.

The trade here leans toward remodelling and finishing work as much as toward new construction. Azure Pool and Deck Design, Inc. describes itself as a family business that specializes in resurfacing and remodeling pool areas and backyards, and it also lists new pool construction, spa additions, pool heaters and paver patios. Celebrity Pool and Spa lists luxury pools, renovations and spas, and its site says every pool is designed in 3D. Christie's Luxury Pool Builders has a name that suggests new construction, but its site centers on resurfacing, waterline tile, coping, pool decks and paver, travertine and stone decking.

Where builders say they work matters in a town that sits between two larger markets. Celebrity Pool and Spa names Naples, Estero, Bonita Springs and Fort Myers. Christie's Luxury Pool Builders names Naples, Bonita Springs and Estero. Ocean Drive Pools works from a Bonita Springs address on Old 41 Road and says it builds residential and commercial pools across Collier, Lee and Charlotte counties. Waterscapes Pools and Spas describes itself as a custom pool builder for Southwest Florida and its menu also links to Tampa and Bradenton, so a Bonita Springs homeowner should ask it directly whether it takes jobs here. Aqua Artisans lists a Big Pine Key address in the Florida Keys, so the same question applies to it.

McDaniel Construction of SW FL, Inc is a different kind of business from the pool specialists. It calls itself a licensed and insured Class A general contracting firm, and its menu runs from new home construction and remodeling to Hurricane Ian recovery and pool construction. Homeowners who want a pool built as part of a larger project may find that shape of company useful, and those who want a pool-only builder will look elsewhere on the page.

Common questions about pool builders in Bonita Springs

Who remodels or resurfaces existing pools in Bonita Springs?

Azure Pool and Deck Design, Inc. says it specializes in resurfacing and remodeling pool areas. Christie's Luxury Pool Builders centers its site on resurfacing, waterline tile, coping and decking, and Celebrity Pool and Spa lists renovations alongside new pools and spas.

Which Bonita Springs pool builders also work in Estero?

Azure Pool and Deck Design, Inc. and McDaniel Construction of SW FL, Inc are both based in Estero. Celebrity Pool and Spa and Christie's Luxury Pool Builders each name Estero among the places they serve, alongside Naples and Bonita Springs.

Does anyone in Bonita Springs build commercial pools?

Ocean Drive Pools says it handles residential and commercial pool construction across Collier, Lee and Charlotte counties. Waterscapes Pools and Spas says it contracts with large developers to build commercial pools, and Azure Pool and Deck Design, Inc. says it is licensed for residential and commercial work.

Can I finance a pool with a Bonita Springs builder?

Azure Pool and Deck Design, Inc. is the business on this page whose site mentions financing. Ask any other builder you contact about payment options directly, because the rest of the sites here do not say.

Which nearby cities have their own page?

Naples, Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Lehigh Acres and North Fort Myers each have a page on this directory. Builders based in Estero appear on the Bonita Springs page, since Estero does not have one of its own.

Is there a general contractor that builds pools in Bonita Springs?

McDaniel Construction of SW FL, Inc is an Estero general contractor. Its site lists pool construction next to new homes, remodeling, kitchen renovations, roof repairs and Hurricane Ian recovery, so it suits homeowners bundling a pool with other work.
