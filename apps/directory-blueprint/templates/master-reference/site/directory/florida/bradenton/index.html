Screen enclosures come up in this market. DuWet Pools & Spas, Umbare Pool builder Lakewood Ranch and Agnelli Pools & Construction all list them. Florida law says a new residential pool must have at least one of five safety features to pass final inspection, and an enclosure that meets the barrier requirements is one of them. Which builders serving Bradenton also work in Lakewood Ranch and Parrish?

Jackson Custom Pools & Outdoor Living LLC names Lakewood Ranch, Parrish, Palmetto and Anna Maria Island. DuWet Pools & Spas names Lakewood Ranch, Parrish, Palmetto and Ellenton. Florida Native Pools says it serves Lakewood Ranch and Parrish.

What does Florida require for pool safety before a new Bradenton pool passes inspection?

A new residential pool must have at least one of five safety features to pass final inspection: a barrier enclosure, an approved safety cover, door and window exit alarms, self-closing and self-latching devices on doors, or a pool alarm. Measured from the outside, the barrier has to be 4 feet high or more.

Who in Bradenton will build a pool with a screen enclosure?

DuWet Pools & Spas, Umbare Pool builder Lakewood Ranch and Agnelli Pools & Construction each list screen enclosures on their sites. Agnelli also says it can add patios, pool enclosures and covered areas around the pool.
