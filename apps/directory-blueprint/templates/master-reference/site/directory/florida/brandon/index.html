Tampa Bay Pools and Splash City Pools both list screen enclosures next to the pool itself, which connects to Florida's residential pool safety law. A new residential pool must have at least one of five safety features to pass final inspection and receive a certificate of completion, and an enclosure that isolates the pool and meets the barrier requirements is the first on that list. Whatever encloses the pool must rise at least 4 feet on the side facing away from it.

Common questions about pool builders in Brandon

Who builds gunite pools in Brandon and who installs fiberglass?

Tampa Bay Pools describes gunite pools on its site, along with custom spas and outdoor kitchens. Splash City Pools lists fiberglass pools among its residential and commercial options, next to remodeling and renovation. The two are the clearest example of each shell type on the Brandon page.

Which Brandon pool builders take commercial work?

Tampa Bay Pools says it takes residential or commercial work, and Splash City Pools lists commercial pools on its menu alongside residential ones. Both are based in the Tampa Bay area and describe wide service territories, so it is worth asking either one about the size and type of commercial job you have in mind.

Do Brandon pool builders offer financing?

Tampa Bay Pools advertises pre-approved financing. Splash City Pools has a financing page on its site, and Reed's Swimming Pool Services also lists financing among its services. Terms are set by the lender, so each company is the place to ask about rates and requirements.

Which builders in Brandon have a showroom or a retail store?

Tampa Bay Pools mentions a design showroom, where a homeowner can look at options before choosing. Reed's Swimming Pool Services runs a retail pool store from its Brandon shop, and it names David Reed Jr. as the chief designer who supervises jobs.

Which towns around Brandon do the local builders say they serve?

Tampa Bay Pools names the Tampa area, Bradenton, Sarasota and Riverview. Splash City Pools describes itself as a Tampa Bay builder. Other Brandon-area sites name Valrico, Lithia, Wesley Chapel, Seffner and Dover, so the trade here covers most of Hillsborough County and some of the towns beyond it.
