Common questions about pool builders in Cape Coral

Who builds gunite pools in Cape Coral?

San Juan Custom Pools & Spas specializes in custom inground gunite pools. Sunset Pools builds a shotcrete or gunite shell. Design Concepts & Construction works exclusively on new inground concrete pools and spas, and MAC Custom Pools & Aluminum offers gunite pool and spa installations.

Which Cape Coral pool companies also build screen enclosures?

Back Bay Pools, LLC lists screen enclosures and rescreens. Proline Pools and Into H2O Pools and Spas list screen enclosures, and San Juan Custom Pools & Spas replaces pool cages and screen enclosures. MAC Custom Pools & Aluminum lists aluminum work as part of its pool business.

What does Florida require for a new pool's safety before final inspection?

A new residential pool needs at least one of five safety features: a barrier enclosure, an approved safety cover, door and window exit alarms, self-closing and self-latching door devices, or a pool alarm. The outside of that barrier has to stand at least 4 feet high. Ask your builder which one the plan uses.

Who works in Cape Coral and Fort Myers both?

Cape Coral Pool Builders names Cape Coral and Fort Myers. Proline Pools says it has served both for over 25 years. Infinity Custom Pools, Inc. has service pages for Cape Coral, Fort Myers and Lehigh Acres. Real Nice Pools, Inc. has area pages for Cape Coral and Fort Myers.

Which Cape Coral builders offer financing?

Contemporary Pools Inc, Keller Pools, Sunset Pools, San Juan Custom Pools & Spas and Proline Pools all list financing. Into H2O Pools and Spas and Infinity Custom Pools, Inc. list it as well. Ask about terms with each company directly.
