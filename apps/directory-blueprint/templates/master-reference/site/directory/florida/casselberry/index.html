Screen enclosures matter here for a reason beyond shade. Florida's residential pool safety law says a new residential pool must have at least one of five safety features to pass final inspection: an enclosure that isolates the pool, an approved safety cover, exit alarms on doors and windows that open to the pool, self-closing and self-latching door devices, or a pool alarm that detects entry into the water.
