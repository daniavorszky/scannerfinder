Pool builders in Clearwater work in a county that is almost entirely Pinellas, and the ones on this page tend to look well beyond it. The page carries 16 pool builders and pool contractors, and 15 of them sit in Pinellas County. Their own websites reach north into Pasco and Hernando counties, east to Tampa and Hillsborough, and south to Bradenton and Sarasota. A homeowner in Clearwater is choosing from businesses that expect to drive.

Concrete is the construction method the local trade describes most often. Island Way Pools builds custom concrete shell pools with shotcrete, and Flagstone Pools and Pavers walks through a shotcrete build with steel reinforcement, plumbing, tile, coping and decking done by an in-house team. Affordable Pools and Spas LLC describes concrete pool construction across Pinellas County, including vanishing edge, beach entry and infinity designs. Clearwater Inground Pool Builders, Construction, Remodeling, Resurfacing lists gunite and fiberglass construction and also covers vinyl liner pools, though its site has a vinyl section written for League City and menu pages for New Port Richey and Ocala, so a caller should confirm what it does in Clearwater.

Where the builders say they work matters here, because few of them stay inside city limits. Florida Pool Tech is based in Clearwater and says it has offered pool equipment repair, leak detection and custom construction since 2009 around Tampa Bay, Bradenton and Sarasota. Tropical Pools and Pavers says it has more than 25 years of experience building pools, spas and paving for homeowners across the Tampa Bay and St. Petersburg area. Lifestyle Pools LLC describes itself as a family owned builder and remodeler working across Clearwater, Pinellas, Pasco, Hernando and Hillsborough counties.

Work on existing pools is a large part of the Clearwater trade. Premium Pool Renovations says it is family owned with more than 27 years of experience, and the work it describes is surface and tile renovation: refinishing in Diamond Bright, Marquis and Hydrazzo finishes, new pool tile, and new decks in thin pavers or acrylic. Artistic Pools of Florida Inc. calls itself a state licensed pool, spa and deck contractor and shows the certified pool contractor number CPC1458877 on its site, including commercial work. Its services cover new pool construction, surface, tile and deck remodels, equipment repair and cleaning. Gulf Bay Chemical Co describes pool service across Pinellas County, from weekly cleaning and repairs to remodeling projects.
