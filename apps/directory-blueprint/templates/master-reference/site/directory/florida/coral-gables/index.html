The trade here leans toward general contractors, renovation firms and luxury builders more than toward one-material pool shops. One Stop Construction presents itself as a full-service general contractor for South Florida, working from Coral Gables, and pools are one line in a long service menu. Its site lists pool installation, renovations, liner replacement, equipment repair, cleaning and inspections next to roofing, windows, kitchens, bathrooms, decks, brick pavers, fences and landscaping. It takes residential and commercial projects, and it shows 4.7 from 82 reviews.

Somar Pools works Miami Dade, Broward and Palm Beach counties and the Caribbean, with a Miami Beach address and separate Fort Lauderdale and Miami phone lines. Its site says it has focused on luxury pool design, construction, leak detection and maintenance for more than two decades, and its builds include infinity edge pools and spas. Its rating is 4.6 from 16 reviews.

ForeverPools is a specialist in one job. Its site describes a pool renovation firm that resurfaces old pools with European glass mosaic tile, and says it serves the southeastern United States and the Caribbean. It claims more than 1,000 pools renovated or resurfaced with glass tile, in both commercial and residential work. A homeowner with an older pool that needs a new surface will find a different kind of business here than a builder of new pools.
