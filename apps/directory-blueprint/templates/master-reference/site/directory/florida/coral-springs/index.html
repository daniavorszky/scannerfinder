Licenses matter in this market because the classes differ. Florida law defines a commercial pool and spa contractor, a residential pool and spa contractor, and a servicing contractor that repairs pools but does not build new ones. Master Touch Outdoor Living, Inc, Brisa General Services and WaterFall Pools, Inc. each state a license on their sites, and Ross Services states a general contractor license. A stated license is a claim the homeowner can check through the state's public license search.

What do the Florida pool contractor license classes mean?

Florida defines a commercial pool and spa contractor, a residential pool and spa contractor, and a servicing contractor. Servicing covers repair and equipment work but not building a new pool. Sites in Coral Springs show pool prefixes and, in the case of Ross Services, a general contractor license. Check any number with the state.
