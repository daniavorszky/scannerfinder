Renovation carries a large share of the pool work in Deerfield Beach, and the pool builders here often describe a service area that runs up the coast into Palm Beach County as easily as it runs inland across Broward County. Some of them build new pools from scratch, some spend most of their time on existing pools, and a few are really hardscape or maintenance companies that Google files under swimming pool contractor.

On the new-build side, Van Kirk & Sons Pools & Spas describes itself as a luxury custom pool builder for South Florida. Its site lists pool and spa designs in several styles, hot tubs, luxury pool renovations, waterfalls, fire pits, lazy rivers, pergolas and outdoor kitchens, and it also lists financing and commercial work. Trevenzoli Pool & Spa says it specializes in custom concrete pool construction, spa installation and pool remodeling across South and Central Florida, and it holds 4.9 from 115 reviews. Its site describes design, engineering and construction of concrete pools, and spas designed to pair with a pool.

Nextgen Pools serves Broward County and Palm Beach County from Deerfield Beach and lists backyard design, new construction, renovation, maintenance and equipment installs. Its site says it can handle brand-new designs as well as existing pools that need remodeling, mentions custom rockwork for residential or commercial projects and gives financing its own page. The rating is 5.0 from 6 reviews, a small base that a homeowner should weigh accordingly.

Master Pool Services says it has more than 17 years in pool maintenance, repairs, renovations and construction, and it holds 5.0 from 52 reviews. Its site states that it is licensed and insured, lists customized designs for new pool construction, and describes renovation work on coping and tile with durable materials for the deck. Near Me Pools leads with cleaning and maintenance, then adds repairs, equipment work, leak detection, drain repair and refill. On the remodeling side it lists resurfacing, Diamond Brite finishes and acid washes, along with pavers and coping for the deck. It says it serves homes and condominiums across Broward County and Palm Beach County, and it has 4.9 from 112 reviews.

Common questions about pool builders in Deerfield Beach

Which Deerfield Beach pool builders specialize in concrete pools?

Trevenzoli Pool & Spa says it specializes in custom concrete pool construction, with design and engineering described on its site, and it adds spas made to pair with a pool. Van Kirk & Sons Pools & Spas also builds custom pools and spas in several styles. Ask either which shell material fits your design.

Who handles pool remodeling and resurfacing in Deerfield Beach?

Renovation is the most common service in town. Trevenzoli Pool & Spa redesigns existing pools, Near Me Pools lists resurfacing with Diamond Brite finishes and acid washes, and Master Pool Services covers coping and tile. Nextgen Pools also takes on existing pools that need remodeling.

Do Deerfield Beach pool companies work in Palm Beach County as well as Broward?

Yes, several say so on their sites. Nextgen Pools serves Broward County and Palm Beach County, and Near Me Pools says it works on homes and condominiums across both counties. Trevenzoli Pool & Spa describes work across South and Central Florida.

Which Deerfield Beach businesses offer financing for a new pool?

Van Kirk & Sons Pools & Spas, Trevenzoli Pool & Spa and Nextgen Pools all list financing on their sites, and Nextgen gives it a page of its own. Lender terms vary, so ask each company what it partners with and what the lender requires.

Who can build a new deck around a pool in Deerfield Beach?

Master Brick & Tile, Inc. presents its work as pool decks, patios and driveways, and Near Me Pools lists pavers and coping. Master Pool Services describes a pool deck page that names durable finish materials. These are worth a call when the pool itself is staying as it is.

Which towns near Deerfield Beach have their own pool builder pages?

Boca Raton, Pompano Beach, Oakland Park, Coconut Creek and Margate each have a page of their own. Boca Raton has 26 businesses and Pompano Beach 19. If you live close to the town line, it helps to look at both pages.
