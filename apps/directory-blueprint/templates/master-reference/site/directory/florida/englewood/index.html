Straight Line Aluminum Construction, LLC has a 4.9 rating from 24 reviews. It is a family-owned aluminum contractor that leads with gutters and lists pool cages, screen enclosures, acrylic rooms, paver installations and handrails, and it says it has served Englewood, Sarasota and Port Charlotte since 2011. Enclosures matter for new pools. Florida law requires a new residential pool to have at least one safety feature before final inspection, and an enclosure that isolates the pool is one of the options. A cage or fence used this way has to be at least 4 feet tall on the outside.
