Fort Myers pool builders tend to name a much wider territory than the city itself. The 36 businesses on this page are all in Lee County. Colonial Pool & Spa, founded in 1984, says it works around Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Naples and the rest of Southwest Florida. Coastal Custom Pool & Spa puts Naples first among its service areas, with Fort Myers, Sanibel, Captiva, Marco Island, Estero and Bonita Springs also named. Custom Pools by Design serves Southwest Florida from Punta Gorda to Bonita Springs. For a Fort Myers job, it is worth confirming a builder's service area with its office.

Concrete construction leads the local work. Jackson Pools, Inc. says it is based in Fort Myers and has served Southwest Florida for decades, and its FAQ walks through construction stages from excavation and steel to gunite, plumbing and deck. Spiro Custom Pools builds fully custom gunite pools and has a commercial side for hotels, resorts, HOAs and municipalities. A&E Pools, LLC is a family-owned builder of custom inground gunite pools that handles permits in house and states plainly that it does not install fiberglass. Barnes Pools has been in business since 1982, when Al Barnes founded it in Cape Coral.

Fiberglass and vinyl liner have a place too. Tropical Pools & Screens lists concrete and fiberglass pools, names Latham, Sun Pools and Barrier Reef, and has a comparison page for fiberglass, vinyl liner and concrete. Colonial Pool & Spa installs new vinyl liner pools and replaces liners in existing ones. Lombardo Pools lists gunite, fiberglass and vinyl liner work. Premier Pools and Spas Ft. Myers is the local office of a national pool builder brand and presents gunite, fiberglass and vinyl inground pools.

Screen enclosures show up often among Fort Myers builders, and they connect to Florida's residential pool safety law. A new residential pool has to have at least one of five safety features, such as an enclosure that isolates the pool, an approved safety cover, door alarms, self-latching doors or a pool alarm, before it passes final inspection. Superior Pools of Southwest Florida walks through a construction process that ends with a screen enclosure, and Bahama Pools of Florida Inc lists pool cage screen on its menu.

Which builders work in both Fort Myers and Naples?

Tropical Pools & Screens lists fiberglass pools with Latham, Sun Pools and Barrier Reef named on its menu. Colonial Pool & Spa installs new vinyl liner pools and replaces liners. Lombardo Pools lists gunite, fiberglass and vinyl liner work.

Who builds custom gunite pools in Fort Myers?

A&E Pools, LLC designs custom inground gunite pools, starts with a 3D design and handles permits in house. Barnes Pools features custom gunite pools and spas in any shape and size. Spiro Custom Pools builds fully custom gunite pools for homes.

Who builds pools for Charlotte County homeowners?
