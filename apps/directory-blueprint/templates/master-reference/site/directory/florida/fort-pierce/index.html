Remodeling and repair have their own place in this market. Blue Revive is a pool and spa remodel and repair company with a 4.8 rating from 16 reviews. Its site shows license CPC1461289 and says the company is led by a licensed pool contractor. Customers can ask for a free quote by phone, text or email. Several of the other businesses here also list remodeling and resurfacing next to new construction, so a homeowner with an older pool has more than one route.

A commercial pool license and a residential pool license are different classes under Florida law. A commercial pool and spa contractor may build any pool, public or private, while a residential pool and spa contractor is limited to residential pools and spas. Louden Bonded Pools, Hang 10 Pools Treasure Coast and Dynamic Pools, Waterfalls & Irrigation all list commercial work, so a homeowner can ask which class the license behind the quote belongs to. Any license shown on a website is stated by the business and should be checked with the state before a contract is signed.
