Common questions about pool builders in Green Cove Springs

Who builds concrete pools in Green Cove Springs?

Southern Pools of Florida builds custom concrete pools, including a packaged concrete model. Premier Outdoor Inc, working from Fruit Cove, describes concrete pool installation and saltwater pools. Blue Solutions Pools lists inground construction in gunite. All three also work around Jacksonville.

Is there a Green Cove Springs builder that installs fiberglass pools?

Southern Pools of Florida installs fiberglass pools from its Imagine Pools line and builds concrete ones too. Blue Solutions Pools also lists fiberglass among its inground options. Ask each which shell sizes and shapes they offer before choosing between a fiberglass and a gunite pool.

Which builders here offer a screened enclosure with the pool?

Breakwater Construction headlines custom pools, spas and screened enclosures. Southern Pools of Florida and Blue Solutions Pools also list screen enclosures among their services. Florida counts an enclosure as one way a new pool can meet its safety rules, so it is worth raising in the first call.

Do builders around Green Cove Springs offer financing?

Breakwater Construction has a financing page on its site. Premier Outdoor Inc, Southern Pools of Florida and Blue Solutions Pools also list financing. Ask each for the terms in writing and check the license and warranty details they state.
