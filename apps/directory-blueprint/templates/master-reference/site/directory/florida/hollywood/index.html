Hollywood pool builders work at the border of Broward and Miami-Dade counties, and the websites of the businesses on this page are written around Miami and Fort Lauderdale more than around Hollywood itself. The page carries 14 pool builders and pool contractors, 10 in Broward County and 4 in Miami-Dade County. Several are based in nearby towns such as Hallandale Beach, North Miami Beach, Aventura and Dania Beach.

Territory here stretches in both directions. Somar Pools, LLC says it works in Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach counties and the Caribbean, and its site lists separate phone lines for Fort Lauderdale and Miami. Focco Pool Builders of Miami has a North Miami listing and says it serves Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach counties. The Pool Group works out of North Miami Beach and says it serves Aventura, Hollywood Beach, Golden Beach, Bal Harbour and Miami Beach. Across the page the builders name Miami most often, then Fort Lauderdale, North Miami and Miami Beach.

The materials on offer are described in different terms. GPI Pools - Florida is a fiberglass pool business with a showroom, and its site calls the company a pioneer in manufacturing fiberglass pools, with model lines named Style, Fun and Mini. Eagle Concrete Corp. is a family-owned Broward County exterior contractor that says it has worked since 2005 on driveways, patios, pavers, travertine and pool decks. It designs and builds new custom inground pools and spas and states plainly that it does not work on vinyl or fiberglass pools. The Pool Group lists custom pool construction and shows styles from resort-style to modern and freeform designs.

Some businesses come to pools from design and construction. TotalScape Design is a landscape design and build firm whose site is written around Fort Lauderdale, with a Google Maps address in Hallandale Beach. It says it brings more than 40 years of experience to residential and commercial properties, and its menu has a Custom Pool page next to hardscaping, landscaping and 3D design. FullHouse Construction Inc. is a Hollywood contractor that presents itself as a residential and commercial construction firm doing new builds and remodels, with renovation pages for kitchens, bathrooms, outdoor living and pools.
