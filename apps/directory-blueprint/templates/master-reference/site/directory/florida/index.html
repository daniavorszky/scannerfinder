Pool Builders in Florida, by city

1,557 pool builders in 134 cities. Every city page ranks the businesses in it by rating and review count, with featured placements marked.

This page lists every Florida city that has its own page on this directory. A city gets a page when at least three listed pool builders are based there. The number beside each city is how many businesses its page shows.

Florida has many small towns with one or two pool builders and no more. Those businesses are not dropped. Each one is shown on the page of the nearest city that has a page, with a note on its card saying where the business is based. A town that sits more than 40 kilometers from any city page keeps a small page of its own, listed under Smaller towns below.

Every city page is ordered the same way. Paid featured and best-of positions come first and are marked as paid. Next come the businesses whose own websites confirm that they build new pools. The rest follow. Inside each group the order is by Google rating and number of reviews, so a business with a long record of reviews sits above one with a handful.

The listings come from Google Maps records filed under swimming pool contractor. We read each business's website and left out the ones that turned out to be cleaning routes, repair shops, supply stores or unrelated trades. Where a business had no website to read, its listing says "not enough info" and stays on the list.

Pool contractors in Florida are licensed by the state. A certified contractor may work in any county. A registered contractor may work only in the places where it is registered. If you are comparing builders from two neighboring cities, ask each one whether its license covers your address, and check the license with the Department of Business and Professional Regulation.
