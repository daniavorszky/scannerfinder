Jacksonville Beach is where the pool builders on this page keep their offices, and it is also where many of them start their service areas: the beaches, then Jacksonville, then out across Northeast Florida. The page carries 13 businesses, all with a ZIP code in Duval County. Atlantic Beach and Neptune Beach each add businesses of their own, so the page covers the beach communities as a group rather than one town.

New construction is the center of the trade. SurfSide Pools & Spas says it has served Duval, St. Johns and Nassau counties from Jacksonville Beach since 1977, with an emphasis on inground pools. The Batts Company, also based in Jacksonville Beach, says it has been building custom inground pools since 1977 and serves Northeast Florida from St Augustine to Amelia Island. Coastal Luxury Outdoors describes new residential pools, including concrete pools shot with gunite, and lists spa builds, decking, pavers and remodel work beside them.

Some of the trade comes at pools from another direction. Coastal Improvement Corporation is a general contractor first, working on custom homes, additions, renovations and commercial projects, and it says complete in-ground pool installation is one of its services. Pools By Stella Inc, based in Atlantic Beach, splits its site into maintenance and remodeling on one side and new construction on the other, with custom designs that use water features, infinity edges and in-pool spa jets. Taurus Pools works from a Jacksonville Beach office and focuses on new pool construction and remodels.

Common questions about pool builders in Jacksonville Beach

Who builds inground pools at Jacksonville Beach?

SurfSide Pools & Spas says it has built new pools and spas from Jacksonville Beach since 1977, with an emphasis on inground pools. The Batts Company says it has built custom inground pools since 1977 and serves St Augustine to Amelia Island. Coastal Improvement Corporation lists complete in-ground pool installation among its general contracting services.

Is there a builder here that pours gunite pools?

Coastal Luxury Outdoors describes new residential pools that include concrete pools shot with gunite. It runs a Jacksonville Beach office and lists spa builds, decking, pavers, remodels and replastering as well. Other businesses on this page describe inground pools without naming the shell material.

Who serves the beaches and Ponte Vedra?

Atlantic Coast Pool, Inc. says it serves the beaches, Ponte Vedra, Nocatee and Duval County, and calls itself a pool building, cleaning and repair specialist since 1985. SurfSide Pools & Spas says it serves Duval, St. Johns and Nassau counties. Ponte Vedra Beach has its own page on this directory.

Which Jacksonville Beach companies remodel older pools?

The Batts Company lists remodeling, resurfacing, replastering and tile and decking work. Taurus Pools focuses on new construction and pool remodels. Pools By Stella Inc has a maintenance and remodeling side, and Accurate Pool Company, which began as Accurate Leak Detective, lists a renovation and remodel crew.

Who has offices outside Duval County?

Coastal Luxury Outdoors says it has satellite offices in Fleming Island, Florida and St Marys, Georgia, alongside its Jacksonville Beach office. Pools By Stella Inc names Fernandina Beach, Saint Augustine and Orange Park among its towns, and The Batts Company covers St Augustine to Amelia Island.
