Jupiter sits where Palm Beach County meets Martin County, and the pool builders here work on both sides of that line. Of the 15 pool builders and pool contractors on this page, 13 are in Palm Beach County and 2 are in Martin County, both based in Tequesta. The builders describe a trade built around custom concrete pools, showrooms and long-running family firms.

Gunite is the material the Jupiter trade describes most. Almar Jackson Pools, Inc. designs and builds custom pools from its Tequesta base and describes a build that ends with a gunite shell, with vanishing edge, Euro edge, free-form and linear designs on its service list. RHR Pools builds custom pools and spas in Jupiter and the Palm Beaches and walks through excavation, steel, a reinforced gunite shell and plumbing inspections on its construction page. Cristallo Pool/Backyard Concepts calls itself a luxury custom pool company and lists custom concrete and gunite pools. Innovative Outdoor Living has separate pages for fiberglass and gunite construction.

Several Jupiter businesses have been at it a long time. Artistic Pools Inc. builds from a Hepburn Avenue office in Jupiter, says it started in 1988 and is celebrating its 38th year, and shows state license CPC056713 on its site. Dolphin Pools & Spas Inc says it has operated since 2001, calls itself family owned and says it serves Palm Beach County. AAA Custom Pools, Inc says its owner and president has designed and built pools since 1976. Artech Pools Inc says it has designed pools since 2000 and lists inground pools, financing and screen enclosures.

Showrooms are part of how Jupiter builders present themselves. Innovative Outdoor Living describes a 7,000 square foot outdoor living supercenter in Jupiter, with spas, pergolas, outdoor kitchens, backyard makeovers, landscaping and lighting alongside its pools. RHR Pools also mentions a showroom, and its site has an FAQ that covers pool barrier rules and decking choices such as marble and brick pavers. Crystal Yards Pool Construction is a family-run company that says it builds pools and outdoor living areas across Palm Beach, Martin and St. Lucie counties, and it lists hot tubs, water features, fire features, tile, synthetic turf and outdoor kitchens.

Who builds gunite pools in Jupiter?

Almar Jackson Pools, Inc., RHR Pools and Cristallo Pool/Backyard Concepts each describe gunite or concrete construction on their sites. Innovative Outdoor Living has a separate page for gunite pools next to its fiberglass page. Artistic Pools Inc. lists beach entry, decking, tile and coping among its construction steps.

What safety features does a new pool in Jupiter need?

Under Florida's residential pool safety law, a new residential pool needs at least one of these to pass final inspection: a barrier that meets the requirements, an approved safety cover, exit alarms on doors and windows, self-closing and self-latching door devices, or a pool alarm. On its outer face the barrier cannot be lower than 4 feet.

Almar Jackson Pools, Inc. is based in Tequesta and says it builds in Martin and Palm Beach counties. Crystal Yards Pool Construction says it works across Palm Beach, Martin and St. Lucie counties. Both name the county line as part of their territory.
