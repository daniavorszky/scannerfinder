Kissimmee is a town where pool builders and pool repair companies share the same page, and a homeowner looking for a new pool has to sort one from the other. Ten businesses are here: nine with ZIP codes in Osceola County and one, CRA MODERN POOLS, LLC, based in Davenport in Polk County. The builders describe work across Kissimmee and Central Florida, with a few reaching to Orlando and Daytona Beach.

Its site says it builds new custom pools and renovates existing ones in the Kissimmee and Central Florida area, and that it has renovated commercial pools since 2009. The build description covers excavation, plumbing, tile, decking, screen enclosures, equipment and salt filtration, with waterfalls, spas and rock features as add-ons. It works with Lyon Financial on financing, shows a contractor license and mentions a warranty.

Hallmark Pool & Spa World / Pool Builder Orlando works out of Kissimmee and says it has been building pools for Central Florida since 1981, led by founder Marshall Huneycutt with Kristina Huneycutt running daily operations. Its site lists custom pool design and construction, fiberglass installation as an authorized Latham dealer, remodeling and repairs including resurfacing and deck renovation, and commercial pools for resorts and HOA communities. It has 4.2 from 82 reviews. That mix makes it the one Kissimmee name that describes both gunite-style construction and fiberglass installation.

It has 3.9 from 33 reviews. CRA MODERN POOLS, LLC presents itself as a Davenport pool designer and installer with a modern style. Common questions about pool builders in Kissimmee

Who installs fiberglass pools in Kissimmee?

Hallmark Pool & Spa World / Pool Builder Orlando lists fiberglass installation as an authorized Latham dealer, next to custom pool design and construction. It works out of Kissimmee and says it has built pools for Central Florida since 1981.

Which Kissimmee pool builders work on commercial or HOA pools?

Hallmark Pool & Spa World / Pool Builder Orlando lists commercial pools for resorts and HOA communities. Bluescape Pools & Spas says it has renovated commercial pools since 2009, and The Pool Repair Guy serves commercial and residential clients.

Who offers financing for a new pool in Kissimmee?

Bluescape Pools & Spas works with Lyon Financial, according to its site. CRA MODERN POOLS, LLC carries a Finance Available page, and Hallmark Pool & Spa World lists financing among its services. Ask each company about terms and lender requirements.

Who resurfaces or repairs an older pool in Kissimmee?

The Pool Repair Guy focuses on resurfacing and repair, including pumps, motors, heaters, leak detection and deck repair. Hallmark Pool & Spa World lists remodeling and resurfacing next to new builds, and Bluescape Pools & Spas renovates existing pools.

Do any of the Kissimmee builders serve St. Cloud and the wider Osceola County area?

Osceola Aquatics Inc. says it serves Osceola County, Kissimmee and St. Cloud, with more than 20 years in the area. Bluescape Pools & Spas describes the Kissimmee and Central Florida area. St Cloud also has a page of its own.

Are there builders on the Kissimmee page from outside Osceola County?
