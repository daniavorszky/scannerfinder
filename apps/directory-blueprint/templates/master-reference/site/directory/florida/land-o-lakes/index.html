Pool builders in Land O' Lakes work in Pasco County, and the trade here is a mix of one established builder and several small companies filed as swimming pool contractors. The work here divides into a few kinds. Rainbow Pools Inc does full construction and renovation. Jeremiahs Above Ground Pool Installation, by its name, does above ground installs. A homeowner should know which of these three jobs they want before making the first call, since the businesses on this page are set up for different ones.

Rainbow Pools Inc says it designs, fabricates, renovates and repairs residential and commercial pools and spas, in concrete or fiberglass, and it advertises pool packages and a new pool with an enclosure. Its site names owner Larry Nelson as supervising every project. It lists financing and a warranty, and it states a certified pool contractor license. Its rating is 4.5 from 8 reviews. Rainbow also lists commercial work beside its residential pools, and its site shows a pool with an enclosure.

Florida separates certified pool contractors from registered ones. A certified contractor holds a state certificate of competency and may contract anywhere in the state, while a registered contractor has met local requirements and may work only in those jurisdictions. A license shown on a site is a claim the homeowner can check with the state.
