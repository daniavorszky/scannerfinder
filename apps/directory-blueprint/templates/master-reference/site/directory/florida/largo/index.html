Pool builders in Largo work mostly in concrete, and they work across Pinellas County and the wider Tampa Bay area rather than inside the town line. This page carries 18 pool businesses, all with Largo addresses and all in Pinellas County. When these builders talk about where they work, Tampa comes up again and again, along with Clearwater, St. Petersburg and Seminole. Pinellas Park, Lutz and even west Orlando appear as well, each tied to a single business.

The custom concrete side of the trade is the most crowded. Pool Perfection has a Largo showroom and headquarters and a second showroom in Lutz. It says it has worked since 2004 and has built more than 1,800 concrete pools, and it describes shotcrete construction, small and plunge pools, spa additions and free 3D designs. Its rating is 4.7 from 273 reviews. Hollywood Pools and Spas works from an office on 72nd Street. It calls itself veteran owned and describes a pneumatically applied concrete shell it calls shockrete, which it says it uses in place of traditional gunite. Its rating is 4.6 from 52 reviews.

LIV Pools builds gunite pools out of Walsingham Road and describes an in-house team that handles design, pricing and construction, with an instant 3D design and Hayward automation and a salt system included. It holds 4.9 from 30 reviews. Mandalay Pools & Spas, on Ulmerton Road, shows customers 3D representations of the backyard after a first consultation and adds outdoor kitchens and fire pits to its pool work. Its rating is 4.7 from 50 reviews. Sunsation Pools & Spas, Inc. builds and renovates in Clearwater, Largo and Seminole, says it has 17 years of experience and also offers enclosures and decking. It stands at 4.4 from 96 reviews.

Some builders here treat the pool as one part of a larger yard. Birchwood Building & Pools pairs pool construction with landscaping, irrigation, artificial turf and hardscape across Pinellas, Hillsborough, Pasco and Manatee counties, and holds 5.0 from 21 reviews. Plankey Custom Pools names Largo, St. Petersburg, Clearwater and Seminole as its territory and describes heavy rebar framing and shotcrete, though it has a single review so far. Financing pages turn up on the sites of Pool Perfection, Mandalay, LIV Pools, Birchwood, Sunsation and Curtis Pools, which matters if you are pricing a full backyard rather than a pool alone.

Common questions about pool builders in Largo

Who builds concrete or gunite pools in Largo?

Pool Perfection, Hollywood Pools and Spas and LIV Pools all describe concrete construction. Pool Perfection says it has built more than 1,800 concrete pools, Hollywood Pools and Spas describes a shockrete shell it uses in place of traditional gunite, and LIV Pools builds gunite pools with an in-house design team. Sunsation Pools & Spas and Birchwood Building & Pools also list concrete pool work.

Which Largo companies resurface or renovate an older pool?

Fibre Tech Inc. and Aquatic Surfaces FL both specialize in fiberglass resurfacing over concrete pools. Curtis Pools and Sunsation Pools & Spas list remodeling and resurfacing alongside new construction, and Boulder Legacies adds hand carved rock features to existing pools. Innovative Pool Concepts Inc. lists remodels next to equipment repairs and upgrades.

Does anyone in Largo build pools for hotels or apartment communities?

Kelly Pools describes commercial construction and remodeling for hotels, apartment complexes and condominiums since 1987. Curtis Pools lists residential and commercial pool construction plus a run of commercial maintenance and repair work. Hollywood Pools and Spas and Sunsation Pools & Spas also list commercial work on their sites.

Which builders take on the whole backyard, not just the pool?

Birchwood Building & Pools pairs pool construction with landscaping, irrigation, lighting, artificial turf and pavers. Mandalay Pools & Spas adds outdoor kitchens and fire pits to its custom pools. Sunsation Pools & Spas lists decking, hardscapes and enclosures next to new pool and spa construction.

Which nearby towns have their own pool builder pages?

Seminole, Pinellas Park, Clearwater, St. Petersburg and Dunedin each have their own page in this directory. Builders in Largo often name those towns as places they work, so it is worth reading both your own town's page and the Largo page before you call.
