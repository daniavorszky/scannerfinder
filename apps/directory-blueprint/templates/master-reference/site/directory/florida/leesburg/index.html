Pool builders in Leesburg serve a market that runs well beyond the city line, into The Villages and the counties around Lake County. Nine businesses share this page in Lake County, and two smaller towns, Fruitland Park and Lady Lake, are folded in with Leesburg. Tri County Pool and Spa is based in Fruitland Park and Villages Pool & Spa Inc is based in Lady Lake.

Premier Pool Contractor is a Leesburg business whose website is titled Folker Custom Pools The Villages, so the same company appears under both names. Its site says it designs, builds and remodels pools and spas across Lake, Sumter and Marion counties. Villagers Choice Pool & Spa also names The Villages in its service areas, along with Leesburg, Ocala, Clermont, Wesley Chapel and Citrus County, and it says its main focus is new pool installations with or without a spa. It offers financing through Lyon Financial.

The kinds of pool built here vary from builder to builder. Wiseman Pools describes a concrete shell installed with gunite and builds both residential and commercial pools. Blue Moon Backyard & Pool Inc lists fiberglass pools next to new construction, renovations and resurfacing, spas, screen rooms and cages, landscape design and pavers and tile, and its navigation includes financing, a warranty page and ADA accessibility. Perfect Pools of Central Florida lists new pool and spa construction, remodeling, repairs and equipment upgrades, and its package pages spell out inclusions such as engineering and permitting.

Screen enclosures are worth a word here. Premier Pool Contractor, Blue Moon Backyard & Pool Inc and Perfect Pools of Central Florida all list them. Florida's residential pool safety law says a new residential pool needs at least one safety feature to pass final inspection, and an enclosure that isolates the pool and meets the barrier requirements is one of the options. That barrier is measured on the outside and must reach 4 feet.

Who builds pools in The Villages area from Leesburg?

Premier Pool Contractor, whose site is titled Folker Custom Pools The Villages, says it works across Lake, Sumter and Marion counties. Villagers Choice Pool & Spa lists The Villages among its service areas, and Perfect Pools of Central Florida says it serves the wider region.

Which Leesburg builders offer fiberglass or gunite pools?

Who in Leesburg offers pool financing?

Villagers Choice Pool & Spa says it offers financing through Lyon Financial. Blue Moon Backyard & Pool Inc has a financing prompt in its site navigation, and Premier Pool Contractor and Perfect Pools of Central Florida also mention financing.

Do Leesburg pool builders also do screen enclosures?

Premier Pool Contractor lists screen enclosures with its pool and deck work. Blue Moon Backyard & Pool Inc lists screen rooms and cages, and Perfect Pools of Central Florida offers builds bundled with a screen enclosure.

What does Florida require for a new residential pool's safety?

A new residential pool needs at least one of five safety features to pass final inspection: an enclosure meeting the barrier rules, an approved safety cover, door and window exit alarms, self-closing and self-latching door devices, or a pool alarm. Any builder you speak with should tell you which one they plan to use.
