Pool builders in Lutz are mostly concrete pool builders, and many of them work well beyond the town, across Tampa Bay and into Pasco County. Nine businesses share this page in Hillsborough County, and Temple Terrace is the smaller town that shares it. Lifestyle Pool and Spa is based in Temple Terrace and still serves Lutz homeowners.

Concrete, which the trade here also calls gunite, is what the builders with the longest histories describe. Pool Perfection says it has built more than 1,800 custom concrete pools across Tampa Bay since 2004, with a Largo headquarters and showroom plus a Lutz showroom. Natural Springs Pools, a family-owned Tampa Bay builder with a Lutz office on Whitaker Road, says it started building pools in 1979 and offers concrete as its custom option. Lifestyle Pool and Spa says its leadership has more than 30 years of Central Florida pool building, with a focus on custom concrete pools, and that it is an Elite Pebble Tec Builder. Top Gunn Pools Inc says it has built and remodeled commercial and residential pools for over 35 years.

Service areas run wide. Lifestyle Pool and Spa lists Hillsborough, Pasco, Hernando, Polk, Lake, Sumter, Pinellas and Manatee counties. Pool Authority, whose website goes by Bay Area Pool Authority, covers Tampa Bay and nearby places such as Wesley Chapel, Land O' Lakes, Odessa and Trinity. WhiteWater Pools and Spas Inc. says it has built pools for over 45 years anywhere in Tampa Bay. Waterscapes Pools and Spas lists Tampa and Bradenton among where it does business.

The work is not only new construction. WhiteWater Pools and Spas Inc. describes remodels that include retiling, new interior finishes and pump and filter upgrades. Pool Perfection lists renovation, small and plunge pools, spa additions and what it calls private lagoons. Tropical Escapes Pool Builders, LLC. focuses on swimming pool construction and adds spas, outdoor kitchens, fire pits, and waterfalls and fountains. Top Gunn Pools Inc lists custom spas, lighting, decking, fencing and enclosures, and financing.

Common questions about pool builders in Lutz

Who builds concrete or gunite pools in Lutz?

Natural Springs Pools offers concrete pools, which it also calls gunite, as its custom option. Pool Perfection says it has built more than 1,800 custom concrete pools across Tampa Bay, and Top Gunn Pools Inc and Lifestyle Pool and Spa also describe concrete construction.

Which Lutz builders work in Wesley Chapel, Odessa and Land O' Lakes?

Pool Authority names Wesley Chapel, Land O' Lakes, Odessa and Trinity as areas near Tampa Bay that it covers. Lifestyle Pool and Spa lists Pasco County among its service areas.

Can I get financing for a pool from a Lutz builder?

Tropical Escapes Pool Builders, LLC., Natural Springs Pools, Lifestyle Pool and Spa, Pool Perfection, WhiteWater Pools and Spas Inc. and Top Gunn Pools Inc all mention financing on their sites. Ask each for the terms, which the sites do not spell out.

Who remodels and resurfaces older pools in Lutz?

WhiteWater Pools and Spas Inc. lists remodels with retiling, new interior finishes and pump and filter upgrades. Lifestyle Pool and Spa says it remodels and resurfaces existing pools, and Pool Perfection lists renovation among its services.

Are there builders in Lutz that also do commercial pools?

Top Gunn Pools Inc builds commercial and residential pools, and Pool Authority and WhiteWater Pools and Spas Inc. list commercial work. Waterscapes Pools and Spas says it contracts with large local developers for commercial pools.

Which nearby towns have their own page?

Land O' Lakes, Odessa, Wesley Chapel, Tampa and Oldsmar each have a page. Lifestyle Pool and Spa, based in Temple Terrace, appears on the Lutz page because Temple Terrace does not have its own.
