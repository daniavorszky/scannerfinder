Melbourne pool builders answer to all of Brevard County, and the page reads that way. The 21 pool builders and pool contractors here all sit in Brevard County by ZIP code, and Satellite Beach, Indian Harbour Beach and Indialantic have a few businesses each on this page. Several sites talk about the county as a whole rather than one town, and Paradise Pools & Spas Construction says it has served South Brevard County since 1982 from an Aurora Road office in Melbourne. A homeowner across the causeway on the barrier island and a homeowner inland both find their builders in the same group.

Concrete, fiberglass and vinyl are all on offer, and the sites say so plainly. Aqua Blue Pools, rated 4.4 from 121 reviews, calls itself Brevard's oldest swimming pool contractor and says the Underwood family founded it in the 1960s. Its construction copy describes gunite shells at least six inches thick with steel rebar, and a lifetime warranty on the concrete shell. Intercoastal Pool & Spa, rated 4.0 from 139 reviews, says it has served Brevard and Indian River counties since 1992, builds custom gunite pools inground, and installs fiberglass when that suits the budget or property. Pineapple Pools of Florida, rated 4.5 from 54 reviews, builds concrete, fiberglass or vinyl pools by customer choice and says it focuses on newer homes, condos and businesses.

Martin Pools & Spas, rated 4.0 from 82 reviews, says it has built pools in Brevard County since 1996 and shows steel-reinforced gunite shells in its package descriptions, which come as pool and spa, pool and screen, or pool only. Florida Custom Pools and Spas LLC, rated 4.9 from 31 reviews, describes luxury pools with outdoor kitchens, screen enclosures, water features and fire features. Best Pools of Brevard, Inc., rated 4.2 from 33 reviews, says it has operated under the same owners since 1987 and that its service technician handles warranty repairs and stays available once warranties run out.

Common questions about pool builders in Melbourne

Who builds gunite pools in Melbourne, Florida?

Aqua Blue Pools, rated 4.4 from 121 reviews, describes gunite shells at least six inches thick with steel rebar. Intercoastal Pool & Spa, rated 4.0 from 139 reviews, builds custom gunite pools inground. Martin Pools & Spas, rated 4.0 from 82 reviews, describes a steel-reinforced gunite shell in its packages.

Can I get a fiberglass or vinyl pool in Melbourne?

Yes. Pineapple Pools of Florida says it builds concrete, fiberglass or vinyl pools depending on the customer's choice. Intercoastal Pool & Spa says it installs fiberglass when that suits the budget or property. Florida Coast Above Ground Pools works in vinyl liner and above ground pools.

Who installs above ground pools around Melbourne?

Florida Coast Above Ground Pools, rated 4.3 from 20 reviews, says it specializes in above ground pool installation, liner replacement and repairs. Its menu lists in-stock pools, Atlas pools and pool liners, and it describes semi inground installs in many Florida cities.

Which Melbourne pool builders have a long history in Brevard County?

Aqua Blue Pools says the Underwood family founded it in the 1960s. Paradise Pools & Spas Construction says it has served South Brevard County since 1982. Best Pools of Brevard, Inc. says it has run under the same owners since 1987, and Intercoastal Pool & Spa gives 1992 as its starting year.

Who offers financing for a Melbourne pool?

Aqua Blue Pools has a financing page that names Lyon Financial as a partner. Intercoastal Pool & Spa, Martin Pools & Spas, Paradise Pools & Spas Construction and Florida Custom Pools and Spas LLC also mention financing on their sites.
