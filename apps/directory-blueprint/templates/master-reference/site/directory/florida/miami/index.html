Miami pool builders work in a county with one name on the map, Miami-Dade County, and 69 businesses appear on this page, every one with a Miami-Dade ZIP code. Many of them look past the county line. Malibu Pools Kendall says it serves Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach counties, and Temptation Pools Inc names the same three. Deep Blue Pool & Spa, Inc. says it has served Miami, Fort Lauderdale and Broward County since 1998. A homeowner here is choosing from a trade that treats South Florida as one working area.

Concrete is the material most of the builders here pour. Dream Pools of South Florida describes its build as a rebar frame followed by shotcrete, Designer Pools describes shotcrete construction with reinforced steel, and Clearwater Pools & Patio lists custom concrete pool construction. Where the builders say they work is worth a look before you call. Clearwater Pools & Patio names Pinecrest, Palmetto Bay, Cutler Bay, Coral Gables, Coconut Grove and South Miami. Florida law sets out a residential pool and spa contractor, a commercial pool and spa contractor and a separate servicing contractor, and the servicing class does not cover building a new pool. Dream Pools of South Florida says on its site that it is a licensed Certified Pool and Spa Contractor. Swimming Pools of Florida, Inc. says owner Manuel Hernandez holds a Certified Pool/Spa Contractor license and has overseen 687 permitted builds, and it holds 4.4 from 118 reviews. A license shown on a website is the business's own statement, so ask which class it holds.

Common questions about pool builders in Miami

Who builds fiberglass pools in Miami?

iGUi Pools describes itself as a supplier and installer of fiberglass pools in South Florida, with a showroom of more than 8,000 square feet. Malibu Pools Kendall lists fiberglass along with gunite and vinyl liner pools. Most other builders on this page pour concrete, so fiberglass is worth asking about by name.

Which Miami pool companies also work in Broward County?

Designer Pools builds in Miami and Broward County and has pages for Fort Lauderdale, Southwest Ranches, Parkland and Las Olas. Deep Blue Pool & Spa says it has served Miami, Fort Lauderdale and Broward County since 1998. Malibu Pools Kendall adds Palm Beach County to that reach.

Who handles pool remodeling and resurfacing in Miami?

Blue Star Pool Construction & Services lists remodeling with resurfacing, tiling, decking and lighting. Pools by GE Corp says it works on renovations of existing pools and lists equipment upgrades. Bluepointe Pools and Spas offers remodeling, resurfacing and automation. Ask each what a remodel covers before you compare quotes.

Is there a Miami pool builder that also does pavers and outdoor kitchens?

Yes. Dream Pools of South Florida builds pools, decks, pergolas and outdoor kitchens. Outdoor Patio Designs presents custom pools, outdoor kitchen islands and pergolas as its main services. Cricket Pavers Outdoor Living of Miami covers driveways, patios, pergolas and outdoor kitchens, along with pools.

Which nearby towns have their own pool builder page?

Coral Gables, Doral, Homestead, Hialeah and Miramar each have a page on this site. Homestead and Hialeah sit on the south and west sides of Miami-Dade County, and Miramar is across the line in Broward County. Some builders here, such as Pool Construction, Inc., list Coral Gables, Hialeah and Homestead as places they work.

What do the Florida license classes mean for a pool builder?

State law names a residential pool and spa contractor, a commercial pool and spa contractor, and a servicing contractor. Servicing covers repair and equipment work, not building a new pool. When a Miami builder states a license number on its site, ask which class it is.
