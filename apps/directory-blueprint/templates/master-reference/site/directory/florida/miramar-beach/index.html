Common questions about pool builders in Miramar Beach

Who builds gunite pools in Miramar Beach?

Tamtech Pool & Spa says on its site that it offers new pool and spa design for gunite construction, and it also lists commercial pools and water features. Its rating is 4.4 from 31 reviews. Ask any builder you call which shell type it would use for your yard.

Which builders work outside Miramar Beach itself?

Custom Pools by Rob Abel names Destin, 30A, Santa Rosa Beach, Fort Walton Beach and Niceville, plus the rest of Walton and Okaloosa counties. Tamtech Pool & Spa describes work along the Florida Panhandle and the Emerald Coast. Confirm that a builder travels to your address before you book a design visit.

Can I find someone in Miramar Beach who remodels or replasters an existing pool?

Yes. Tamtech Pool & Spa says it renovates and replasters pools, Custom Pools by Rob Abel lists remodeling and resurfacing, and King of the Sea Pool Service, LLC covers plaster and tile along with repair and remodel work. King of the Sea's rating is 4.8 from 22 reviews.
