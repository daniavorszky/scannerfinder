Florida law splits pool contractors into three scopes: a commercial pool and spa contractor, a residential pool and spa contractor, and a servicing contractor who repairs and refinishes but does not build new pools. Accro General Contractors and Andelson Construction both state a contractor license on their sites, and Essig Pools & Pavers does too. Which scope a license covers matters here, because so many of the businesses in Miramar do commercial work next to residential. The state runs a public license search at myfloridalicense.com. A license shown on a website is a claim to check there, not something confirmed on this page.

Common questions about pool builders in Miramar

Who builds gunite pools in Miramar?

Essig Pools & Pavers, Shoreline Swimming Pool Contractor and Accro General Contractors all list concrete or gunite pool work on their sites. Essig describes concrete pools and spas, Shoreline has a dedicated gunite section, and Accro publishes blog posts on gunite pool construction steps.

Does anyone in Miramar build vinyl liner pools?

Shoreline Swimming Pool Contractor has a section on liner pools alongside its gunite work. The rest of the builders here describe concrete construction, so liner is the less common choice on this page.

Which Miramar businesses do commercial pool work?

Andelson Construction, a Miramar general contractor, lists new commercial pool builds, renovations and recertification work. Essig Pools & Pavers and Dillon Pools Inc also describe commercial pool construction, and Dillon mentions competition and Olympic size pools.

Do Miramar pool builders also remodel older pools?

Yes. Shoreline Swimming Pool Contractor lists new tile and coping, decking, pumps, lighting and spa additions. Accro General Contractors says it restores existing pools, and Essig Pools & Pavers lists renovations along with pavers and driveways.

Which nearby cities have their own page?

Pembroke Pines, North Lauderdale, Hialeah, Davie and Hollywood each have a page. Builders that name Hollywood, Weston or Coral Springs as service areas, such as Accro General Contractors and Dillon Pools Inc, may cover those towns too.

What are the three kinds of pool contractor license in Florida?

Florida law defines a commercial pool and spa contractor, a residential pool and spa contractor, and a servicing contractor. The servicing scope covers repair and refinishing but not building a new pool. The public search is at myfloridalicense.com.
