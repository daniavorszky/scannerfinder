Naples pool builders work almost entirely inside Collier County, and 55 businesses appear on this page, with Marco Island and Ave Maria adding 2 each. The trade also looks north into Lee County. Edgewater Pools & Spas runs its main office in Naples, works across Collier and Lee counties, and has location pages for towns from Marco Island to Fort Myers. Suncoast Custom Pools says it has built pools in Naples since 1983 and serves Bonita Springs, Fort Myers and the rest of Collier and Lee counties.

Some of the oldest names in town describe custom work for Naples families. Slusser Pools says it has built custom pools and outdoor spaces since 1980, with travertine decking, spa upgrades and renovation. Classic Pools of Naples works out of an office on Tamiami Trail East and says it has spent over 25 years building pools. Summit Pools, Inc. describes a build with 3D renderings, steel reinforcement and a gunite shell. Pools by Wyatt says custom concrete pools are a popular choice locally and mentions a pebble finish. It also serves Bonita Springs, Marco Island, Estero and Fort Myers.

Fiberglass and vinyl are offered, but by fewer companies. Pool Builders Naples says it builds both concrete and fiberglass pools, either pouring and shaping concrete or setting a prefabricated shell, depending on what the client picks. Pool & Deck Concepts lists fiberglass pools beside resurfacing, screen enclosures and fencing. Premier Pools and Spas of Naples lists gunite, fiberglass and vinyl pools. Homes here often carry a screened lanai. Pool and Patio Works offers pool cage construction and says it transforms outdated lanais, and Knaak Design Group treats the pool, spa, deck, plantings, lighting and screen enclosure as one project.

Who builds concrete pools in Naples?

Summit Pools, Inc. describes steel reinforcement and a gunite shell. Pools by Wyatt says custom concrete pools are a popular choice locally. RL Poolscapes lists custom gunite pools with infinity edges. Pool Builders Naples builds concrete and fiberglass pools.

Which Naples companies build or replace pool cages and lanai screening?

Pool and Patio Works lists high-end pool cage construction and says it transforms outdated lanais. Pool & Deck Concepts lists screen enclosures and fencing. Knaak Design Group includes the screen enclosure in a whole-project design. Pools by Wyatt also lists screen enclosures.

Who builds pools on Marco Island?

Calusa Pool Company is based on Marco Island and covers new construction and renovations. Dun-Rite Pools Inc is a family-owned company on the island that says it has served it for more than 30 years. RL Poolscapes and Pools by Wyatt both name Marco Island as part of their work.

Are there Naples pool builders that do commercial projects?

Classic Pools of Naples lists new commercial pools and says it works with developers, hotels and resorts. Pinnacle USA, Inc. says it serves hospitality, condominiums and water parks. Park Shore Pools mentions resorts and large-scale renovation.

Which Naples pool companies also cover Fort Myers and Bonita Springs?
