Fiberglass is the first thing to know about pool building around New Port Richey. Several of the pool builders on this page install or sell fiberglass shells, and the trade here works out of Pasco County toward the Tampa Bay area and Hernando County. All eleven businesses sit in Pasco County. Two are based in Port Richey, two in Trinity and one in Holiday, so the page covers several nearby communities rather than one town.

Hawaiian Island Pools is the clearest example. Its site describes a veteran-owned, family-run company that installs inground fiberglass pools across the Tampa Bay area, and it credits itself with more than 30 years in business and over 1,200 pools built. It lists a long run of named pool models and three spa and spillover designs, and it builds paver decks and screen enclosures. It has a 4.8 rating from 102 reviews. Pettit Pools of Port Richey calls itself both a fiberglass pool manufacturer and a licensed residential and commercial pool contractor, with pools made in Tampa and sold factory direct under one warranty. It also installs concrete pools for customers who want larger sizes or shapes. Its rating is 3.8 from 91 reviews.

Concrete and gunite work sits alongside it. Certified Pool Renovations, based in Holiday, says it specializes in custom concrete and gunite pool construction through a design-build process, with add-on spas, water features and custom shapes, plus remodeling, resurfacing, tile, coping and decking. It has a 4.9 rating from 32 reviews. National Pools of Pasco, based in Port Richey, builds residential and commercial pools around the Tampa Bay area and says it has 50 years or more in the business. Its construction page walks through a gunite shell, plumbing, tile and coping, a deck finished with pavers or another surface, and an optional screen enclosure. It has a 3.3 rating from 21 reviews.

Piccs Designs Pool & Spa is based in New Port Richey and serves a long list of Tampa Bay and Hernando County communities. It presents itself as a boutique custom builder run by a friends-and-family team with generations of construction experience, and it handles pools of all shapes, hot tubs, waterfalls, fire features and custom patio finishes, along with remodels such as resurfacing and tile updates. It lists financing and has a 4.7 rating from 77 reviews.

Homeowners who compare builders here will run into license numbers on websites. Under Florida law, the pool and spa contractor classes are commercial, residential and servicing, and only the first two cover building a new pool. Certified Pool Renovations shows license number CPC 1459640 on its site, a CPC prefix that marks a pool and spa contractor. Templar Contracting, a Trinity remodeling and custom home contractor rated 4.6 from 54 reviews, shows CBC1255382, a building contractor prefix. A number on a website is only a statement, and the state's public license search at myfloridalicense.com is where a homeowner checks it.

Some other New Port Richey businesses are filed as swimming pool contractors on Google Maps and lean toward service work. The Pool Sweepers is a weekly pool service company with a 4.6 rating from 57 reviews. A & W Pool Service, in Trinity, has 3.7 from 6 reviews. Coastal Pools & Spas Inc has a 2.8 rating from 24 reviews, with 10 five-star and 12 one-star ratings. Common questions about pool builders in New Port Richey

Who installs fiberglass pools near New Port Richey?

Hawaiian Island Pools says fiberglass is its focus and lists named pool models and spillover spa designs. Pettit Pools of Port Richey says it manufactures fiberglass pools in Tampa and sells them factory direct.

Which New Port Richey builders do gunite or concrete pools?

Certified Pool Renovations in Holiday specializes in custom concrete and gunite pools through a design-build process. National Pools of Pasco describes a gunite shell built with plumbing, tile, coping and a paver deck, and Pettit Pools installs concrete pools for larger sizes.

Can I get a screen enclosure with a new pool in New Port Richey?

Hawaiian Island Pools builds screen enclosures along with its paver decks, and National Pools of Pasco lists an optional screen enclosure over the pool and deck. Ask each what the enclosure covers before you sign.

Who serves Hernando County from New Port Richey?

Piccs Designs Pool & Spa says it serves a long list of Tampa Bay and Hernando County communities from its New Port Richey base. Other companies here name Port Richey, Trinity and Tampa as places they work.

What do CPC and CBC on a license number mean?

CPC marks a pool and spa contractor and CBC marks a building contractor. Certified Pool Renovations shows a CPC number on its site and Templar Contracting shows a CBC number. Check any number at myfloridalicense.com.

Which nearby towns have their own pool builder page?
