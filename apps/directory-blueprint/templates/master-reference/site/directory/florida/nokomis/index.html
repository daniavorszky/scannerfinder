Gulf Breeze Enterprises By J & J, Llc takes a different path. Its short site centers on pool and deck renovation, new surfaces, brick pavers on decks and driveways, and equipment installation, and it says it takes on complex renovations that other companies avoid. It states that it is a certified pool contractor in Florida and shows license CPC1456510. Under Florida law a certified contractor holds a state certificate of competency and may contract anywhere in the state, and the CPC prefix marks a pool and spa license. That is a claim on the company's own site, and the license itself can be checked at myfloridalicense.com. Its rating is 4.1 from 8 reviews.

Common questions about pool builders in Nokomis

Who builds gunite pools around Nokomis?

Swim Incorporated says its pools are gunite and shows a 3D design before commitment, with a rating of 4.3 from 26 reviews. Aquatech Pools GC lists shotcrete among its subcontracted trades and has 4.5 from 279 reviews. Lucas Lagoons builds custom luxury pools and spas.

Do the Nokomis pool builders only work in Nokomis?

No. Aquatech Pools GC and Swim Incorporated both describe their work in terms of Sarasota, and Amazing pools and pavers inc is based in Osprey. Ask each builder whether your address falls inside the area it covers.

Who handles pool renovation and deck work in Nokomis?

Gulf Breeze Enterprises By J & J, Llc centers its site on pool and deck renovation, new surfaces and brick pavers. Aquatech Pools GC lists remodeling and pavers as well, and Swim Incorporated lists remodeling and resurfacing.

What does a CPC license number mean on a builder's website?

CPC is the prefix Florida uses for pool and spa contractor licenses. Gulf Breeze Enterprises By J & J, Llc shows CPC1456510 on its site. Certified contractors may work anywhere in Florida, while registered ones work only where they have met local requirements. Look up any number at myfloridalicense.com before you hire.

Which towns near Nokomis have their own directory page?

Venice, Englewood, Sarasota, North Port and Lakewood Ranch each have a page. Osprey does not, so Amazing pools and pavers inc appears here.
