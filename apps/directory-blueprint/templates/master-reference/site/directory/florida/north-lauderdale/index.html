Florida law separates pool contractors into three scopes: commercial pool and spa contractors, residential pool and spa contractors, and servicing contractors, who repair and service pools but do not build a new one. License numbers that begin with CPC belong to the pool and spa class. Because much of the work in North Lauderdale is repair and service, it is worth knowing which scope a business holds before you ask it to build.
