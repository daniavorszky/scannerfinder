Common questions about pool builders in North Port

Who builds concrete or shotcrete pools in North Port?

Messina Pools says it builds custom concrete pools. Chosen Pool and Spa describes a shotcrete build from excavation and steel reinforcement through tile, decking and startup. Gilley's Custom Pools Inc. lists gunite among the materials it works in.

Which North Port builders install fiberglass pools?

Gilley's Custom Pools Inc. covers custom inground pools in both gunite and fiberglass. It designs and builds in North Port, Port Charlotte, Venice and Englewood, so it is the clearest choice on the page for fiberglass.

Why are there two Gilley's businesses on the North Port page?

Gilley's Custom Pools Inc. is the pool company. Gilley's Custom Home Builders is a residential builder serving North Port, Port Charlotte, Punta Gorda and South Gulf Cove, whose site points to a pool builders division and a Custom Pools page. Each has its own reviews and rating.

Who adds screen enclosures or outdoor kitchens in North Port?

Messina Pools lists screen enclosures, outdoor kitchens and stone pavers as add-ons to its concrete pools. Gilley's Custom Pools Inc. lists screen enclosures too, and Chosen Pool and Spa lists outdoor kitchens and marble decking.

Do North Port builders work in Port Charlotte, Venice and Punta Gorda?

Yes. Gilley's Custom Pools Inc. names Port Charlotte, Venice and Englewood. Gilley's Custom Home Builders names Port Charlotte and Punta Gorda. Chosen Pool and Spa names Sarasota, Lakewood Ranch and Longboat Key, and each of those nearby towns has a page of its own.

Which North Port businesses handle service and repair rather than new pools?

Daniels Pool Service and Repairs describes maintenance, equipment repair and restoration work and does not say it builds new pools. Classic Pool Services and Sparkle Pool Maintenance Co are also filed as service names. Messina Pools handles repairs and remodels as well as new builds.
