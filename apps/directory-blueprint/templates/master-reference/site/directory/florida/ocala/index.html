Florida law separates pool contractors into three kinds. Commercial and residential pool and spa contractors may build, repair and service pools, while a swimming pool and spa servicing contractor may repair and service them, including equipment repair and interior refinishing, but does not build new pools. Yandle's Splash Time Pools says on its site that both owners hold a Florida swimming pool contractor license.

What is the difference between a pool contractor and a servicing contractor in Florida?

Florida defines commercial and residential pool and spa contractors, who may build, repair and service pools, and swimming pool and spa servicing contractors, who repair and service them but do not build new pools. Ask any Ocala company to state the license class that covers your job.

Which Ocala pool builders say they use shotcrete?

Koah Pools says its own shotcrete crews form the pool shell. Yandle's Splash Time Pools describes the shotcrete step in its building process, and Agua Pools Central Florida describes gunite or shotcrete application handled by one in-house team.
