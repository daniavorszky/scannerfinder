Common questions about pool builders in Oldsmar

Who builds concrete pools in Oldsmar?

Gulfstream Pools & Spas builds custom inground concrete pools and spas from an Oldsmar office and showroom. Its site says it has built more than 9,000 pools since 1992. Freestyle Pools & Spas, on Burbank Road, also builds custom pools and lists spas, decks and remodeling. Ask each what the shell is made of before you compare quotes.

Which Oldsmar pool builders also put up screen enclosures?

Five Star Pool lists screen enclosures alongside new builds and renovations. Freestyle Pools & Spas describes screen enclosures among its services too, and Gulfstream Pools & Spas covers them as well. If you want the pool and the cage from one company, ask whether the enclosure is built by their own crew.

Who does resurfacing and pool finishes near Oldsmar?

Southwest Pool Finishers Inc focuses on plastering and finishes. Five Star Pool describes resurfacing with pebble and quartz finishes, and Dig It Pools LLC lists resurfacing among its main lines. Freestyle Pools & Spas shows pebble interiors and salt systems in its renovation projects.

Do Oldsmar pool builders work in Tampa and Pasco County?

Yes, their own sites describe wide areas. Dig It Pools LLC covers north Pinellas, west Hillsborough and Pasco counties. Gulfstream Pools & Spas serves Hillsborough, Pinellas and Pasco. Five Star Pool and Freestyle Pools & Spas both describe work across Tampa Bay.

Is financing offered by an Oldsmar pool builder?

Gulfstream Pools & Spas is the Oldsmar business whose site points to financing. It also states a contractor license and a warranty. Ask any builder to put the terms of financing and the warranty in writing before you sign.

Which nearby cities have their own pool builder pages?

Dunedin, Palm Harbor, Clearwater, Odessa and Tampa each have a page in this directory. Clearwater and Palm Harbor are also in Pinellas County, so they are the nearest towns to check if you want more choice than Oldsmar offers.
