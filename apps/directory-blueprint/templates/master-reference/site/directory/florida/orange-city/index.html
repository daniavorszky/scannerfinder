Pool builders in Orange City are spread across the west side of Volusia County, and the page reflects that: the businesses are as likely to be based in DeBary, Deltona or Lake Helen as in Orange City itself. Nine businesses share this page, all in Volusia County, and those three smaller towns are folded in with Orange City. Southern Screen LLC and Tropicana Pools are based in Deltona, Aquaworks Pool Service & Repair Inc. and Oak Wells Aquatics are in DeBary, and CollinsBuilt Pools is in Lake Helen.

The town has one business that sells pools as kits. POOLS FOR LESS is based in Orange City and describes itself as a nationwide fiberglass swimming pool dealer. Its site sells fiberglass pool kits in small, medium and large sizes and stresses do-it-yourself installation, with instructions that include dig specifications. It also has a pool installation page. That is a different route to a pool from hiring a contractor, and a homeowner should treat it as one.

The contractors here run from family shops to builders with commercial reach. CollinsBuilt Pools is a family-owned custom pool and spa builder whose site says the owners are present throughout the build. Aquaworks Pool Service & Repair Inc. has a design showroom on Charles Richard Beall Blvd in DeBary, and it says its founder, Ron, and his team have more than 30 years of experience in remodeling, service, repair, leak detection and weekly cleaning as well as new pools. Oak Wells Aquatics has a DeBary location on Highbanks Road and says it has built residential and commercial pools and spas since 2014, with commercial work that includes competition pools, hotel and resort pools and interactive water features.

Southern Screen LLC now trades on its site as Southern Construction. It describes itself as a local swimming pool specialist since 2005 that builds concrete, fiberglass and bio pools, and its menu adds spas, pool deck resurfacing, pavers, decorative concrete, custom decks, sunrooms and screen enclosures. It says it serves Volusia, Seminole and Orange counties. Artesian Pools of East Florida works out of offices in Orlando and Daytona Beach and says it has built custom inground gunite pools since 1976. Its site says both offices build new pools and that major renovations and resurfacing are handled only through the Daytona Beach office.
