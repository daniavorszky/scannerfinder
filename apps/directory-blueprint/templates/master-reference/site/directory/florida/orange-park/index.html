Pool builders in Orange Park work the Clay County side of the Jacksonville area, and the 8 businesses on this page are split between full pool construction and stores that sell pool products. Six are in Clay County, and the rest reach out to Middleburg, Fleming Island and Macclenny in Baker County. Jacksonville is the city the local websites name most often as a place they work, alongside Clay County itself, Fleming Island, Middleburg and Macclenny.

The construction side of this market is concrete and vinyl. Crystal Pools And Spas Of North Florida Inc serves the Orange Park area and says it builds custom inground concrete and inground vinyl pools using CAD and 3-D design. It has pages for new construction, renovation and liner replacement, and it cites more than 20 years in one place and 25 years in another.

Stanley Pools is a family pool company serving Northeast Florida, with an Orange Park listing and a Jacksonville history on its site. The site says full swimming pool construction began in the early 1990s after the father joined his sons. It lists screen enclosures and paver decking next to pool construction and remodeling. Its rating is 4.8 from 22 reviews.

W T Construction, Inc. comes at pools from the home building side. It is a Middleburg builder of custom homes and pools serving Clay County and Northeast Florida, and its site says it specializes in new home construction and inground pool construction. Pool options it names are tanning shelves, spillover spas, custom paver decking, water features and upgraded lighting. Its rating is 4.7 from 14 reviews, and its site lists financing.
