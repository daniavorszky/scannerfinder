This page covers 46 pool builders and pool companies in Orlando, and every one of them is in Orange County. The local trade is mostly concrete pool construction, with fiberglass installers, a vinyl liner specialist and a group of remodelers working alongside it. Some are Orlando firms with decades behind them, some are the local office of a larger Florida company, and a few are general contractors that added a pool division. The businesses below say they work in Orlando and, in many cases, across Central Florida and beyond.

Gunite and concrete construction is where much of the local trade sits. Grunit Pool Contractors describes itself as an engineer-owned design and build firm and says each gunite shell is steel-reinforced. Bowles Custom Pools & Spas says it has served Central Florida since 1988, and its site describes spraying gunite to form the shell, then adding screen enclosures and super gutters. Leron Homes & Pools builds inground concrete pools and new homes. Larry Bowles Pools, which appears on its site as Larry Bowles Inc., walks through construction from excavation and steel forming to pouring, decking and coping. Dunota Pools offers pool construction and service in Orlando and central Florida.

Fiberglass and vinyl liner work is a smaller part of the picture, but it is here. Fiberglass Pools Outlet sells and installs fiberglass pools and says its pools carry a 15-year warranty, with screen enclosures, fences and decks handled by the same in-house team. InstaPools Florida LLC sells and builds fiberglass pools out of Orlando and shows a lineup of named models sorted into small, medium and large. The Pool Guy USA is a fiberglass pool installer that says it serves all of Florida. For vinyl liner, Pacific Pools of Orlando says it has been established since 1986 and both builds vinyl liner pools and replaces liners in existing ones. Custom Orlando Pools lists gunite or vinyl shell options among its services.

Where a builder works matters as much as what it builds. Custom Orlando Pools builds pools in Orlando, Winter Park, Windermere and Lake Nona. Southern Pool Designs works from Orlando with a focus on Lake Nona and St. Cloud. Belair, an air conditioning and general contracting company with a pool division, names Orlando, Kissimmee and St. Cloud as its service areas. D&J The Pool Resurfacers works in Orlando, nearby areas and Florida's east coast. Dunota Pools also has pages for Tampa, Jacksonville, Lakeland, St. Augustine, Clearwater and St. Petersburg, so some builders here reach across the state. It is worth asking any of them which crew would work at your address.

Common questions about pool builders in Orlando

Who builds gunite or concrete pools in Orlando?

Grunit Pool Contractors is an engineer-owned design and build firm that says each gunite shell is steel-reinforced. Bowles Custom Pools & Spas says its crew sprays gunite to form the shell. Leron Homes & Pools builds inground concrete pools and also builds new homes.

Which Orlando businesses install fiberglass pools?

Fiberglass Pools Outlet sells and installs fiberglass pools and says they carry a 15-year warranty. InstaPools Florida LLC shows named models in small, medium and large sizes and says the shells are made in the USA. The Pool Guy USA says it serves all of Florida.

Who works on older pools in Orlando?

Simple To Sensational Pools, Inc. says renovations of older pools are part of its work. Image Pools Florida lists renovations as its own section and says it has served Central Florida since 2000. D&J The Pool Resurfacers specializes in resurfacing and custom remodeling.

Who builds pools in Lake Nona?

Southern Pool Designs builds luxury custom pools from Orlando with a focus on Lake Nona and St. Cloud. Custom Orlando Pools names Lake Nona alongside Orlando, Winter Park and Windermere as places where it builds.

Which Orlando builders take commercial pool projects?

Leron Homes & Pools says its commercial work includes hotels, resorts, theme parks and community developments. American Pools & Spas, a Cody Pools company, lists residential and commercial construction. Grunit Pool Contractors lists residential, commercial and luxury pool work.

Which towns near Orlando have their own pool builder pages?
