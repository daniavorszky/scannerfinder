Ormond Beach is a market of full-service pool builders that lay out the whole job, from design and permits to the deck and the screen enclosure. All eleven businesses on this page sit in Volusia County, and most of the ones that build pools describe the same package: a gunite or fiberglass shell, a spa, pavers, and a cage over the top. The pool builders here also tend to say they cover a wide stretch of the coast, from Flagler County into St. Johns County and Seminole County.

C M Custom Pool Designs is a family owned company serving Volusia and Flagler counties, within roughly a 50 minute radius of its shop. Its site says owner Coby Moore has worked in the pool industry since 1994, and it lists custom inground construction, renovations and repairs, pool decks with pavers and other finishes, new screen enclosures, hot tubs and monthly maintenance. It has a 4.6 rating from 193 reviews. Affordable Pools builds inground gunite pools for homeowners in Volusia, Flagler, St. Johns and Seminole counties, and its site says the team has more than three decades of experience and handles design, permits, decking, water features and equipment. It has a perfect 5.0 rating from 49 reviews.

East Coast Pools & Spas builds from Ormond Beach for East Central Florida and says it is now serving St. Augustine too. Its site describes owner Tom Gilbert as having more than 24 years in the pool industry and says the company has been a News Journal Readers' Choice pick each year since 2014. It has a 4.5 rating from 68 reviews. Extreme Pools is the custom pool division of Extreme Alterations Inc. and shows license CPC1458462 on its site. It describes itself as serving Daytona Beach, Palm Coast, Orlando, St. Augustine and Jacksonville, and it has a 3.5 rating from 11 reviews.

Screen enclosures are part of the local package, and a Florida law bears on them. A new residential pool has to have at least one safety feature to pass final inspection: an enclosure that meets the barrier requirements, an approved safety cover, door and window exit alarms, self-closing and self-latching devices on doors, or a pool alarm. The law sets the barrier's outside height at 4 feet or higher. The builders that list screen enclosures here, including C M Custom Pool Designs, Affordable Pools, East Coast Pools & Spas and Serenity Waterscapes Pools | Spas, can build that barrier as part of the job.

Common questions about pool builders in Ormond Beach

Who builds gunite pools in Ormond Beach?

Affordable Pools builds inground gunite pools for Volusia, Flagler, St. Johns and Seminole counties. Serenity Waterscapes Pools | Spas and Blue Haven Pools & Spas both describe gunite construction, and Simply Elegant Pools and Spa LLC lists steel and gunite among its steps.

Is there a fiberglass pool dealer in the Ormond Beach area?

Which Ormond Beach builders will travel to St. Augustine or Palm Coast?

C M Custom Pool Designs lists renovations and repairs. Blue Haven Pools & Spas lists resurfacing and equipment upgrades, and Above Code Pools Spas and Lagoons has customer reviews on its site about an older pool remodeled and new decking.
