Common questions about pool builders in Pace

Who installs vinyl liner pools around Pace?

Robert's Pools says it has built vinyl and gunite in-ground pools since 1979 and installs vinyl liners. Tropical Sun Pools lists vinyl liner replacement, and E Z Cool's Pool & Yardscape lists liner replacement and installs above ground, semi-inground and fully inground pools.

Is there a fiberglass pool installer in Pace?

Tropical Sun Pools builds inground fiberglass pools for the Panhandle. Its site sorts the models into compact, medium and large, in rectangle, Roman, kidney and pool and spa combo designs. Robert's Pools works mainly in gunite and vinyl liner.

Which Pace builders serve Milton and Crestview?

Tropical Sun Pools says it serves Pensacola, Pace, Milton and Crestview. Medallion Pools of Florida has a Milton office. Robert's Pools is in Pace and has built pools in the area since the 1970s, according to its site.

Does a new pool in Pace need a safety feature?

Yes. Florida law says a new residential pool needs at least one approved safety feature, such as an enclosure, a safety cover, door alarms, self-latching doors or a pool alarm, to pass final inspection. E Z Cool's Pool & Yardscape lists safety fencing among its services.

Who offers financing for pools in Pace?

Tropical Sun Pools and Medallion Pools of Florida both list financing. Robert's Pools states a contractor license. Ask any of them for the financing terms in writing.

Can a Pace builder also do patios and pavers?
