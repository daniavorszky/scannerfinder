Common questions about pool builders in Palm Beach Gardens

Who builds concrete pools in Palm Beach Gardens?

Sapphire Pools and Spas builds concrete pools and spas, working from a West Palm Beach address. Its site says it covers Palm Beach, Broward, Martin and St. Lucie counties, so ask whether it takes projects in Palm Beach Gardens. It has 5.0 from 6 reviews on Google.

Which pool builders near Palm Beach Gardens take commercial projects?

Spado Pool Construction, based in Lake Park, names hotels, resorts and recreational facilities among the commercial pools it has built. Sapphire Pools and Spas also lists commercial work. Pool Troopers - West Palm Beach serves commercial customers too, but for cleaning and repair, not construction.

Can I get financing for a pool from a Palm Beach Gardens builder?

Paradise Swimming Pools & Spas, Inc. and Sapphire Pools and Spas both list financing on their sites. Ask each for the terms in writing before you sign anything, since the sites do not spell them out.

Who does pool decks and pavers around Palm Beach Gardens?

GP&C Pools lists pavers and coping and says it installs durable pavers. Spado Pool Construction lists patio and pool decks. Sapphire Pools and Spas lists paver decks and driveways alongside its pool work.

Do any Palm Beach Gardens builders work in Jupiter, Tequesta or Stuart?

Paradise Swimming Pools & Spas, Inc. says it builds custom pools in Palm Beach, Martin and St. Lucie counties and names Jupiter, Tequesta and Stuart. Sapphire Pools and Spas says it covers Palm Beach, Broward, Martin and St. Lucie counties. Jupiter also has its own page.

Which towns share the Palm Beach Gardens page?
