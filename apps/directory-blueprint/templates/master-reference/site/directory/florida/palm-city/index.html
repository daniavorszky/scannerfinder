Who does pool remodeling and refinishing in Palm City?

Pool Specialist, Inc. lists remodeling, and Palm City Pool & Spa describes pool refinishing and repairs. Both say they serve commercial customers as well. Ask each which finishes they install and how long the finish is warranted.

Is there a family owned pool company in Palm City?

Pool Specialist, Inc. says it is family owned and operated and has 39 years in the pool industry. Palm City Pool & Spa also calls itself a family owned business and says it has operated for over 35 years.
