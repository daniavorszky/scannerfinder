Pool builders in Palm Coast are almost all doing the same job: building new pools in Flagler County and reaching into the two counties on either side. That three-county circuit, with Palm Coast in the middle, is the defining feature of this page.

Florida's residential pool safety law requires a new residential pool to have at least one listed safety feature to pass final inspection. The choices include an enclosure that meets the barrier rules, an approved safety cover, door and window alarms, self-closing and self-latching devices, or a pool alarm. Pinch A Penny Pool Patio Spa, Utopia Custom Pools & Spas Inc. and Palm Coast Pools llc all list screen enclosures, so a homeowner can ask which safety feature the quote is built around.
