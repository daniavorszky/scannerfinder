Pool builders in Palm Harbor work in northern Pinellas County, close enough to Tarpon Springs, Dunedin and Clearwater that their service areas overlap all of them. All 8 pool businesses in town are in Pinellas County, one based over in Tarpon Springs. The towns their own websites name most often are Dunedin and Tampa, with Tarpon Springs, St. Petersburg, Clearwater, Sarasota and Orlando also named. This is a market where the trade is as much remodeling as new construction.
