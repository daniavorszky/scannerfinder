Homeowners looking for pool builders in Palmetto will find 14 businesses on this page, and every one of them has a ZIP code in Manatee County. Most have a Palmetto address. The custom pool trade here leans toward concrete. Coastal Creations Pools and Lagoons, LLC lists a Palmetto address and says on its site that it works in Manatee, Sarasota and a long string of nearby counties.
