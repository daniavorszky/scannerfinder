Pool builders in Panama City Beach work in Bay County on the Panhandle, and the businesses here divide between residential concrete pools and commercial jobs for resorts. Seven of the 8 are in Bay County and one is across the line in Washington County, with businesses based in Panama City, Southport, Lynn Haven and Ebro. Their own websites name Panama City more than any other town, with Santa Rosa Beach, Destin, Freeport, Crestview, Lynn Haven and Ebro also named.

The oldest name is Cox Pools, whose site says it has served Northwest Florida since 1958 and is now in its third generation. The site describes engineered gunite shells, residential and commercial pools, resort, HOA and municipal work, and structural changes such as attached spas. It says plaster, paver decking and warranty service are kept in house. Its rating is 4.3 from 190 reviews. Cox Building Corporation is listed separately on Google Maps, with a 4.9 rating from 11 reviews, and points to a website branded Cox Pools.

Waterclear Pools is a Panama City Beach pool construction company that also lists pool service. Its site shows Florida license CPC1459723 and says it is one of only a few licensed commercial pool construction contractors between Fort Walton and Panama City. It takes residential construction as well and says it will quote new pools across northwest Florida. Its rating is 3.8 from 64 reviews.

Palm Bay Pools LLC builds and renovates pools from its base in Southport. Its site says its founder, James, has over 30 years of experience in every type of concrete pool construction, and describes designing custom pool and spa projects with clients after a consultation. Menu pages cover designs, renovations and construction, and a financing page points to Lyon Financial. Its rating is 4.8 from 71 reviews.
