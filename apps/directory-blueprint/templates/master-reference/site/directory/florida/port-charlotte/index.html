Who builds concrete pools in Port Charlotte?

Who restores or resurfaces pools in Port Charlotte?

Resurrection Pools, rated 4.6 from 215 reviews, focuses on pool restoration with resurfacing, tile and leak detection. Unique Stone USA, rated 4.6 from 56 reviews, began in concrete resurfacing and says it has resurfaced hundreds of pools. Does the builder pull the permit for a Port Charlotte pool?

SFC Pool Corp says permitting is handled for its customers. Fulton Pools Inc lists obtaining permits as part of its services, and Custom Pools by Precision describes permits as a step between design and the start of the build. Florida law requires a permit from the local building department before a new pool is built.
