Pool builders in Port Orange are a group of five, all with Volusia County addresses, and the work they describe is wider than the name of the trade suggests. Concrete and fiberglass pools sit beside spas, pavers and decking, remodels, and in one case a whole home design and remodeling business with an outdoor side. This page introduces the five and describes what each one says it does.

Concrete is the material most of the local trade describes. East Coast Pools-Licensed builds in gunite and fiberglass, and its website presents it as a family business run by owner Tom Gilbert, who is credited on the site with over 24 years in the pool industry. Aquascapes walks through its build steps on its own site, including gunite application and decking. Prime Pool Builders Port Orange lists gunite and shotcrete alongside fiberglass installation and vinyl liner installation, which makes it the only business in town that names all three shell types.

Cypress Creek - Outdoor Living Company is listed on Google Maps as a swimming pool contractor, but its website mostly presents a home design and remodeling business, with an indoor side for kitchens, baths and closets and a separate outdoor side. That outdoor side covers pool and spa construction, custom new pool builds, pool renovation, paver installation, outdoor kitchens, fireplaces and firepits. It shows 4.5 from 45 reviews, and East Coast Pools-Licensed shows 4.2 from 15.

Aqua Clear Pools & Spas Inc describes over 40 years of experience helping homeowners with backyard pools. Its site says it builds custom swimming pools and turns outdated pools into modern ones, with sections for new pools and spas, pool renovation, and equipment upgrades and repairs. It shows 3.5 from 4 reviews.

The builders here work beyond the town limits. Aquascapes has a Port Orange address on Taylor Road and describes itself as a luxury pool designer and builder for Daytona Beach, Orlando and Central Florida. Prime Pool Builders Port Orange takes the narrower line: it says it has served Port Orange since 2015, building pools from design through start-up. Service areas that builders here state also take in Ormond Beach and St. Augustine.

Several of the five list extras that change what a project involves. East Coast Pools-Licensed and Aquascapes both list screen enclosures. For a new residential pool, Florida's pool safety law requires at least one safety feature to pass final inspection, and an enclosure that meets the barrier requirements is one of the five options, with a barrier at least 4 feet high on the outside. Financing is stated by Cypress Creek, East Coast Pools-Licensed and Aquascapes, and warranties by Aquascapes and Prime Pool Builders Port Orange.

Common questions about pool builders in Port Orange

Who builds gunite pools in Port Orange?

East Coast Pools-Licensed builds in gunite and fiberglass. Aquascapes describes gunite application among its build steps, and Prime Pool Builders Port Orange lists gunite and shotcrete on its site. All three have Port Orange addresses.

Does anyone in Port Orange install fiberglass or vinyl liner pools?

Prime Pool Builders Port Orange lists fiberglass installation and repair as well as vinyl liner installation and replacement. East Coast Pools-Licensed also builds fiberglass pools alongside gunite ones.

Which Port Orange businesses offer screen enclosures?

East Coast Pools-Licensed says it designs and builds screen enclosures with its pools, spas and water features. Aquascapes lists enclosed lanais and pool cages among its features. Florida's pool safety law counts a compliant enclosure as one way to meet the requirement for a new residential pool.

Who in Port Orange builds outdoor kitchens along with the pool?

Cypress Creek - Outdoor Living Company lists outdoor kitchens, fireplaces and firepits beside pool and spa construction. East Coast Pools-Licensed mentions summer kitchens, and Aquascapes lists outdoor kitchens among its features.

Which nearby towns have their own pool builder page?

Daytona Beach, New Smyrna Beach, Ormond Beach, DeLand and Orange City each have a page. Builders in Port Orange state service areas that include Daytona Beach and Ormond Beach, so those pages overlap with this one.
