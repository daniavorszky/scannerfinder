Florida law gives pool cages a practical role. A new residential pool must have at least one approved safety feature to pass final inspection, and an enclosure that isolates the pool is one of the five options in the state's pool safety act, with a barrier at least 4 feet high on the outside. What safety features does Florida require on a new pool?

A new residential pool needs at least one of five safety features to pass final inspection, such as a barrier at least 4 feet high, an approved safety cover, door alarms, self-closing latches or a pool alarm.
