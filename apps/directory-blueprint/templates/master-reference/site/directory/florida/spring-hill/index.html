What does Florida require before a new Spring Hill pool passes inspection?

A new residential pool needs at least one of these: an enclosure that meets the barrier rules, an approved safety cover, door and window exit alarms, self-closing and self-latching doors, or a pool alarm. Ask a builder which one its contract includes.
