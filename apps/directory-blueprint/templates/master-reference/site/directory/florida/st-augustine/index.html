Florida's residential pool safety law is worth a sentence in a town with this much enclosure work. Florida Coastal Contractors, LLC, Oasis Pools & Outdoors, Stanford Pool & Spa and Tier 1 Pools and Spas list screen enclosures with their pool work, and the law requires a new residential pool to have at least one of five safety features to pass final inspection. An enclosure that meets the barrier requirements is one of them. The others are an approved safety cover, exit alarms, self-latching doors and a pool alarm.
