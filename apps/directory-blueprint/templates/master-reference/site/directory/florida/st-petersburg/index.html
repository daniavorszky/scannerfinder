In St. Petersburg, pool builders share the work with a strong remodeling trade. The 27 businesses on this page are all in Pinellas County, and one of them, Commercial Pool Services, is based in St Pete Beach. Remodeling and resurfacing are as common here as building new pools, and some builders started somewhere other than pool construction. Oasis Pavers and Pools says the company began with paver patios and driveways, then added paver pool decks and remodeling, and now offers new in-ground pools from a base on Anvil Street North.

Concrete and shotcrete are the shells the builders here describe. Tilney Pools designs and builds custom swimming pools in the Tampa Bay area and says it has more than 25 years of experience. Its construction process runs from layout through fencing and excavation, a rebar structure and a hand-formed shotcrete shell, then plumbing, equipment, coping and decking. Separate pages cover residential pools, commercial pools for hotels, communities and businesses, and renovation. Oasis Pavers and Pools also lists gunite or concrete construction, spas and financing.

Several St. Petersburg builders present themselves as locally rooted. Sunshine City Pools says it is locally owned and based in St. Petersburg and groups its work into custom builds and designs, renovations, and pool cleaning and repair. It lists paver decks and artificial turf among its outdoor features. Sekas Custom Pools has a short website built around its portfolio, with galleries sorted into modern, contemporary, freeform, traditional and classical styles, plus an Under Construction gallery, and an address on 9th Street North. Living Pools Inc offers luxury pool design, new pool construction and remodeling in St. Pete.

Neikens Pool and Spa serves Pinellas County and lists its process as consultation and design, permitting, new pool construction and renovation. Its FAQ says the company specializes in installing in-ground pools, offers financing, and provides a warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship, with specifics varying by pool type. Goff's Pools, Inc. says it has built and remodeled swimming pools and spas since 1971, working in Pinellas, Hillsborough and Pasco counties, including St. Petersburg and Clearwater.
