Florida law separates the kinds of pool work, and that matters when a business describes itself in Stuart. State law defines a commercial pool and spa contractor, a residential pool and spa contractor, and a swimming pool and spa servicing contractor. The servicing scope covers repair and servicing, including equipment repair and interior refinishing, but not construction of a new pool. A business that describes itself as a pool service company is describing a different scope from one that builds.
