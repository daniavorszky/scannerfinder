Pool builders in Tallahassee describe their territory as North Florida, and the trade here is a mix of custom construction, spa installation and renovation. This page carries 18 pool businesses. Seventeen are in Leon County and one, in Havana, is in Gadsden County. Some builders reach well beyond the county: Southern Poolscapes lists a Pensacola address with Tallahassee in its license block.

Custom building is led by a handful of established names. ServCo Pools Inc. builds custom residential pools and outdoor entertainment areas in North Florida, says it has 24 years of experience and offers a 3D pool design. Its rating is 4.9 from 108 reviews. Salvo Pools is a family-owned Tallahassee builder that says it has operated since 1991 and is now led by third-generation contractor Ryan Salvo. It describes itself as a custom concrete pool specialist and lists water features, fire features, outdoor kitchens and patio covers. It holds 4.5 from 57 reviews.

Southern Poolscapes builds custom gunite and fiberglass pools and outdoor living spaces, and says it has built inground pools across the Gulf Coast since 2015. It lists phone numbers for Florida, Alabama, Mississippi and Tennessee or Georgia, and also offers renovations and replastering. It holds 5.0 from 88 reviews. Agua Pools Tallahassee builds custom inground pools, spas and outdoor living spaces with a display center in town and a 3D design process, though it has only 2 reviews so far. Peddie Pools of Tallahassee Inc. designs and installs custom swimming pools and holds 5.0 from 7 reviews, all at five stars.

Common questions about pool builders in Tallahassee

Which Tallahassee builders make custom concrete or gunite pools?

Salvo Pools calls itself a custom concrete pool specialist and says it has operated since 1991. Southern Poolscapes builds custom gunite and fiberglass pools and lists a Tallahassee license block. ServCo Pools Inc. builds custom residential pools, and Swain Pools & Spas builds new pools and spas as well as liner replacements.

Who replaces vinyl liners in Tallahassee?

Swain Pools & Spas describes Latham in-ground pool liners and liner replacement, and says it has been in the business for over 46 years. Premier Pools of Tallahassee also lists liner replacement along with weekly service and equipment repairs. Both list pool and spa renovation as well.

Can a Tallahassee builder add a screen enclosure?

ServCo Pools Inc. lists screen enclosures next to design, construction, decking and equipment. Agua Pools Tallahassee also lists screen enclosures among its menu services, with outdoor kitchens and spas. Both name North Florida as the area they serve.

Does anyone in Tallahassee handle commercial pools?

Premier Pools of Tallahassee describes commercial maintenance and lists commercial pool maintenance as its own service. Swain Pools & Spas and Southern Poolscapes both list commercial work on their sites as well.

Which Tallahassee builders offer 3D design before construction?

ServCo Pools Inc., Salvo Pools and Agua Pools Tallahassee all describe 3D pool design as part of planning a new build. Agua Pools describes a single in-house team handling the design, and Salvo lists 3D design next to water features and outdoor kitchens.
