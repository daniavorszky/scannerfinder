In Tampa, the pool builders range from specialists to design-build firms that treat a pool as one part of a larger outdoor or home project. This page covers 37 businesses, all in Hillsborough County, and Patio Pools, Inc. is based in Carrollwood. The mix runs from long-established pool companies to newer firms with showrooms. Landscape Fusion, a South Tampa design-build firm, pairs pool construction with landscaping, outdoor kitchens and hardscaping. Ultra Custom Group is a home remodeling and outdoor living company with a pool division. Watermark Design Build lists luxury pools next to home additions and whole-home renovations.

Concrete is the shell most of these builders describe. Cody Pools calls itself a gunite builder, says it has built more than 32,000 pools since 1994, and also names Ocala and The Villages as areas it works. Larsen's Pool & Spa builds residential shotcrete pools and says it has done so for 18 years across the Greater Tampa Bay Area. Watermark Design Build says pool renders become engineered plans with structural steel and shotcrete. JJB Pools & Spas makes inground concrete pools and concrete spas. SiteWorx Pools, Spas and Outdoor Living states that it builds only concrete pools, not fiberglass or vinyl liner.

Fiberglass is present but smaller. J & C Fiberglass Pools calls itself the Tampa Bay fiberglass pool experts, and Sharper Image Pools separates custom, cement and fiberglass pools on its menu. Patio Pools, Inc. says it has built custom residential inground pools around Tampa Bay since 1979, with three generations of the family involved, and offers AutoCad renderings.

Screen enclosures and pool decks come up often in Tampa. Andux Aquatics Pools & Spas helps families design a custom pool and screen enclosure, and builds new enclosures and remodels existing ones. Dynasty Pools and Spas lists screen enclosures, pergolas and outdoor kitchens beside new construction. Landscape Fusion lists pool enclosures and composite decks.
