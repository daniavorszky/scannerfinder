Common questions about pool builders in Vero Beach

Who in Vero Beach installs fiberglass pools?

Ministering Hands puts most of its site copy on fiberglass pool installation. Right Way Enclosures, Pools and Spa says it builds inground pools in fiberglass, vinyl liner and concrete, and installs above-ground pools too.

Which Vero Beach companies work with pavers and travertine?

Castaway Pool and Spa Service, LLC lists new pool decks in brick pavers or travertine. Coastal Deck & Pools installs pavers on driveways, decks and patios in brick, marble or travertine. Hi-Tech Pools & Pavers lists pavers and travertine along with pool and patio renovation.

Do Vero Beach builders serve Sebastian and Fellsmere?

Odom Custom Pools names Sebastian in a service area that runs to Fort Pierce and Port St. Lucie. Indian River Pools, LLC names Fellsmere, Sebastian, Melbourne and Palm Bay. Right Way Enclosures, Pools and Spa names Sebastian and Melbourne.

Who builds pools in Indian River, Brevard and St. Lucie counties?

Oasis Pools & Spas of the Treasure Coast says it serves Indian River, Brevard and St. Lucie areas. Crystal Pools IRC builds for residential and commercial customers in the same three counties and says it has done so for over twenty years.

Which Vero Beach businesses state a contractor license on their sites?

Bluewave Outdoors, LLC shows a Florida license at the top of its site, and Ministering Hands states that it holds a certified pool contractor license. Coastal Deck & Pools says it is licensed and insured but gives no number.

Does anyone in Vero Beach handle commercial pools?

Oasis Pools & Spas of the Treasure Coast and Crystal Pools IRC both describe residential and commercial pool work. Treasure Coast Pools lists private and commercial work, and Hi-Tech Pools & Pavers also states that it takes commercial work.
