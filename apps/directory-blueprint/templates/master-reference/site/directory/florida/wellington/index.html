Pool builders in Wellington lean toward the pool that already exists. Remodeling and resurfacing is the work Palm Pool & Spa, Inc., Sakko Pool LLC and King Custom Designs each describe, alongside new construction at the first two. All five businesses here have Palm Beach County addresses. This page describes what each one says it does and how its Google Maps record reads.

Palm Pool & Spa, Inc. describes itself as a family-owned pool company in Wellington that has worked in the area since 2000. Its site says it serves Palm Beach and Martin counties among other areas. It lists new pool construction, spas, remodeling and resurfacing, and pavers and decking, and it states financing and a contractor license. Its record is 4.8 from 113 reviews.

Sakko Pool LLC is a Wellington company with a focus on ICF block pool construction. It states a pool contractor license and describes itself as the official U.S. representative of Cefil, a maker of reinforced PVC pool liners, which fits its vinyl liner work. It also lists inground construction, remodeling, financing, a warranty and commercial work. Its record is 5.0 from 20 reviews.

King Custom Designs is a Wellington business whose site centers on remodeling pools, patios and driveways. Its service list names pool resurfacing, pool tile, pool plaster, coping, Diamond Brite and pool equipment, along with pavers, travertine, sandblasted marble, stamped concrete and pool decks. Screen enclosures, outdoor kitchens and artificial turf also appear on that list. Its record is 4.8 from 17 reviews.
