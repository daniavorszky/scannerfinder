Common questions about pool builders in Wesley Chapel

Who builds custom concrete pools in Wesley Chapel?

Pool Pros Construction, based in Dade City, builds custom in-ground concrete pools with shotcrete shells and works across Pasco, Pinellas, Hillsborough and Manatee counties. It holds 5.0 from 151 reviews. Zen Pools and Outdoor Living also lists residential and commercial pools with a 5.0 from 6 reviews.

Can I get a fiberglass pool from a Wesley Chapel builder?

Latham Pool, listed in Zephyrhills, is a manufacturer of fiberglass and vinyl liner pools that sells through independent builders, and its site has a page for finding one. It is not a local builder itself, so ask which installer would handle your yard.

Who adds a screen enclosure or an outdoor kitchen with a new pool?

Zen Pools and Outdoor Living lists screen enclosures, pergolas, firepits and outdoor kitchens. Pool Pros Construction lists screen enclosures and decking with pavers, marble or turf.

Which builders here mention financing?

Pool Pros Construction and Zen Pools and Outdoor Living both list financing on their sites, and Latham Pool does as well for its manufactured pools. Ask each what lender and terms apply.

Where else in Pasco County should I look?

Dade City and Zephyrhills builders are on this page. Land O' Lakes and Odessa have their own pages, as do Lutz, Tampa and Brandon.
