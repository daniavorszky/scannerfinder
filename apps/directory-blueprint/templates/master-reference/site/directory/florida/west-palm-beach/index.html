Pool builders in West Palm Beach include a good many that began with weekly service and repair. The 39 businesses on this page are all in Palm Beach County, and one of them, Groove Pools, is based in the town of Palm Beach. Several grew from service into construction. Finn's Pool Services says it starts with cleaning and maintenance and extends to new pool construction. Pool Kings of Palm Beach covers weekly maintenance, repair, luxury construction and renovation. SNF Pools leads with pool service and maintenance and also lists new construction with design, engineering and permits handled by its team.

That mix is where the license class matters. Florida law defines three pool contractor scopes: commercial pool and spa, residential pool and spa, and a servicing contractor whose scope covers repair and servicing but not construction of a new pool. A homeowner comparing a service company that also builds with a builder that only builds can ask which class each one holds.

Gunite is the shell most of the builders here describe. Fountain Blue Pool Services Inc says it has built gunite pools for South Florida and Treasure Coast families since 1996 and completed more than 10,000 projects. Wavelength Swimming Pool Contractors focuses on custom inground pools and poolscapes and says it builds custom gunite pools. Epic Watershapes says its crews design, permit and build gunite pools. Top Notch Pools describes a shotcrete or gunite shell followed by tile, coping and decking. Two businesses are shell specialists rather than full builders: Southern Gunite Inc builds custom pool shells and fountains for homeowners and builders, and Prestige Gunite applies gunite and shotcrete to pools and to structures such as walls and culverts.

Fiberglass is a smaller part of the market. Pool Doctor of the Palm Beaches describes itself as a San Juan fiberglass pool installer for Palm Beach and the Treasure Coast. Hang 10 Pools names custom gunite construction and Latham fiberglass installation, and Cool Pool of Florida builds inground and fiberglass pools.

Common questions about pool builders in West Palm Beach

Which West Palm Beach pool companies also do maintenance?

Finn's Pool Services starts with cleaning and maintenance and adds repair, leak detection, resurfacing and new construction. Pool Kings of Palm Beach covers weekly maintenance alongside luxury pool construction. SNF Pools leads with pool service and also lists new construction.

Who builds only gunite shells in Palm Beach County?

Southern Gunite Inc builds custom pool shells and fountains and works with homeowners and builders. Prestige Gunite applies gunite and shotcrete to pools and to other structures. Both are shell contractors rather than full pool builders.

Who installs fiberglass pools around West Palm Beach?

Pool Doctor of the Palm Beaches describes itself as a San Juan fiberglass pool installer for Palm Beach and the Treasure Coast. Hang 10 Pools names Latham fiberglass installation alongside custom gunite. Cool Pool of Florida builds inground and fiberglass pools.

Which builders work beyond Palm Beach County?

Hang 10 Pools serves South Florida from Vero Beach to Fort Lauderdale. Craft Master Custom Pools says it works in Palm Beach, Martin, St. Lucie and Broward counties. FDS Pools says it builds and services pools from Palm Beach County to Miami.
