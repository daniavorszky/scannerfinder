Winter Park pool builders on this page work across a county line. Two of the four businesses are in Seminole County and two are in Orange County, so a homeowner here is comparing companies that are used to driving between the two.

The trade leans toward custom work and renovation. Premier Pools of Central Florida calls itself a family owned and operated Orlando pool builder and says every pool it builds is fully custom. Its site lists modern geometric, infinity, lap, plunge and freeform pools, along with hot tubs, fire features, grottos, waterfalls and pool bars. Central Florida Homes and Pools Inc says it builds new swimming pools and summer kitchens, renovates existing pools, and also does landscape design, decking, driveways, walkways and new screen enclosures.

Parent's Pools works the other side of the trade. Its site says it specializes in total renovations and resurfacing and serves all of Central Florida, and it offers free consultations. That suits a homeowner whose pool is already in the ground and needs work rather than a new build.

Where these businesses say they work reaches well past the town. Premier Pools of Central Florida names Orlando, New Smyrna Beach, The Villages, Windermere and Winter Park as places it builds. Parent's Pools describes its territory as all of Central Florida. A builder based near Winter Park may be just as ready to take a job in Winter Garden or Orlando as one on your own street.

Common questions about pool builders in Winter Park

Who builds new pools in Winter Park?

Premier Pools of Central Florida says it makes every pool fully custom and lists infinity, lap, plunge and freeform designs. Central Florida Homes and Pools Inc says it builds new swimming pools and summer kitchens. Both are in Seminole County.

Who does resurfacing or renovation of an older Winter Park pool?

Parent's Pools says it specializes in total renovations and resurfacing and serves all of Central Florida. Premier Pools of Central Florida also lists renovations of existing pools, and Central Florida Homes and Pools Inc says it renovates existing pools and kitchens.

Can I add a screen enclosure or new decking with my pool?

Central Florida Homes and Pools Inc says it handles new screen enclosures, decking, driveways and walkways alongside pool construction and adding a spa. Its site also mentions landscape design, which can matter if the whole backyard is changing.

Does any Winter Park builder offer financing?

Premier Pools of Central Florida and Central Florida Homes and Pools Inc both mention financing on their sites. Ask either one for the terms in writing before you commit to a design.

Which places do Winter Park builders say they serve?

Premier Pools of Central Florida names Orlando, New Smyrna Beach, The Villages, Windermere and Winter Park. Parent's Pools says it serves all of Central Florida. Nearby towns with their own pages include Maitland, Casselberry, Altamonte Springs and Longwood.
