# Page Sections (order)

The order is the [[master-template]] and does not vary between directories. The look does ([[design-seed]]).

**Header:** brand wordmark (the home link) · [Niche] (to `/directory/`) · About · Contact · "Get listed" button (to `/claim/`).

**Home:** hero (H1 with the state in the action color, one line, the search pill, three promises, the guide link, the quiet stat row with icons) · "Find [niche] in your city" cards with the "See all N cities" button · featured band · highest-rated grid · claim callout · "Find [niche] by city" A to Z · vetting band with four steps · buyer's guide article · FAQ cards.

**Browse (`/directory/`):** hero with search · cities A to Z · every listing by city.

**State hub:** breadcrumbs · H1 · intro · featured band · cities with counts · smaller towns (under-floor pages kept by `foldMaxKm`, indexed like any city page, out of the nav) · FAQ cards · claim callout.

**City page:** breadcrumbs · H1 "[Niche] in [City], [ST]" · key facts pills · first half of the copy · featured band · "Best [niche] in [City]" cards · overflow list · second half of the copy · FAQ cards · "[Niche] near [City]" cards · claim callout.

**Listing page:** breadcrumbs · mark · H1 · rating line · "[Niche] listing in [City], [ST]" · (source order) About · Services and details (with links to its service pages) · Contact and Hours · "Other [niche] in [City]" · disclaimer line; sidebar: map (optimized and preview) · Location with Get directions · claim card · disclaimer card.

**Featured page:** band head · tier cards · three steps · contact callout.

**Service page (optional):** breadcrumbs · H1 "[Service title] in [State]" · key facts pills · intro · top listings as cards · the service by city · other services · claim callout.

**Footer:** About with the publisher line · Quick links (with "[Niche] by service" when service pages exist) · Largest cities, ending in "All N cities" · Contact with social icons · legal bar.

**Cards:** listing card (H3 name, rating line, address, three chips, phone with icon at the foot) · city card (name, count, verified count) · featured card (gold band, navy tab, city, rating, name, excerpt, chips, link) · open-position card (same anatomy, dashed).
