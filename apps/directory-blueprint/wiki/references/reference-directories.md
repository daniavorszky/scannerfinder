# Reference directories: seven live sites for design cues and structure

Shawn picked these seven on 2026-09-29 as "good design cues and structurally in the ballpark" of what this Blueprint builds. **They are no longer the fallback for Gate 1 question 7** (Shawn, 2026-10-01): a member with no inspired site gets the master template's look, the reference site in `templates/master-reference/site/`, and is not shown this list. Show it only when a member asks to see other looks or examples; a pick from here is then a design pointer like any other, and Gate 4 derives the tokens from it ([[design-seed]]). They also remain a comparison set at Gate 4 and Gate 5 (what a good state page, city page and listing card carry), after the reference site, which comes first.

Rules that still hold:
- Borrow the styling and the page structure, never the content, images, logo or copy.
- Never present a site here as a niche to copy. The niche of the example is irrelevant; a member without a vertical gets the vertical check in [[gate-1-intake]].
- Offer the list only on request; the member picks. Do not pick for them and do not default to one.
- None of these is the Blueprint's shape one-for-one. What each teaches is in its row.

Live check 2026-09-29 (curl from Shawn's Mac): five answered 200; Checkatrade and Pickleheads answer 403 to scripts (bot protection) and were checked through search snippets and page titles only.

## The seven

### 1. Potty Directory, https://pottydirectory.com/
- **What it is:** porta potty rental providers, US, 50 states, 2,300+ cities, 3,400+ providers. Built by Ascend Systems.
- **Structure:** home with search, Browse States, state page (provider and city counts, six featured verified providers, city grid with provider counts, three guide cards, "Own a business in Texas? Add Your Business" band), city page, listing page with quote request. Plus a cost calculator, pricing page, blog and FAQ for topical depth.
- **Design cues:** clean card grid, one accent color, blue links, hero image with breadcrumbs, stats line at the top of the state page, provider cards that show rating, review count, open status, phone, View Details and Website.
- **Monetization shown:** free listing, featured listing upsell, "Featured Verified" badge, claim and verify, quote leads from every profile.
- **Closest to the Blueprint:** yes. This is the state to city to listing shape with featured slots on the state page. Difference: national scope; the Blueprint stays one state.

### 2. Liquidation Map, https://www.liquidationmap.com/
- **What it is:** liquidation and wholesale suppliers, bin stores, pallets, flea markets, US and Canada. Run by Chris Traylor. WordPress.
- **Structure:** map plus state and category filters (state counts on the home page), category directories (wholesale, flea markets), state pages, featured newest listings block, Get Listed, educational articles.
- **Design cues:** state and category thumbnails as the navigation, a "featured newest listings" strip, a vetting story on the home page ("be smart, shop wisely").
- **Monetization shown:** featured listings, Get Listed registration, vetted-seller badge (announced), a marketplace for truckloads.
- **What it teaches:** a directory that sells a vetting story as the reason to list; a featured strip that changes as listings are sold.

### 3. Discover Plasma, https://www.discoverplasma.com/
- **What it is:** plasma donation centers, US, 1,256 centers, 750+ cities, 46 states. Frey Chu's own directory (GeoDirectory founder story, https://wpgeodirectory.com/founder-story-frey-chou/). He does not publish the URLs of his earning directories; this is the one with his name on it.
- **Structure:** search by city, state, zip or company; state browse pages; city pages; center pages; a leaderboard; company filter pages.
- **Design cues:** clean and modern, center photography, pay shown as "1st visit vs returning" with a "Verified by donors" badge, distance indicators, a $100 monthly giveaway for contributions.
- **Monetization shown:** none visible. The asset is the data nobody else has (donor-reported pay per visit).
- **What it teaches:** one datum the incumbents do not show (here, real pay) is a reason to rank and a reason to return. In the Blueprint, that is the enriched attribute set ([[enrichment]]).

### 4. Expertise.com, https://www.expertise.com/
- **What it is:** "best of" lists per US city and category across 200+ industries; the company says it researches 60,000+ businesses a month.
- **Structure:** category page, city page with the chosen businesses, one profile per business, a five-stage selection story (identification, qualification, reputation, experience, mystery shopping).
- **Design cues:** editorial, magazine-like list pages; a "why we chose them" panel per business; a selection-process page that reads like a methodology.
- **Monetization shown:** advertising on the list pages ("providers may choose to advertise on the page"). Winners get an email and a complimentary badge for their site (Local Search Forum, https://localsearchforum.com/threads/expertise-and-other-award-badges.57759/).
- **What it teaches:** the badge-for-a-backlink pitch at scale and the best-of tier in [[monetization-slots]]. Frame in the Blueprint: the best-of page lives on the directory, the badge is an HTML embed that links back ([[authority-and-links]]).

### 5. ThreeBestRated, https://threebestrated.com/
- **What it is:** top three per city and category, US, Canada, UK, Australia and more; 200+ cities.
- **Structure:** city and category pages with exactly three businesses each, a "50-Point Inspection" page (reputation, history, complaints, ratings, proximity, satisfaction, trust, cost, reviews, excellence), submit-business page, a badge for winners.
- **Design cues:** scarcity as the layout (three, not fifty), the inspection score as the trust device, a free-listing story on every page.
- **Monetization shown:** ads; listings are free by policy (forum threads report renewal "donation" asks; not verified).
- **What it teaches:** a short list sells itself; a named method makes the list defensible. Do not copy the "no pay to play" promise if the directory sells slots.

### 6. Checkatrade (UK), https://www.checkatrade.com/
- **What it is:** vetted tradespeople by trade and town, UK. The incumbent UK members compete with.
- **Structure:** trade plus town search, trade profile (name, phone, locality, score out of 10, review count, skills and sub-skills, vetting badges, up to six recent reviews embedded), a "12 checks" page, membership tiers.
- **Design cues:** the tick of approval, the score out of 10 with review count, vetting flags on every profile.
- **Monetization shown:** trade membership. Public pricing on 2026-09-07: free profile, Approved at £30 a month, Growth from £59 a month; twelve-month terms; most active members pay far more with lead fees (NobleLeads and LocalAdder round-ups).
- **What it teaches:** a paid tier per listing on a twelve-month term, the Yell sponsored-slot model's successor. Not fetched from this machine (403); read through search snippets.

### 7. Pickleheads, https://www.pickleheads.com/
- **What it is:** pickleball courts, about 25,000 courts in 9,800 US cities, then games, groups and gear. Started as a searchable court directory with player-submitted courts; now the official USA Pickleball court finder.
- **Structure:** `/courts/us/<state>/<city>` city pages ("78 Most Popular Pickleball Courts in Chicago, IL") and one page per court with type (indoor or outdoor, dedicated lines and nets), surface, lights, fees, reservations, amenities, and a "suggest a change" door.
- **Design cues:** the count in the page title, filter chips (court type, surface, amenities, lighting), attribute rows on every court page, user corrections invited on every page.
- **Monetization shown:** gear affiliate, facility partnerships, the app.
- **What it teaches:** the attribute grid as the page ([[page-types]]), the count-first title pattern, and the endgame where the directory becomes the brand. Not fetched from this machine (403); URL pattern and page titles from search results.
